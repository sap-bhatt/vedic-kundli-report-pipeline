## Personality and temperament

### The rising sign

Your lagna is Leo, at 18°51' of Leo. The sign would only change if the birth time were off by about 45 minutes. Leo rising is traditionally read as proud, brave and commanding: someone who wants recognition, can be quick to anger, and is drawn to leadership and to the company of people in power. The traditional texts also describe warmth, sincerity and generosity, though some note a lack of tact.

Mars does the most for Leo, since it rules the 4th and 9th houses and so acts as the yogakaraka, the planet that carries both the best houses for this lagna. Its strength is tempered by where it sits. Mars is debilitated in Cancer in the 12th house, though that weakness is cancelled, and it is close enough to the Sun to be overpowered by its light. Its good work tends to happen quietly and behind the scenes rather than in open view. Venus and Saturn, which the classical lists count as harder for Leo, share the 10th house. Venus sits in its own sign, which softens that.

### The lagna nakshatra

Your lagna falls in Purva Phalguni, ruled by Venus. The classical notes on this star, Bhaga's star of pleasure and union, describe a sweet-spoken, generous and radiant person, with a theme of love, marriage and family life. These are tendencies, not fixed traits; sign, house, aspects and dasha all modify them. Venus, the lord of this star, sits in its own sign in the 10th house, close to Saturn, so the warmth of the star has a serious, working edge.

### The lagna lord and its placement

The Sun is the lagna lord. It is a friend by sign and sits in the 12th house, in Cancer. This is the most important placement for the self in the chart. Classical texts read a lagna lord in the 12th as pulling energy toward expense, distance and withdrawal, and they warn of reduced comfort, heavy spending and anger unless benefics help. Modern readers add foreign residence and an inward, spiritual pull.

Some things soften this. In the navamsa chart the Sun sits in moolatrikona, a strong dignity, so its inner strength is real. Through the Sun the chart also forms the Raja combination (kendra and trikona lords) and the Dhana combination (wealth lords). What does not soften it is the company it keeps. No benefic aspects the Sun. It is aspected only by Saturn and Rahu, both malefic in this chart, and it shares Cancer with Mars, Mercury and Ketu.

The placement dominates your daily temperament. You are likely to work out of sight, abroad or in service, with your drive showing through effort rather than display. The navamsa dignity and the combinations mean your inner sense of worth is solid and that outward standing, though slower, is real. Spending is worth handling mindfully. Money decisions stay with you.

### Planets in the lagna or aspecting it

No planet occupies the lagna. Jupiter aspects it from Sagittarius. Classical texts count this as one of the strongest supports a lagna can have, bringing religious and virtuous habits, respect from authorities, good company and protection. Jupiter is a natural benefic here and sits in its own moolatrikona sign, so that protection is real. It also rules the 8th house, which adds a note of hidden matters, but its aspect on the self is the part that shapes temperament: optimism, principle and a dignified manner. Read it as protection and growth, not as a guarantee.

### The Sun

The Sun sits in Cancer, a friendly sign, in the 12th house with Mars, Mercury and Ketu. Ketu is within four degrees of it. The traditional texts split on this placement. The more favourable view gives an honourable, eloquent person of good qualities, though undertakings can be unsteady. Another view describes a hot temper, hard work for others and some friction with relatives. Both agree on effort and strain. Because the Sun receives only the aspects of Saturn and Rahu, its honour is earned through effort rather than handed over.

Classical texts are cautious about standing and wealth for a Sun in the 12th. They also credit it with energy and with a shine that appears behind the scenes, abroad, or in service and spiritual settings. That is the side to work with.

### The Moon sign

The Moon is in Scorpio, in the 4th house, and counts as a benefic in this chart because it is waxing. Its debility is cancelled, since the lord of its exalted sign, Venus, sits in a kendra. The Moon's detail is in the Mind section. Here it adds a softer, feeling layer beneath the firmer Leo rising.

### Birth day and tithi

You were born on a Saturday, the day of Saturn. Traditional sources read a Saturday birth as a serious, enduring temperament, not a sign of misfortune. Your tithi is Shukla Ekadashi, the eleventh day of the waxing Moon. One traditional text describes such a person as devoted to the gods and the learned, someone who keeps vows, gives in charity, and is pure-hearted and capable. These are minor colourings of temperament, not fate.

### Temperament, drawn together

The front the world sees is a proud, brave Leo rising, with Jupiter's protection on the self and a dignified, principled manner. Underneath is a lagna lord that works out of sight in the 12th house, among malefic company and without benefic aspect, but with real inner dignity from the navamsa. The Moon's softer, feeling side sits in Scorpio. The result is a person who can seem confident and proud at one moment and then withdraw, reflect and serve quietly the next. The pride is genuine, but the chart asks it to be expressed through service, work done well and inner conviction rather than public display.

The Atmakaraka, the planet with the highest degree in its sign and the planet of the self, is Mercury. It sits in a sign unfriendly to it. Traditional texts read this as a call to grow through truthful speech and steady judgment, and not as bad luck. Rahu and Ketu are both vargottama, sitting in the same sign in the birth chart and the navamsa.

### When this area is most active

From December 2030 to June 2031, the Moon period moves into its Sun sub-period. This stretch is supportive because the Sun is the lagna lord, and it tends to favour self-confidence and a clearer sense of direction. The same supportive tone returns in the Mars-Sun sub-period from June to November 2037. Between August and October 2032, Jupiter and Saturn both aspect the 1st house, a classical marker that the self and its presentation come into focus. It is a good time to ask whether your outward style matches what you want to express.
