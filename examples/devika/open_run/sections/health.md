## Health and vitality

This is traditional astrology, not medical advice. For any health concern, please see a doctor. What follows describes how the classical texts read your constitution and the times to take extra care.

### Overall constitution

Your rising sign (lagna) is Leo, ruled by the Sun. The Sun sits in the 12th house, in Cancer, a sign friendly to it. The ruler of the rising sign in one of the difficult houses (6, 8 and 12) gets its results with more effort, so your vitality tends to come in steady stretches that need rest to renew them. The Sun is naturally a malefic in this chart, yet as your rising lord it governs the body, which is why steady care matters more than intensity. Jupiter aspects your rising sign, which the classical texts read as a sound, well-built constitution. Your rising sign sits 18.9 degrees into Leo, so this reading holds unless the birth time is off by roughly 45 minutes.

The Moon, which governs the mind and emotional steadiness, is in Scorpio in the 4th house. It is debilitated there, but the weakness is softened: Venus, the ruler of Taurus (the Moon's sign of exaltation), sits in a corner house, the 10th, and a classical rule counts that as cancelling the debilitation. The Moon in the 4th house is one of its best placements for comfort at home.

### Traditional sensitivities

- **6th house, daily routine:** Capricorn, ruled by Saturn, which sits in the 10th house, the career house. Rahu sits here too. The texts see Rahu in the 6th as strong for overcoming rivals, while noting puzzling or recurring complaints as the trade-off. The practical lesson is to notice small, repeated symptoms early. Saturn's position suggests that workload is where strain would show first, and a steady routine is the traditional answer.

- **8th house, recovery:** Pisces, ruled by Jupiter, which is strong in its own sign in the 5th house. The texts link a heavily afflicted lord of the 8th with bodily strain and nervous tension. Here Jupiter is strong, so this is a caution rather than a forecast, and good rest supports steady recovery.

- **12th house, rest and sleep:** Cancer, ruled by the Moon, with four planets gathered in it: the Sun, Mars, Mercury and Ketu. A crowded 12th is traditionally linked with restless nights. The texts say a strained Mercury keeps the mind busy after dark, and Mars in this house is linked with restless sleep. Mars sits close to the Sun, which the texts take as dimming it, though its debilitation is softened by classical rules.

### Periods to take more care

Three stretches stand out. The Timeline section has the full dates.

- **July to December 2028:** Jupiter and Saturn both bear on the 6th house. The texts mark this as a time for daily routine.
- **March to August 2029:** the same double pass again, in the same house.
- **November 2031 to November 2032:** a Mars-Rahu sub-period, with Rahu in the 6th. Active for this area, but not supportive by itself.

In each, keep up rest and routine.
