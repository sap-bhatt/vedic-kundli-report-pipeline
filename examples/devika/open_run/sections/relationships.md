## Relationships, marriage and partner

### The 7th house and its lord

For a Leo lagna the 7th house is Aquarius, and its lord Saturn sits in the 10th house in Taurus, a sign where Saturn is at ease, together with Venus. Saturn joining Venus in the house of career and public standing is a classic link between partnership and work. The texts describe a devoted, career-minded spouse who helps the native's work, and often a profession that involves travel or work abroad. They also warn that a spouse may not always follow the native's wishes, and that when Saturn is under strain a partner can become over-ambitious and a career can lose its footing. The fuller picture is of a partner who is independent and capable, with goals of their own.

The 7th house itself is empty, so no planet in it colours the partner directly.

### Venus and the partner's qualities

Venus is the natural signifier of marriage. It sits in its own sign Taurus, in the 10th house, which makes it a steady and well-placed benefic. The Malavya yoga forms from this placement. Classical texts link it with a happy marriage and a loving spouse, and modern astrologers read it as happiness from a partner and from children. In practice this shows up as warmth, affection and closeness, and a generally sociable temperament.

Venus is debilitated in the navamsa (D9), in Virgo. A debilitated navamsa gives less of the planet's good results: its gifts start well but need more effort to hold on to. So the strong promise of the main chart is partly held back, and it is best seen as something to build, not a guarantee.

### The Darakaraka and the navamsa

The Darakaraka, the planet with the lowest degree in its sign, is the Sun. It describes the partner's qualities and the themes of the partnership. The Sun is also the lagna lord, placed in Cancer in the 12th house, a friendly sign for it, alongside Mars, Mercury and Ketu. In the navamsa it is moolatrikona in Leo, which gives it a secure footing. Some classical texts are cautious about the Sun in Cancer where marriage is concerned, so the 7th house is the better guide to the spouse itself.

In the navamsa the 7th house is Pisces, ruled by Jupiter, which sits in the 10th of the navamsa in Gemini. Pisces is a benefic sign, and the texts describe a spouse shaped by Jupiter as virtuous and restrained. Mercury, a natural malefic in this chart and debilitated in the navamsa, also sits in that 7th house. This suggests the partner's thinking and manner of communicating may need patience. The navamsa refines the main chart rather than replacing it. Where the two differ, the results are mixed, and both sides are worth reflecting on.

### Manglik status

Mars sits in the 12th house from the lagna, the 9th from the Moon and the 3rd from Venus. Classical rules treat Mars in certain houses as sensitive for marriage. Some lists count the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th houses, others the 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th, and modern practice uses all six. Mars in the 12th from the lagna meets that rule. Counted from the Moon or from Venus, it does not. So the Manglik (Kuja) dosha is present from the lagna alone.

It is cancelled on three counts. Mars sits in a movable sign (Cancer). The lagna is Leo, and the rules treat a Cancer or Leo lagna as cancelling. And Mars is debilitated. The classical cancellation that matters most is a matching Mars placement in the partner's chart, which is worth knowing if a match is being considered.

Modern astrologers, from Raman onward, judge the dosha to be over-applied. They read it as a Mars theme in partnership: energy, independence and friction that need to be channelled. It is common, and it is only one factor among many in marriage matching. The 7th house, its lord and Venus carry more weight.

### Aspects on the 7th house

Saturn, from the 10th house, aspects the 7th. Its aspect is cooler in tone: the texts associate it with delay, distance or reserve in marriage, and with a serious, duty-bound approach. Two things soften this. Saturn owns the 7th in a Leo lagna, which the texts count as supportive, and Saturn's steadiness can become patience and loyalty in a relationship. No benefic aspect reaches the 7th to balance it, so the Saturn theme carries more weight here.

Mars, from the 12th house, also aspects the 7th. Mars is a natural malefic, but for Leo it is the yogakaraka, ruling the 4th and 9th, two of the good houses. Its aspect brings energy and passion into partnership, with a need for patience and fairness. It is steadier because it rules good houses, and less steady for sharing the 12th house with Ketu.

### Friendships (11th house)

The 11th house is Gemini, ruled by Mercury in the 12th house, in its enemy sign Cancer. Braha reads a smaller circle of friends for this placement, so choosing companions carefully matters. Jupiter helps here. It sits in its own moolatrikona sign, Sagittarius, in the 5th house, and aspects the 11th, which supports a warm and genuine circle of friends. The net picture is a few close, well-chosen friendships rather than a large group.

### When this area is most active

The current stretch is the Saturn sub-period within the Moon period, running until April 2027. Saturn rules the 7th and shares the 10th house with Venus, so it brings seriousness and career-minded thinking into partnership. It is a demanding stretch rather than an easy one, and better suited to steady groundwork than to a fresh commitment.

Later, the 7th house is activated twice when Jupiter and Saturn both touch it: from late August to early October 2029, and again from May to September 2030. The texts treat these as stretches when partnership comes to the foreground. They are timing markers, not predictions.

Decisions about partnership remain yours. These are themes to reflect on, and whether they match your own experience is the real test.
