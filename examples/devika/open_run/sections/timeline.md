## Timeline

Vimshottari dashas are nine planetary periods of fixed length that follow one another through life. The first starts from the lord of the Moon's birth star, which for you is Mercury, with about 15 years of it still to run at birth. Each period brings forward what its planet rules and signifies in your chart, and the house it occupies and the planets it joins or is aspected by decide which matters come to the front. Slow transits of Jupiter and Saturn act as secondary timers inside the periods, and they count most when they fall on the same houses. These are patterns to compare with your own experience; the decisions stay yours.

### How timing works

**Mercury, 1972 to 1988 (ages 0 to 15).** This period often coincides with early learning, speech and friendships, and with a family life that was not always settled. Mercury rules the 2nd and 11th, the houses of money and gains, but sits in the 12th, a house of expense, and is weak in the navamsa (the divisional chart the texts use for the finer reading). The texts therefore split between a sharp mind and trade gains on one side, and quarrels with relatives and worry on the other. Saturn's passage around the Moon overlaps this period too: an Ashtama Dhaiya (Saturn's two-and-a-half-year pass through the 8th sign from the Moon) in 1973 to 1975, and a Sade Sati (the seven-and-a-half-year cycle around the Moon's sign) from 1982 to 1990. Both are popular traditions built on older house-by-house rules, and modern authors disagree about how severe they are. Does this match your early years?

**Ketu, 1988 to 1995 (ages 15 to 22).** This period often coincides with restlessness, a move or a change of study, and friction with people around you. Ketu sits in the 12th and takes on the nature of its sign lord, the Moon, which the texts link to an inward, spiritual interest where it is supported. The Kantaka Dhaiya (Saturn's pass through the 4th sign from the Moon) overlaps its close, from 1993 to 1996, and is often linked with domestic strain and separation from friends or relatives. Does that ring true?

**Venus, 1995 to 2015 (ages 22 to 42).** This long period often coincides with comfort, partnership or marriage, and steady work. Venus sits in its own sign in the 10th, the career house, rules the 3rd and 10th, and is your Amatyakaraka, the role the reference notes tie to counsel, career and profession. Malavya and Amala, two benefic yogas, both sit in Venus, and the texts connect them with comfort, taste and a clean public name. A Dhaiya stretch (2002 to 2005) and a Sade Sati from late 2011 to early 2017 also overlap it. Does this match your twenties and thirties?

**Sun, 2015 to 2021 (ages 42 to 48).** The Sun is your lagna lord, which ties this period to self, authority and recognition. It sits in the 12th, so its results come with more effort. It is in Cancer, a friend's sign, which the texts link to standing won through effort and to travel, but it is with malefics, which they link to friction with people in power and to losses. The setting phase of Sade Sati (Saturn in Sagittarius, 2017 to 2020) overlaps this period, with a peak in 2017. This period often coincides with standing built slowly under pressure. Does that match?

**The Moon period, 2021 to 2031 (ages 48 to 58, the period you are in now).** This is a mixed period. The Moon rules the 12th but sits in the 4th, which the texts rate among its best places: home, mother, vehicles, friends and inner comfort. It is waxing, counts as a benefic in your chart, and is debilitated in Scorpio. The weakness is partly cancelled: Venus, lord of the Moon's exalted sign, sits in a kendra (the 10th), and the Moon itself sits in one. The Moon is in the middle of its sign, so its results gather in the middle of the period. It also holds the Putrakaraka role, which the reference notes tie to children, so family events stay central. Expect home life, property and family matters to be the strong side, with money strain, worry and concern for the mother as the difficult side.

### Sub-periods of the Moon period

**Moon within Moon, Jun 2021 to Apr 2022.** This opening stretch often coincides with a gentle start: learning, devotion and family events. Its debilitation, softened as above, often coincides with some money strain and friction with authority. Does that match?

**Moon within Mars, Apr to Nov 2022.** Mars sits in the 12th, and the Moon and Mars swap signs, tying home and loss together. This short stretch often coincides with heat and friction at work or at home, disputes, and money pressure from property or business.

**Moon within Rahu, Nov 2022 to May 2024.** Rahu sits in the 6th, a house the texts rate well for overcoming rivals, so this stretch often coincides with success in contests, pilgrimage or charity. It also often coincides with unsettled health, dealings with officials, and a need for care with routine and food.

**Moon within Jupiter, May 2024 to Sep 2025.** Jupiter, a trikona lord, sits in its moolatrikona sign in the 5th, a strong seat. This stretch often coincides with recognition, family celebrations, new clothes or vehicles, and gains through children.

**Moon within Saturn, Sep 2025 to Apr 2027 (current).** Saturn sits in the 10th, a kendra, in a friend's sign alongside Venus, and rules the 6th and 7th. Its good side, as the texts describe it, is children, friendship, property, business gained through working people, and royal favour. Its difficult side is enjoyment at some times and strain with the wife and children at others, with a call for extra care of health and vitality. This is the stretch in which the current Saturn transit (below) feels strongest, because the Saturn sub-period is running. Expect steady work and property progress, with worry about children and money as the points to watch.

**Moon within Mercury, Apr 2027 to Sep 2028.** Mercury sits in the 12th and rules the 2nd and 11th. The texts link that to money that is hard to keep and gains that flow out, though it also carries a Dhana (wealth) link that supports earning. The good side is study, trade, writing, settling disputes and close dealings with authorities. Keep rest and general health steady here, since this stretch calls for a steady pace.

**Moon within Ketu, Sep 2028 to Apr 2029.** Ketu sits in the 12th with the Sun, Mars and Mercury, and is aspected by Saturn and Rahu. This short stretch often coincides with distraction, some loss, quarrels with friends, and public criticism. Its gentler side, through its sign lord the Moon, is wealth, family happiness and religious inclination, when the chart supports it. Keep finances careful and look after the spouse's health.

**Moon within Venus, Apr 2029 to Dec 2030.** Venus sits in its own sign in the 10th, rules the 3rd and 10th, and forms Malavya and Amala, so this stretch is where those yogas show. The texts describe comfort and standing: high position, clothes, ornaments, vehicles, a new house, happiness with the wife and children, and sound health. The shadow is Venus's company with Saturn in the same sign, which the texts link to strain over land, partnership and friction with government. This is one of the most comfortable stretches of the Moon period.

**Moon within Sun, Dec 2030 to Jun 2031.** The Sun is the lagna lord but sits in the 12th, so its results come with effort. It sits in Cancer, a friend's sign, and forms a Raja yoga (a link between the lord of an angle and the lord of a trine, for standing and status) and a Dhana link. The good side is recovery of position, recognition from authorities and a sense of confidence. The strain is friction with people in power and losses of honour or money, since the Sun is with malefics. Jupiter's transit into Sagittarius, the 2nd from the Moon, from Feb to Jun 2031 supports this stretch. Advise rest toward its end.

### The Mars period, Jun 2031 to Jun 2038 (ages 58 to 65)

Mars is your chart's yogakaraka, the one planet that rules both a kendra and a trikona (here the 4th, home, and the 9th, fortune). Its period is the largest turn of the decade. Mars sits in the 12th in Cancer, debilitated, combust with the Sun, and exalted in the navamsa. The weakness is partly cancelled: the Moon, lord of Mars's sign, sits in a kendra; Saturn, lord of its exalted sign, sits in a kendra; and Mars is exalted in the navamsa. The favourable side is strong, with property, initiative, courage and competition. The difficult side is real: quarrels with siblings and family, expenses, and heat-related strain. Mars sits in the last ten degrees of its sign, so its main effects are more likely toward the later part of the period, and the texts warn of some unfavourable turns at the end of even a strong Mars period.

#### Inside the Mars period, one stretch at a time

**Mars within Mars, Jun to Nov 2031 (the key stretch).** This is the first stretch of the Mars period and the one that matters most in the next decade. Mars's role as yogakaraka puts its own stretch at the top of the chart's functional ranking, and it is the first taste of that promise. Two things temper it. Mars sits in the 12th, combust and in a debilitated sign, and a planet's own stretch gives only part of its promise; the texts disagree on how much of a yogakaraka's yoga shows in its own stretch. The softeners are the cancellation of its weakness and its navamsa exaltation. The slow timers add weight: Jupiter moves over the Moon's sign from Jun to Oct 2031, and the 4th house is under double transit from Apr 2030 to Oct 2031. Expect property and initiative to come up, and treat home questions as things to test, not forecasts.

**Mars within Rahu, Nov 2031 to Nov 2032.** Rahu sits in the 6th, a seat many texts rate for defeating rivals, and it is vargottama. Its sign lord is Saturn, a lord of the 6th and 7th, and this stretch is among the hardest in every source. Rahu and Mars aspect each other, so the Mars period's main results show here. The favourable side is contests won and gains from effort. The difficult side calls for caution with legal matters, travel, money, health checks, and people of doubtful intent. From May 2032 the Dhaiya overlaps the second half of this stretch.

**Mars within Jupiter, Nov 2032 to Oct 2033.** Jupiter, the trikona lord ruling the 5th and 8th, sits in its moolatrikona sign in the 5th and is retrograde. It also forms the cancellation of Mars's weakness, so this stretch is tied directly to the softened side of the period. The texts see mentors, a good name, family happiness, property and children, a welcome easing inside a demanding period. Two cautions apply. It is the 6th from Mars, which one school reads as unfavourable, and the Dhaiya (Saturn in Gemini, May 2032 to Jul 2034) covers all of it, which pulls the reading down. Keep health and dealings with officials in view.

**Mars within Saturn, Oct 2033 to Dec 2034.** Saturn rules the 6th and 7th, sits in the 10th (a kendra, for recognition and property) in a friend's sign, and is in the 11th from Mars, a house of gains. The good side, as the texts describe it, is recognition from government, a growing reputation, wealth and happiness from children. The difficult side is foreign journeys, loss of position, mental strain and defeat in contests. The Dhaiya runs until Jul 2034 and the Sankata yogini until Mar 2034, so this stretch carries both. The texts link it to a need for extra care of health and vitality, so keep a steady routine and be cautious with authority and property matters.

**Mars within Mercury, Dec 2034 to Dec 2035.** Mercury rules the 2nd and 11th, sits in the 12th in an enemy's sign, and is debilitated in the navamsa. It is joined with Mars, so the period's quarrels show here too. The texts split: trade, learning, negotiation and settling disputes on one side; enemies, rivals, worries about theft and mental strain on the other. This is the most demanding of the Mars stretches for the number of difficult readings it carries. Keep speech careful, take time over contracts and travel plans, and keep general health steady.

**Mars within Ketu, Dec 2035 to Apr 2036.** Ketu sits in the 12th, takes on the nature of its sign lord the Moon, and shares Mars's sign. The older texts describe this stretch sharply: sudden hazards, quarrels with relatives, family disputes and a pull to leave home. Later authors see withdrawal and spiritual insight in Ketu's 12th-house seat. Keep care with hazards, travel and family disputes.

**Mars within Venus, Apr 2036 to Jun 2037.** Venus sits in its own sign in the 10th, forms Malavya and Amala, and is 11th from Mars, a house of gains. The texts describe a pleasant interval of comforts, relationships, family events, property and charity. Its difficult side comes from Venus's company with Saturn, which the texts link to family disagreements, losses and friction with officials. Because the yogas show in their own periods, this is the gentlest stretch of the Mars period and a bright close to the decade. Keep care with reputation and close relationships.

### Slow transits, Sade Sati and yoginis

Saturn is in Pisces now, the 5th from your Moon, which the texts link to worry over children, a dip in money, a clouded mind and quarrels. The current Moon-Saturn stretch makes this transit feel stronger. Saturn gives most of its transit effect in the last ten degrees of a sign, so the heavier part of each stretch falls near its end. From Jun 2027 it sits in Aries, the 6th from the Moon, where it is debilitated and the usual relief from enemies and worries fails for those months. It returns briefly to Pisces in Oct 2027 and settles in Aries from Feb 2028 to Aug 2029. From Aug to Oct 2029, and again from Apr 2030 to May 2032, it sits in Taurus, the 7th from the Moon, a strain on partnership and travel, and over the natal Venus and Saturn in the 10th, so career matters are under review. From May 2032 it enters Gemini, the 8th from the Moon. This is the Ashtama Dhaiya, which the texts count among the hardest transits, with losses, strain on the body and separation listed. Treat it as the clearest testing stretch of the decade: rest, caution with money and debt, and regular health checks. These are the hard results the texts associate with it, not a forecast.

Jupiter is in Leo, the 10th from the Moon, until Jan 2027 and again from Jun to Nov 2027. The texts read this as a test of position and property, but a reverse-vedha check (a second planet's position that blunts the difficulty) holds here, so the hard side is softened. Its best stretch is Cancer, the 9th from the Moon and an exalted sign, from Jan to Jun 2027. Virgo, the 11th from the Moon, runs from Nov 2027 to Mar 2028 and is a house of gains and honour. Libra from Dec 2028 to Mar 2029 is the 12th, with grief and worry about travel. Scorpio, over the Moon's sign, runs from Jan to May 2030 and again from Sep 2030 to Feb 2031, linked with displacement, expenses and quarrels. Sagittarius from Feb to Jun 2031 is the 2nd from the Moon, moolatrikona: money, home and respected speech. Capricorn from Mar to Aug 2032 is the 3rd from the Moon and debilitated, so the texts say its work-related obstacles are heavier.

Double transits (Jupiter and Saturn both acting on one house from the lagna) cluster in the 10th (career) from Aug 2029 to May 2032, the 4th (home and property) from Apr 2030 to Oct 2031, the 9th (fortune and teachers) from Jun 2027 to Jan 2030, the 11th (gains and friends) from Dec 2028 to Jan 2030, the 5th (children and creative work) from Nov 2026 to Nov 2027, and the 1st, 5th and 11th together from Aug to Oct 2032. Use these for timing decisions about home and career, not as predictions of events.

The current yogini is Sankata, Rahu's period of eight years (Mar 2026 to Mar 2034). The yogini texts treat it as the most demanding of the set: setbacks, health strain, separations, legal or official trouble and confusion. Rahu's seat in the 6th is among the better ones for overcoming rivals, which tempers the tone; the texts ask for patience and care rather than fixed outcomes. Mangala, the Moon's one-year yogini (Mar 2034 to Mar 2035), is middling because the Moon is debilitated, with home events, devotion and comfort. Siddha, Venus's yogini, has just ended (Mar 2019 to Mar 2026); the texts describe it as a time of success and comfort, which you can check against those years. Chara periods, dates only, as a timing marker: Vrishabha from Jul 2023 to Jul 2035, Mithuna from Jul 2035 to Jul 2036, and Karka from Jul 2036 to Jul 2043.

### The next ten years at a glance

The key stretch is Mars within Mars (Jun to Nov 2031), the yogakaraka's own stretch, with its softened weakness and a double transit on home that same year. The brightest stretches are Moon within Venus (2029 to 2030) and Mars within Venus (2036 to 2037), while the most testing are the current Moon within Saturn stretch, the Ashtama Dhaiya from May 2032, and Mars within Saturn (Oct 2033 to Dec 2034).
