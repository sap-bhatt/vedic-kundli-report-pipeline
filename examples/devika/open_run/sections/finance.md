## Finance and wealth

Leo rising gives your money houses an unusual shape. The 2nd house (savings and family money) and the 11th house (income and gains) are both ruled by Mercury, and Mercury sits in the 12th house of expenses. Two strong supports pull the other way: Jupiter in the 5th house, in its moolatrikona sign, and Mars, which rules the 4th and 9th houses and so is a yogakaraka (a planet that rules both a kendra, an angular house, and a trikona, a fortunate house, and works strongly for this lagna). The overall pattern is money that can be earned well but moves out easily unless it is managed on purpose.

### The wealth houses, one by one

**2nd house: savings.** Virgo is ruled by Mercury, which sits in the 12th house in Cancer, an enemy sign for it. Savings are hard to keep, and money tends to be spent before it builds. Some classical renderings add that the person leans on others' resources; Raman is milder and speaks of respectable income. Mercury is also weak in the navamsa (D9, a finer divisional chart), where it is debilitated.

**11th house: income and gains.** The same planet, Mercury, rules gains. Gains tend to flow out, often toward travel, charity or distant ventures, and Raman links this house to fines and penalties. Jupiter's aspect on the 11th is the counterweight: it brings steady, honourable income through institutions and good work. The texts treat it as a protective influence that is hopeful but not a guarantee.

**5th house: investments and merit.** Jupiter sits here in Sagittarius, where it is strong. Jataka Parijata links this house to choice riches, and Braha to successful investments and a liking for speculation. Jupiter also rules the 8th, which adds some caution, so gains here tend to come with ups and downs. Even so, this is one of the firmest supports for money in the chart.

**9th house: fortune.** Aries is ruled by Mars, which sits in the 12th house, close enough to the Sun to be overpowered (combust). Classical texts count a 9th lord in the 12th as a drain on fortune, and Raman links it to a hard start and money that often runs short. The balance comes from Mars itself. As a yogakaraka it is strong for Leo, and its debilitation in Cancer is cancelled: the Moon, which rules its sign, sits in a kendra, and Mars is exalted in the navamsa. Brihat Jataka links Mars in Cancer to wealth from water or farming, earned despite hardship. Jupiter's aspect on the 9th brings good fortune (Saravali). Fortune here comes through effort and blessing rather than ease.

**12th house: expenses.** The Moon rules this house and sits in the 4th, in Scorpio, where it is debilitated. That weakness is softened, because Venus, lord of the Moon's exaltation sign, is placed in a kendra. Spending is a real part of this chart, and the texts ask for care with it. The choices remain yours.

### Dhana yogas and gains against expenses

Dhana yoga is a classical combination that links the lords of the wealth houses. Three are present, each a conjunction in the 12th house: Mars with Mercury, Mars with the Sun, and Mercury with the Sun. Uttara Kalamrita 4.5 links the 2nd and 11th lords to affluence. Here one planet carries both, so the earning power is real, but the combination sits in the house of expenses, and the outflow comes with it.

Sunapha, Jupiter's pattern, points to wealth built personally; Raman stresses that it has much influence on whether finances rise or fall. Shakata, a classical pattern of ups and downs, is present but cancelled. The texts describe losses followed by recovery, and Raman finds such hardship usually mild in practice.

Gains and expenses share one planet, so the net result depends more on spending habits than on fate. Jupiter's aspect on the gains house keeps steady income in view. Income may flow to travel, charity or distant ventures, and a budget with a habit of saving first helps.

### Overall

The strongest factor is Mercury's placement. It ties the 2nd and 11th houses to the house of expenses, and that dominates the reading. Jupiter (in the 5th, aspecting the 9th and 11th) and Mars (a yogakaraka with a cancelled debilitation) provide the support. The balance is a person who can earn through effort and merit, and who does best with steady saving and careful budgeting. Decisions about money, property and investments stay with you; the chart points to patterns, not to outcomes.

### When this area is most active

The Moon-Mercury sub-period, April 2027 to September 2028, is mixed and demanding for money matters, with Mercury as sub-lord of both wealth houses. It is a time to budget carefully rather than the best time for large moves. The Mars-Jupiter sub-period, November 2032 to October 2033, is the supportive one, with Jupiter as sub-lord ruling the 5th. Double-transit spells on the 11th house, when Jupiter and Saturn both bear on the gains house, fall in December 2028 to March 2029 and in October 2029 to January 2030. The texts treat these as markers for income matters, not as promised events.
