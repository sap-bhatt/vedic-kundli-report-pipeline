## Chara karaka meanings (library)

- Sun is the Darakaraka (`karaka/darakaraka`): The Darakaraka is the planet with the lowest degree in its sign and signifies the spouse; the planet itself (not the 7th from it) describes the partner. Governs: wife or husband (Jaimini 1.1.18; BPHS 32.13-17 'Streekaraka'; Raman art. 14)
- Moon is the Putrakaraka (`karaka/putrakaraka`): The Putrakaraka is the fifth planet by degree in the 7-karaka scheme (sixth in the 8-karaka) and signifies children; with the Atmakaraka it forms a key raja yoga. Governs: children (Jaimini 1.1.16; BPHS 32.13-17; Raman art. 12); subordinates and followers (PVR 8.2 note)
- Mars is the Matrikaraka (`karaka/matrikaraka`): The Matrikaraka is the fourth-highest planet by degree and signifies the mother; in the 7-karaka tradition it may also stand for children. Governs: the mother (Jaimini 1.1.14 Abhyankar / 1.1.15 B.S. Rao; BPHS 32.13-17; Raman art. 11); children as well, for those who merge the mother and son karakas (Jaimini 1.1.19; BPHS 32.13-17)
- Mercury is the Atmakaraka (`karaka/atmakaraka`): The Atmakaraka is the planet with the highest degree within its sign; it stands for the self or soul and is the 'king' of the chart, whose condition colours the whole horoscope and governs bondage and liberation. Governs: the native's self and soul (Jaimini 1.1.10-11 Abhyankar numbering; BPHS 32.3-8); bondage and release - misery and happiness (Jaimini 1.1.11 Abhyankar / 1.1.12 B.S. Rao); the main reference point for the karakamsa (its navamsa sign) and for raja yogas, sustenance and spiritual progress
- Jupiter is the Gnatikaraka (`karaka/gnatikaraka`): The Gnatikaraka is the sixth planet by degree in the 7-karaka scheme (seventh in the 8-karaka) and signifies cousins and kinsfolk; it is widely used for rivals and obstacles. Governs: kinsfolk and cousins (Jaimini 1.1.17; B.S. Rao; Raman art. 13); rivals and enemies in common use; literal meaning paternal cousin (PVR 8.2 note)
- Venus is the Amatyakaraka (`karaka/amatyakaraka`): The Amatyakaraka is the planet with the second-highest degree; as the 'minister' to the Atmakaraka it shows counsel, career and the means by which the native rises. Governs: ministers and advisers (Jaimini 1.1.12 Abhyankar / 1.1.13 B.S. Rao; PVR 8.2); profession (P.S. Sastri on Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4); career elevation and social status (Murthy ch. 10)
- Saturn is the Bhratrikaraka (`karaka/bhratrikaraka`): The Bhratrikaraka is the third-highest planet by degree and signifies brothers and sisters; some read it also for courage and manliness. Governs: brothers and sisters (Jaimini 1.1.13 Abhyankar / 1.1.14 B.S. Rao; Raman art. 10; PVR 8.2); valour and manliness (P.S. Sastri on Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4)
- Karakamsha Pisces (`karaka/karakamsha_pisces`): With the Atmakaraka in Pisces navamsa, Parasara and Jaimini point to steady righteousness and the aim of final liberation (moksha).

- **Raja (kendra-trikona lords)** (calculator): pairs: (pair: Mars, Sun; how: conjunct; houses_owned: (Mars: 4, 9; Sun: 1))
  - Library `yoga/raja_kendra_trikona` (high): A link (conjunction, exchange, mutual aspect, or one placed in the other's kind of house) between the lord of an angle (1, 4, 7, 10) and the lord of a trine (1, 5, 9) is the basic Parashari Raja Yoga for status, power and prosperity.
  - Weight: Common. PVR (11.7.2) says the conjunction of a quadrant lord and a trine lord is found in many charts and its magnitude depends on strength and closeness.
  - Timing: Laghu Parashari (Verma sl. 18-19, 34): greatly auspicious results come in the dasa and bhukti of the yogakaraka planets. Related inauspicious planets give mixed results in their bhuktis. Phaladeepika 20.54: in a yogakaraka's dasa, Raja Yoga begins in the bhuktis of the karaka planets and is increased by malefic bhuktis that follow. Raman (300 Combinations, Introduction): the stronger of the two yoga planets fulfils most of the yoga in its own dasa and the weaker as sub-lord. Jataka Parijata 7.66 note: Raja Yogas fructify in the dasa/antardasa of the lord or occupant of the lagna or 10th.
  - Phrasing: Present it as potential for rising responsibility and recognition, not guaranteed power or wealth. Because many charts have some kendra-trikona link, mention it only when the planets are strong and close, and tie it to the dashas of those planets.
