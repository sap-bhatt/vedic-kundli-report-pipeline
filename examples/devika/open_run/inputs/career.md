- `planet_in_house/venus/10` (high, accepted) - Venus occupies house 10: Venus in the 10th is read as renown, honour and wealth, often through women, land or the arts; all classical texts read are favourable.
  - For this section (career): Decoration, fashion, entertainment, hospitality, luxury, diplomacy, counselling (PanchangBodh); property and buildings; with Saturn, cosmetics and women's goods (Raman).
  - Strengthened by: Exalted (Pisces) or own sign (Taurus, Libra): Brihat Jataka 20.11 scales a benefic's good results from full in exaltation to half in own sign; Bhrigu Sutras repeatedly makes the best results depend on dignity or benefic company; In a kendra in Taurus, Libra or Pisces it forms Malavya Yoga (PanchangBodh; Bhrigu Sutras commentary); With Mercury, Jupiter and the Moon: vehicles, religious ceremonies, wide fame, many Raja yogas (Bhrigu Sutras 61)

  - Phrasing: Present as a career in beauty, arts, hospitality, property or diplomacy with good public standing. Avoid guaranteeing fame.
