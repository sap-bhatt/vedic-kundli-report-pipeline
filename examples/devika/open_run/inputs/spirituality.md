- `conjunction/sun_mercury` (explained in Personality) - Sun with Mercury in house 12: Sun with Mercury forms Budha-Aditya yoga: intelligence, skill, learning and a good name. Most authorities rate it well; its strength depends on Mercury not being too deeply combust.
- `conjunction/sun_ketu` (explained in Personality) - Sun with Ketu in house 12: Sun with Ketu is treated in modern sources as an eclipse of the Sun: the Sun's significations are obstructed or turned inward. Frawley adds that Ketu can boost a planet that is strong in its own or exaltation sign.
- `conjunction/mars_mercury` (medium, accepted_caveats) - Mars with Mercury in house 12: Mars with Mercury points classically to medicine, herbs, oils and drugs, metal and building trades, and combative skill; Jataka Parijata adds eloquence. Wealth and marriage are often described as weak.
  - General results (not specific to this area; use only what fits): work with medicines, herbs, oils or drugs; skill in wrestling or boxing; sharp, eloquent speech
  - Strengthened by: Planetary war: if the two are within about a degree, the winner gains and the loser weakens; a planet that wins is strong and auspicious (Saravali 4.36, 5.40).; Saravali 15.23: good vargas increase the results.
  - Weakened by: Planetary war: the planet that is southern, dim or small loses (Brihat Samhita 17.9); Frawley instead says the planet with the lower longitude wins. A defeated planet loses its auspiciousness and can give bad results in full (Saravali 5.2-4, 5.27).; Saravali 15.23: bad vargas reduce the results.
  - Disagreements: All texts read share the medicine/drugs theme; Jataka Parijata is positive (eloquence, arts) where Saravali is negative on wife and wealth. Planetary-war winner: Brihat Samhita (northern and brighter wins) versus Frawley (lower longitude wins).
  - Phrasing: Present as a quick, practical, debating mind with aptitude for medicine, technical or hands-on trades. Do not predict marital misfortune.
- `conjunction/mars_ketu` (low, accepted_caveats) - Mars with Ketu in house 12: Mars with Ketu: Frawley treats Ketu as Mars-like and says it boosts a strong Mars (e.g. great military prowess); Raman warns of danger when Mars is afflicted by Ketu in the 6th. The classical two-planet chapters read do not cover it.
  - General results (not specific to this area; use only what fits): sharp, sudden energy
  - Strengthened by: Frawley: Ketu gives considerable power to a planet in its own sign or exalted (Mars in Aries, Scorpio or Capricorn).
  - Weakened by: Raman: affliction of Mars by Ketu is unfavourable.
  - Disagreements: Frawley allows a strongly positive result with a dignified Mars; Raman, speaking of the 6th house and affliction, is negative.
  - Phrasing: Describe focused, sudden bursts of courage and a need for caution with impulsive action. Never mention poisoning or death.
- `conjunction/mercury_ketu` (explained in Mind) - Mercury with Ketu in house 12: Mercury with Ketu has little sourced material: Raman groups Ketu with Rahu and Saturn as afflictions of Mercury that can bring despondency or excitability, and Frawley's node rules apply.
- `aspect/jupiter/9` (high, accepted_caveats) - Jupiter aspects house 9: Jupiter aspecting the 9th gives piety, pilgrimages and dutifulness to parents; his own aspect on the 9th (as a strong planet from the lagna or 5th) makes one very fortunate.
  - For this section (spirituality): Piety, pilgrimage, respect for teachers (Raman HJH vol. 2 ch. XIII).
  - Strengthened by: Saravali 32.4: as 9th lord (Cancer and Leo lagnas) his aspect keeps fortune at home.; Saravali 32.26-27: a benefic aspect relieves or improves a malefic in the 9th.
  - Weakened by: Saravali 32.4: if Jupiter does not own the 9th, fortune tends to come abroad.
  - Disagreements: Strength scheme: BPHS 26.3, Brihat Jataka 2.13, Jataka Parijata 2.30 and Raman/Frawley give 3rd/10th = 1/4, 5th/9th = 1/2, 4th/8th = 3/4, 7th = full; Saravali 4.32-33 (Santhanam, as scanned) gives half to the 4th/8th and a different (illegible) fraction to the 5th/9th. Phaladeepika 4.9: some hold only the 7th aspect fully effective, others hold the special aspects equally effective.
  - Phrasing: Speak of faith, generosity, mentors and good fortune. Describe Jupiter's aspect as protection, growth, wisdom and blessing on that house; keep it hopeful but not a guarantee.
- `karaka/atmakaraka` (high, accepted) - Mercury is the Atmakaraka: The Atmakaraka is the planet with the highest degree within its sign; it stands for the self or soul and is the 'king' of the chart, whose condition colours the whole horoscope and governs bondage and liberation.
  - For this section (spirituality): Jaimini 1.1.11: the AK rules bondage and liberation. B.S. Rao: exalted or benefic-joined AK gives release, residence in holy places and moksha; debilitated or malefic-joined AK gives confinement and denies moksha unless benefics aspect. Rath: a malefic AK suggests a more advanced soul than a benefic one; a combust AK gives spiritual insight; a retrograde AK shows a deep-rooted desire behind the birth. PVR 14.3: the lord of the 8th from the AK (Maheswara) shows the channels through which the soul seeks liberation.
  - Strengthened by: exaltation, moolatrikona, own or friendly sign (Murthy ch. 2; B.S. Rao on 1.1.12); benefic conjunction or aspect (B.S. Rao on 1.1.12); conjunction with the Putrakaraka or Amatyakaraka for raja yoga (PVR 11.7.3, 11.8; Murthy ch. 7)
  - Weakened by: debilitation, combustion or an inimical sign (Murthy ch. 2) - though Rath reads combustion of the AK as giving spiritual insight; malefic conjunction or aspect (B.S. Rao on 1.1.12; Raman art. 129)
  - Disagreements: 7- vs 8-karaka: BPHS 32.1-2 records both views without choosing; Jaimini 1.1.19 says some authorities merge mother and son karakas.
  - Phrasing: Describe the Atmakaraka as the planet that shows the person's core drive and life lessons. Do not call a malefic or weak AK bad luck; frame it as the area of growth. Avoid predictions of imprisonment, death or moksha as certainties.
- `karaka/karakamsha_pisces` (high, accepted) - karakamsha (AK Mercury in D9 Pisces): With the Atmakaraka in Pisces navamsa, Parasara and Jaimini point to steady righteousness and the aim of final liberation (moksha).
  - For this section (spirituality): BPHS 33.2-8: grants final emancipation; Jaimini 1.2.13: devoted to dharma and kaivalya; Murthy ch. 6: philanthropist, attains salvation at the end.
  - Strengthened by: benefic aspect (BPHS 33.2-8); B.S. Rao on 1.2.13 (quoting old commentators): benefics in kendras from the karakamsha give wealth or rule; a well-placed planet next to the AK gives heaven after death
  - Weakened by: malefic aspect (BPHS 33.2-8)
  - Disagreements: Which sign: BPHS, B.S. Rao, Raman, PVR and Murthy take the Atmakaraka's navamsa sign; Murthy also uses the same sign in the rasi chart ('karakamsa rasi') and notes that some read 'swamsa' as the navamsa lagna; Abhyankar's translation speaks only of the 'division occupied by' the significator, counted from Aries; Kalidasa (Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4) reads the Atmakaraka's rasi position, which P.S. Sastri rejects.
  - Phrasing: The sign results are old, often literal (animals, water, falls); present them as areas to be careful with or symbolic themes, never as predicted accidents or diseases. Lead with supportive themes where sources give them, and do not state illegitimacy, adultery or criminality claims to a user.
