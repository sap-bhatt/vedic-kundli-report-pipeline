## Chart at a glance

Leo rises with the Sun as its lord, and much of the chart's activity gathers in the twelfth house. The themes below form a map of the whole chart. Each one points to the section that explains it in full.

### The chart's shape

The fourth and tenth houses are both occupied: the Moon sits in the fourth, and Venus and Saturn share the tenth. The first and seventh houses are empty. Five planets fall in the sixth, eighth and twelfth houses, the traditionally harder places: the Sun, Mars, Mercury and Ketu in the twelfth, and Rahu in the sixth. In the navamsa (D9), the divisional chart that the texts link with partnership and inner values, the Sun sits in its moolatrikona sign and Mars is exalted.

Sign lordship loops back on itself. The Moon and Mars hold each other's signs, and the chains of the Sun and Mercury end in that loop. Jupiter, in its own sign, leads only back to itself, so it is not the chart's final dispositor (the planet at the end of the chain). Venus, in its own sign, is the final dispositor of Saturn.

### The lagna's star

The lagna's star is Purva Phalguni, ruled by Venus, which sits in the tenth house. Its traditional theme is pleasure, union and warmth. Personality and Relationships develop that theme.

### Six themes that define this chart

1. **A lagna lord in the twelfth.** The Sun, lord of Leo, sits in friendly Cancer in house 12, with Mars, Mercury and Ketu. Its results come with more effort because of the twelfth house, but its strong navamsa position helps them come through. Personality covers the Sun, and Spirituality covers the twelfth house.

2. **Mars: debilitated, cancelled, and a yogakaraka.** Mars is debilitated in Cancer, but the debility is cancelled on three of six tests, and it is exalted in D9. It is combust, close enough to the Sun to be outshone by it. It rules the fourth and ninth houses, which makes it the yogakaraka, the planet that rules both a kendra (angular house) and a trikona (trinal house). Relationships covers the Manglik question, which the chart cancels.

3. **The Moon's debility, partly softened.** The Moon in Scorpio, house 4, is debilitated, but only one of six tests cancels it, so the softening is partial. As a natural benefic, it stays supportive of the mind and the home. Mind covers the Moon in full.

4. **Venus and Saturn in the tenth.** Venus, in its own sign, and Saturn, in a friendly one, share the house of work and public standing, within five degrees of each other. Venus is the planet that Saturn's sign chain leads back to, so the pair is anchored in Venus's steadier base. Career covers the pair.

5. **Jupiter: moolatrikona, and the only planet aspecting the lagna.** Jupiter in Sagittarius, house 5, sits in its moolatrikona sign and is retrograde. It rules the fifth and the eighth houses, so its gifts come with a testing side, since the eighth is a harder house. Family covers the fifth house, and Spirituality covers the eighth.

6. **Rahu and Ketu: vargottama nodes.** Rahu, in house 6 in Capricorn, and Ketu, in house 12 in Cancer, are both vargottama, meaning they occupy the same sign in D1 and D9. Texts treat that as a source of strength and reliability. Ketu sits with the Sun, Mars and Mercury, and Rahu lies in Saturn's sign. Health covers Rahu, and Spirituality covers Ketu.

### Strengths and tensions in one view

Strengths come first. Mars is a yogakaraka, exalted in D9, with its debility cancelled. Venus is in its own sign in the tenth, Jupiter is in moolatrikona, and the Sun is in moolatrikona in D9. The Sun and Mars form a raja combination, the link between a kendra lord and a trikona lord, which the texts connect with standing and influence. The Sun, Mars and Mercury each take part in a dhana (wealth) combination. The vargottama nodes add steadiness.

Tensions come next. The twelfth house is crowded, and Mars is combust there, so the chart's energy is concentrated and turned inward. Mercury, the Atmakaraka (the planet that stands for the self and the chart's core drive), sits in an enemy's sign and is debilitated in D9. Venus is debilitated in D9, in Virgo, so its navamsa promise falls short of its good placement in D1. The Moon and Mars are debilitated in D1, though both are cancelled.

### Birth-time sensitivity

The lagna sits 18.9 degrees into Leo, so the sign changes only if the birth time is off by about 45 minutes. The lagna's divisional positions (D9 at 66% of its division, D10 at 29%) are not at the edges where a few minutes could change them. D10 still depends closely on the birth time, so treat D10 readings as provisional. The Moon moves about half a degree an hour, so its sign and nakshatra are far less sensitive than the lagna and the divisional charts.

### Functional roles for this lagna

For a Leo lagna, Mars is the yogakaraka and the Sun is the lagna lord. Jupiter rules a trikona (the fifth) and the eighth house. Venus rules a kendra (the tenth) and a house of effort and gain (the third). Mercury rules the eleventh, another house of effort and gain, and the second. Saturn rules the seventh, a kendra, and the sixth, which is both a harder house and one of effort. The Moon rules the twelfth. Rahu and Ketu rule no houses.
