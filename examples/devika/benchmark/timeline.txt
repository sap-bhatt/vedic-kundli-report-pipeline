11Timeline

Parashari timing rests on the Vimshottari dasha system: the life is divided into nine major periods of fixed lengths, starting from the lord of the Moon's nakshatra at birth. Devika was born in the Mercury mahadasha (Jyeshtha is Mercury's nakshatra) with about 15.9 years remaining. A planet's period brings forward what that planet signifies and rules in the chart; transits of the slow planets (Jupiter and Saturn) then indicate when those promises are triggered.

The current period: Moon mahadasha (Jun 2021 – Jun 2031)

In this chart the Moon:

Occupies the 4th house (home, mother, property, peace), a kendra, debilitated but bright, in the "adult" avastha, and cancelled through Venus.

Rules the 12th house (expense, retreat, foreign, liberation) and is in exchange with Mars, the 4th and 9th lord, so the period also carries Mars's matters.

Aspects the 10th house and its occupants Venus and Saturn, and receives their aspects in return: career and emotional life are linked throughout.

Parashara's chapter on dasha results (BPHS ch. 47) says a strong Moon in a kendra gives good results in its period (v. 17), that a debilitated or waning Moon brings loss of wealth (v. 23), and that a Moon that is strong but unfavourably placed gives mixed results for which propitiation is advised (v. 26). This Moon is strong by brightness and house but weak by sign: expect a mixed, emotionally significant decade centred on home, family and inner life, costly in expenditure, and improving in its second half as stronger sub-period lords (Venus above all) take over.

Moon–Saturn (Sep 2025 – Apr 2027): now

Saturn rules the 6th and 7th, and sits in the 10th, a kendra, in Venus's friendly sign, 7th from the Moon. BPHS (ch. 53, vv. 32–34) gives Saturn's sub-period in the Moon's major period good results when Saturn is in a kendra or trikona or in its own sign or navamsa: gains in business, growth of home and land, favour from those in power, and support from children and friends. The kendra condition holds. But Saturn's lordships are functional malefic ones (service, health, partnership) and it exchanges a full aspect with the Moon: heavy responsibility at work, care for dependants, attention to a partner's affairs and health routines, and a serious emotional tone, alongside solid progress in work and property.

Transits frame it. Saturn in Pisces (8th from the lagna, 5th from the Moon) is demanding, while Jupiter on the lagna protects; from 26 January 2027 Jupiter moves to Cancer, the favourable 9th from the Moon. The Rahu–Ketu axis is due to shift around 5 December 2026 (per published Lahiri panchangs), bringing Rahu into her 6th house, a good position for it, and Ketu over her 12th-house planets. The remaining months suit steady delivery, conserving energy and preparing for the Mercury sub-period.

Secondary timers: Yogini and Chara dashas

Yogini dasha is a shorter 36-year cycle of eight "Yoginis", each linked to a planet, used by many practitioners as a confirming timer.

Siddha (Venus), 23 Mar 2019 – 23 Mar 2026: a benefic Yogini of accomplishment, wealth and relationships; it softened the end of the Sade Sati and the Kantaka Dhaiya.

Sankata (Rahu), 23 Mar 2026 – 23 Mar 2034: traditionally the most difficult Yogini (loss, illness, disruption); modern practitioners read it as upheaval and reinvention. Here Rahu is vargottama in the 6th, an upachaya house, among its better placements (Phaladeepika ch. 8, sl. 26: wealth and resilience against adversaries). The fair reading is pressure she is equipped to meet.

Mangala (Moon), 23 Mar 2034 – 23 Mar 2035: a benefic Yogini; relief and good fortune after Sankata.

Chara dasha (Jaimini's sign-based system, popularised in modern India by K.N. Rao and Sanjay Rath) times life by signs rather than planets.

Vrishabha (Taurus), 23 Jul 2023 – 23 Jul 2035: the sign of her 10th house, holding the Amatyakaraka Venus in its own sign and Saturn, and aspected by Jaimini sign-aspect from Cancer (the Atmakaraka Mercury and Darakaraka Sun) and Capricorn (Rahu). Twelve years with career, standing and partnership central.

Mithuna (Gemini), 23 Jul 2035 – 22 Jul 2036: the 11th house, empty, ruled by Mercury: gains, networks, siblings and friends.

Karka (Cancer), 22 Jul 2036 – 23 Jul 2043: the 12th house with the Atmakaraka, Darakaraka, Mars and Ketu: a long inward, spiritual and possibly foreign chapter.

The key window of the decade

The stretch that stands out is April 2029 to early 2031: the Moon–Venus sub-period, Saturn over the natal 10th house, Jupiter over the natal Moon and the Taurus Chara dasha all point to recognition, a meaningful role and lasting decisions about home. From June 2031 the yogakaraka's mahadasha follows, opened under Jupiter's return to Sagittarius. The more demanding stretch is mid-2032 to mid-2034, when the Ashtama Dhaiya overlaps the last years of Sankata Yogini; it is a time to keep life simple and routines strong, not one to fear.
