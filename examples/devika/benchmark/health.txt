6Health and vitality

Please read first

This section describes only what traditional astrology associates with this chart. It is not medical advice, is not a diagnosis, and should never replace a doctor's opinion or routine screening appropriate to her age. Astrological "indicators" describe areas that tradition says deserve attention, not illnesses that will happen.

Overall constitution

Vitality in jyotish is judged mainly from the lagna, the lagna lord, the Sun and the Moon. The lagna is Leo, a fiery, strong sign, and it is aspected by Jupiter from its own sign, which is classically one of the most protective factors for the body. The lagna lord and natural vitality significator, the Sun, is in the 12th house, the house of rest, sleep and hospitals; Parashara's line that the lagna lord in the 12th lacks bodily comfort (BPHS ch. 24, v. 12) is best read here as a constitution that is fundamentally sound but needs rest to be protected deliberately. The 8th lord Jupiter is strong in the 5th, and the 6th house holds Rahu, which Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 26) associates with long life and the capacity to overcome adversaries, a placement modern authors read as good fighting power against illness. Overall: good recuperative power, a tendency to overextend, and a body that responds well to regular sleep and calm.

Timing notes

In traditional practice, health deserves extra attention during Saturn's transit through the 8th house from the lagna (Pisces, now until June 2027, with a return from October 2027 to February 2028); Saturn's Ashtama Dhaiya, its transit 8th from the Moon (Gemini, May 2032 to July 2034); the short Moon–Sun sub-period (December 2030 – June 2031), the Sun being in the 12th; and the opening phase of the Mars mahadasha from June 2031, Mars being in the 12th. None of these implies illness; they are windows when tradition advises regular check-ups, steady routines and not overloading the body.

Some classical texts make statements about longevity for individual placements. Those statements are not meant to be read in isolation, and longevity judgement is deliberately not part of this report.
