The 2nd and 11th lord in the 12th

The single most important financial fact in this chart is that Mercury rules both wealth houses (the 2nd of savings and the 11th of gains) and sits in the 12th house of expenditure. Parashara is direct about both lordships: the 2nd lord in the 12th gives a person who has courage but not much accumulated wealth and who relies on others' fortune (BPHS ch. 24, v. 24), and the 11th lord in the 12th gives constant spending on virtuous causes (v. 132). In practice this rarely means poverty; it means a life in which money is a means rather than a store. Income is spent on the household, on relatives, on travel or relocation, on health, on learning and on charitable or religious causes, and saving requires a conscious system.

The wealth combination in the 12th

The computation records a Dhana yoga (wealth combination) between the Sun (1st lord), Mars (9th lord) and Mercury (2nd and 11th lord), all conjoined. The lords of self, fortune, savings and gains together are a genuine wealth combination, but their location in the 12th conditions it: money is made in connection with distant or foreign places, large institutions (hospitals, universities, government, charities), work done privately, and is also directed back into those same areas. Parashara adds that the 9th lord in the 12th brings constant expense on auspicious works and on hospitality (v. 108). Mars, the yogakaraka, is combust and debilitated, which delays the result; its exaltation in the navamsa and exchange with the Moon suggest that what is built through this route grows in the second half of life.

Earned income: the 10th house

Against the outflow in the 12th, the 10th house is strong and self-supporting: Venus, the 10th lord and Amatyakaraka, in its own sign, with Saturn. Steady salaried or professional income, authority over budgets, and money linked to valuables, design, administration or care are well indicated. Phaladeepika (ch. 6, sl. 6) gives Sunapha yoga (here Jupiter in the 2nd from the Moon) self-earned wealth and a reputation for wisdom and wealth. Saturn's presence makes accumulation slow and dependable rather than spectacular.

Investments and the 5th house

Jupiter in its own sign in the 5th, aspecting the 11th, is the chart's best financial protector. It favours sound, patient, principled investment, long-horizon assets, and gains through knowledge, children, students or advisory work. Jupiter also rules the 8th, the house of others' money (inheritance, insurance, a spouse's resources), so these sources can contribute too. The 5th is unaspected by malefics, which supports good financial judgement.

Property

The 12th lord Moon sits in the 4th house of property and exchanges signs with the 4th lord Mars. Parashara's line for the 12th lord in the 4th speaks of repeated losses connected with land, vehicles and houses (v. 136), and for the 4th lord in the 12th of a lack of domestic comforts (v. 48). The modern reading of a 4th–12th exchange is a home that is far from the place of birth or abroad, significant spending on property and its upkeep, or more than one residence. The exchange also means each planet is, in effect, in its own sign, so the home is ultimately a source of security, but one that costs money and attention.

Fluctuation

Shakata yoga (the Moon 12th from Jupiter) is cancelled under several classical rules, but its theme of financial ups and downs is worth noting together with the 12th-house outflow. A wise approach is to treat good years as the time to build reserves.

Overall

The wealth picture is of strong earning (10th house) paired with heavy, largely meaningful expenditure (12th house), protected by a good Jupiter. The key discipline is structural: automatic saving, clear separation of family and personal funds, and a ceiling on giving. The best financial windows in the coming years are the Moon–Mercury sub-period (April 2027 – September 2028), when the wealth lord's own sub-period coincides with Jupiter transiting the 2nd house from the lagna (11th from the Moon) and Saturn moving to the favourable 6th from the Moon, and the Moon–Venus sub-period (April 2029 – December 2030). The current transit of Saturn through the 5th from the Moon (to June 2027) is traditionally a time for caution with speculation.
