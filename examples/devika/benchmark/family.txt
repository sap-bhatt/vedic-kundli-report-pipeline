8Family, home, siblings, children, education

Family of origin and speech (2nd house)

The 2nd house (family, speech, food, early values) is Virgo, empty, aspected only by Rahu; its lord Mercury is in the 12th. Parashara's line for the 2nd lord in the 12th (BPHS ch. 24, v. 24) speaks of courage without much accumulated wealth and of reliance on others' fortune. In family terms, this often describes a family that moved, lived away from its roots or spent heavily on its members; Rahu adds something unconventional. Speech is careful and precise, with an emotional undertone; when hurt, she can say less than she feels, then say it sharply.

Mother and home (4th house)

The 4th house (Scorpio) holds the debilitated Moon, the natural significator of the mother, and its lord Mars is debilitated in the 12th; the two are in exchange. Venus and Saturn aspect the 4th from the 10th, and Ketu also influences it. The texts give contrasting pictures. Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 6) gives the Moon in the 4th happiness, comforts, vehicles and friends. Parashara, by contrast, says that when the 12th lord is in the 4th the native lacks happiness from the mother and has losses through land, vehicles and houses (v. 136), and that the 4th lord in the 12th lacks domestic comforts (v. 48).

The balanced reading is a strong, protective, emotionally intense mother (Jyeshtha, Scorpio) with whom the bond is deep but not always easy; a mother who may have carried hardship, illness or great responsibility; and a pattern of living at some distance from her, physically or emotionally, at some point. The exchange with Mars means each planet is effectively "at home": what is difficult early tends to be repaired with maturity. More than one home, or a home far from the birthplace, is likely. The 4th house is activated by Jupiter over the natal Moon (2030–2031) and by the Mars mahadasha (from 2031), the 4th lord's period.

Father and fortune (9th house)

The 9th house (Aries) is empty but aspected by Jupiter, a strong protection for fortune, faith and the father. Its lord Mars is in the 12th, debilitated and combust, beside the Sun (the natural significator of the father) and Ketu. Parashara's line for the 9th lord in the 12th (v. 108) speaks of diminished fortune and constant expense on auspicious works; Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 4) associates the Sun in the 12th with estrangement from the father. The computation also flags the modern category of Pitra dosha (Sun with Ketu; Sun with Mars; the 9th lord with the Sun and Ketu). In practice this often coincides with a father who was away for long periods, abroad, religious or withdrawn, or whose life involved hardship, and with a sense that she had to find her own dharma. Mars's exaltation in the navamsa and Jupiter's aspect on the 9th soften the picture considerably. These are tendencies to understand, not judgements on the father.

Siblings (3rd and 11th houses)

The 3rd house (younger siblings, courage, skills) is Libra, empty, aspected by Mars; its lord Venus is in its own sign in the 10th. Parashara's verse for the 3rd lord in the 10th (v. 34) gives happiness and wealth earned through one's own efforts. Younger siblings are likely to be capable and successful, though Mars's aspect can bring rivalry or strong opinions. The 11th house (elder siblings) is empty, ruled by Mercury in the 12th and aspected by Jupiter: elder siblings may live at a distance or go their own way, with goodwill. Birth in Jyeshtha, a Ganda Moola nakshatra, is traditionally linked with effects on an elder sibling (sources disagree on which pada; one notes the classical texts are silent), offered only as a traditional note. Jyeshtha, "the eldest", often shows as taking the senior role in the family.

Children (5th house)

The 5th house is Sagittarius, Jupiter's own sign, occupied by Jupiter in moolatrikona, with no malefic aspects. Parashara's verse for the 5th lord in the 5th (v. 53) gives children when there is benefic influence, and a virtuous person fond of friends in any case. Jupiter is also the natural significator of children, which raises the classical maxim karako bhava nashaya ("the significator in its own house harms that house"); most modern authors apply it only when the karaka is weak or afflicted, and at most read it as a delayed first child or a small family. Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 15) adds that Jupiter in the 5th makes the native intelligent and fit to be a king's adviser, with some anxiety through children. The D7 (saptamsa) is mixed: its lagna lord Jupiter is debilitated in the D7 2nd and Saturn debilitated in the D7 5th, read as worry around children's lives rather than their absence.

The balanced reading is that children are well indicated, are likely to be intelligent and principled, and are a source of pride and also of worry; the relationship with them deepens as they mature. Jupiter's return to Sagittarius in 2031–2032 is a traditional window for milestones in children's or grandchildren's lives.

Education and learning

Jupiter strong in the 5th is one of the best indicators of a good intellect and of learning that continues through life. Mercury, the Atmakaraka, in the 12th gives a mind suited to languages, psychology, healing, literature, accounts and subjects studied in quiet. Her first Sade Sati (October 1982 – December 1990, ages 10–18) overlapped the later Mercury mahadasha and the start of Ketu's, a combination that often coincides with pressured school years and a serious, early-maturing mind. The Ketu mahadasha (1988–1995, ages 15–22), with Ketu in the 12th, often coincides with higher studies that are unconventional, interrupted, or pursued away from home.
