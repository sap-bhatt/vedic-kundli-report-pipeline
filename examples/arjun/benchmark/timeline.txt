11Timeline

Parashari timing rests on the Vimshottari dasha system: the life is divided into nine major periods of fixed lengths, starting from the lord of the Moon's nakshatra at birth. Arjun was born in the Jupiter mahadasha (Purva Bhadrapada is Jupiter's nakshatra) with about 9.3 years remaining. A planet's period brings forward what that planet signifies and rules in the chart; transits of the slow planets (Jupiter and Saturn) then indicate when those promises are triggered.

Past periods: validation notes

These are offered as checks. If the descriptions broadly fit, it supports the recorded birth time and the reading.

Jupiter (to Jan 2011, ages 0–9): the 9th lord's period in early childhood, traditionally a protected and fortunate start, with emphasis on learning, siblings or cousins (3rd house), and family values. Its last sub-period, Jupiter–Rahu (Aug 2008 – Jan 2011), activated the Guru-Chandal pair and overlapped Saturn's Ashtama Dhaiya (Saturn 8th from the Moon, Sep 2009 – Nov 2011): a phase often remembered for a change of school or home, a family upheaval, or some childhood illness.

Saturn–Saturn (Jan 2011 – Jan 2014, ages 9–12): the start of the 10th lord's long period, often a more serious, duty-bound phase of childhood, with family finances (2nd house) or a parent's work more prominent.

Saturn–Mercury (Jan 2014 – Sep 2016, ages 12–15): Mercury is 5th from Saturn and exalted; this often coincides with academic strength, competition and an analytical turn of mind.

Saturn–Ketu (Sep 2016 – Oct 2017, ages 15–16): Ketu is 8th from Saturn; a short, unsettled phase, sometimes with an abrupt change, loss of interest, or a period of introspection.

Saturn–Venus (Oct 2017 – Dec 2020, ages 16–19): the 2nd/7th lord in the 4th; important school-leaving exams, choices about higher education, home comforts or a move, and first serious romantic interests. Saturn entered Capricorn in January 2020 and began his first Sade Sati.

Saturn–Sun (Dec 2020 – Dec 2021, age 19–20): the 5th lord in its own sign, 4th from Saturn; often a year of study, self-assertion and dealings with authority or the father, under Sade Sati.

Saturn–Moon (Dec 2021 – Jul 2023, ages 20–21): the 4th lord, 10th from Saturn; Saturn crossed over the natal Moon in 2022–2023 (the peak of Sade Sati). Emotional pressure, home or family changes, and a strong role of friends and groups are typical.

Saturn–Mars (Jul 2023 – Aug 2024, ages 21–22): the lagna lord's sub-period. This commonly coincides with graduation, a first job or a decisive change of direction, and with assertive, sometimes conflictual energy.

The current period: Saturn mahadasha (Jan 2011 – Jan 2030)

Saturn rules the 10th (career) and 11th (gains) for Aries and sits in the 2nd (wealth, family, speech) in Taurus, a friendly sign, in Rohini (the Moon's nakshatra). It aspects the 4th, 8th and 11th houses, including Venus and the natal Moon. Parashara's chapter on Saturn's mahadasha (as summarised from BPHS) describes good results — recognition, prosperity and name — when Saturn is in its own, moolatrikona or a friendly sign, and difficulties when it is in the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitated or combust. Saturn here meets the favourable conditions and none of the unfavourable ones. The realistic expectation is a long, effortful but constructive period that builds career, earning capacity and responsibility, with much of the reward arriving in its second half.

Saturn–Rahu (Aug 2024 – Jun 2027): now

Rahu sits in the 3rd house (an upachaya house, where Rahu traditionally does well), in Gemini, with Jupiter, in its own nakshatra Ardra. It is 2nd from Saturn, the dasha lord. BPHS (as summarised) describes Rahu's sub-period in Saturn's major period as giving gains, increase and later good relations with those in power when Rahu is exalted, in its own sign, in a kendra, or in the 11th from the lagna or the dasha lord, and quarrels, mental agitation and trouble from authority otherwise. Neither the best nor the worst conditions are fully met: Rahu is in a good house for itself, is accompanied by a natural benefic, and its dispositor Mercury is exalted, but it is not in a kendra or the 11th.

This sub-period often coincides with ambition, restlessness and a strong push to acquire skills, contacts and visibility, through communication, media, technology, short travel or a bold change of role. Phaladeepika's line on Rahu in the 3rd (ch. 8, sl. 25), proud, strong-willed and wealthy, describes the positive side well. It overlaps the setting phase of Sade Sati, with Saturn in the 12th from the lagna, and Rahu's own transit over the natal Moon, so it is also likely to feel pressured, expensive and mentally busy. The last eight months of this sub-period are best used to finish what has been started, to consolidate skills and finances, and to prepare for the steadier Jupiter phase.

Saturn–Jupiter (Jun 2027 – Jan 2030): the key window

Jupiter is the 9th lord (fortune, higher learning, teachers, father) and 12th lord (foreign lands, expenses), in mutual aspect with the lagna lord Mars, and 2nd from Saturn. BPHS (as summarised) describes Jupiter's sub-period in Saturn's major period as giving all-round success, family well-being and favour from authority when Jupiter is linked with the lagna lord or is 2nd, 5th, 9th or 11th from the dasha lord — both of which hold here — and difficulties when it is in the 3rd, 6th or 12th from the lagna, as it is (3rd). On balance the favourable conditions dominate. This sub-period also activates the lagna lord–9th lord Raja and Dhana yoga.

It is the most promising stretch of the next few years for: higher or professional studies; a better position through a mentor or senior person; travel or work involving foreign links (12th lordship); and relationship commitment (Jupiter aspects the 7th natally, and the double transit falls in this sub-period, see section 7).

Three transit factors frame it. Sade Sati ends on 23 February 2028. Saturn moves into Aries, his lagna, from June 2027 (briefly back to Pisces from October 2027 to February 2028, then in Aries until 2029–2030). Saturn is debilitated in Aries and its transit over the lagna typically brings personal responsibility, fatigue and a sense of being tested; but from the Moon it is the 3rd, which Phaladeepika's transit chapter (ch. 26, sl. 22) counts as favourable for gaining position, helpers and money. Jupiter, meanwhile, returns to Leo on the very day the sub-period starts (27 June 2027), aspecting the lagna, the 9th and the natal Moon. The sub-period is therefore constructive at the level of position, learning and relationships, with personal stamina and patience the main things to manage.

The next mahadasha: Mercury (Jan 2030 – Jan 2047)

Mercury is the 3rd and 6th lord, exalted in its own sign in the 6th, forming Harsha yoga; it is the final dispositor of Mars, Jupiter and both nodes. BPHS (as summarised) gives Mercury's mahadasha good results — wealth, reputation, knowledge — when Mercury is exalted or in its own sign, but warns of illness, disputes and loss when it is in the 6th, 8th or 12th. Both apply; with the strict Harsha yoga present, the classical logic of Viparita yogas favours the reading that difficulties are met and overcome. The expectation is a period of specialist skill, competitive success, service and steady advancement through expertise, with health routines and disputes the areas to manage. Mercury is debilitated in the navamsa, which advises humility and care with contracts.

Saturn's current transit and Sade Sati

Saturn in Pisces is 2nd from the natal Moon, the third and last phase of Sade Sati. Phaladeepika's transit chapter (ch. 26, sl. 22) associates Saturn's transit of the 2nd from the Moon with trouble to wealth and children; from the lagna Saturn is in the 12th, the house of expenses, sleep and distance. The lived experience is usually high expenses, a sense of carrying a load, and a slow clearing of what is no longer needed. Many practitioners, including the school of Hart de Fouw and Robert Svoboda (Light on Life), stress that Sade Sati is a period of maturation rather than of automatic misfortune, and that its results depend on Saturn's role in the chart.

Secondary timers: Yogini and Chara dashas

Yogini dasha is a shorter 36-year cycle of eight "Yoginis", each linked to a planet, used by many practitioners as a confirming timer.

Sankata (Rahu), 4 Jan 2022 – 4 Jan 2030: traditionally the most difficult Yogini, associated with upheaval, loss and illness. One classical-style rule quoted in practitioner sources holds that if Rahu is joined by a benefic in an auspicious house its Sankata period can instead bring advancement; Rahu here is with Jupiter in the 3rd, an upachaya. That is how it should read: a pressured, transformative eight years that coincide with Sade Sati and the Rahu and Jupiter sub-periods, demanding but not without growth.

Mangala (Moon), 4 Jan 2030 – 4 Jan 2031: benefic; success and prosperity, with the full Moon as its planet.

Pingala (Sun), 4 Jan 2031 – 4 Jan 2033: traditionally malefic (worries, health), softened by the Sun's own-sign strength; overlaps Kantaka Dhaiya.

Dhanya (Jupiter), Jan 2033 – Jan 2036 (following in the fixed sequence): benefic, prosperity and favour, running almost exactly alongside Mercury–Venus.

Chara dasha (Jaimini's sign-based system, popularised in modern India by K.N. Rao and Sanjay Rath) times life by signs rather than planets.

Simha (Leo), 3 Sep 2020 – 3 Sep 2032: the sign of the 5th house, holding the own-sign Sun. Twelve years emphasising education, intelligence, creative work, romance and, later, children. In K.N. Rao's practice houses are counted from the dasha sign: the 10th from Leo is Taurus, holding Saturn, the Amatyakaraka (career significator), and the 7th from Leo holds the Atmakaraka Moon. That puts career-building and the self-in-relationship at the centre of this period, agreeing with the Saturn mahadasha.

Kanya (Virgo), 3 Sep 2032 – 3 Sep 2045: the sign of the 6th house, holding exalted Mercury. Thirteen years of work, service, competition and skill, agreeing with the Mercury mahadasha. By sign aspect Virgo receives the influence of Gemini (Jupiter, Rahu) and Sagittarius (Mars as Darakaraka, and Ketu), so partnership and principle are also active.

Tula (Libra), 3 Sep 2045 – 4 Sep 2054: the 7th-house sign; partnerships, public dealings and balance.
