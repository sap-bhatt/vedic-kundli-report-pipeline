4Career and vocation

The 10th house: empty Capricorn, aspected by Venus

The 10th house (career, reputation, action in the world) is Capricorn, ruled by Saturn. It holds no planets and receives only the aspect of Venus from the 4th. An empty 10th is not a weak career: it means the career is read mainly through its lord, the planets aspecting it, and the dashas. Capricorn is an earthy, movable sign of organisation, structure and long-term building; it describes a career that rises through patience, competence and responsibility rather than a single lucky break. Venus's aspect from the home sign Cancer adds diplomacy, pleasant manners and some link between work and comfort, finance, design or people-facing service. Venus is the 2nd and 7th lord, so money and partnerships are tied into professional life.

The 10th lord Saturn in the 2nd, with the 11th lordship

Saturn rules both the 10th and the 11th for Aries, and sits in Taurus in the 2nd house of wealth, speech, family and accumulated resources. Parashara (BPHS ch. 24) gives the 10th lord in the 2nd a person who is wealthy and endowed with good qualities (v. 110), and the 11th lord in the 2nd a person endowed with all kinds of wealth and accomplishments (v. 122). Phaladeepika's line on Saturn in the 2nd (ch. 8, sl. 21) is harsh, speaking of an unattractive face, little wealth and wrong ways; as usual a single-placement line is not meant to be read in isolation, and Saturn here is in a friendly sign as the lord of the two most practical houses of the chart. The modern reading, given by lagna-wise sources such as jagannathhora's notes on Saturn in the 2nd, is that career and income reinforce each other, that earning follows profession closely, and that wealth is built through discipline and saving rather than quick gains.

Saturn is in Rohini, the Moon's nakshatra, and the Moon is the 4th lord in the 11th. Career, home and gains therefore move together, and Saturn's aspects from the 2nd fall on the 4th (home, Venus), 8th and 11th (the Moon). For Aries lagna Saturn is a functional malefic and the badhaka lord, so it tends to give success after delay and hard work: onlinejyotish's lagna-wise lesson for Saturn puts it as "success in profession after hard work and gains through the masses".

Strong houses of effort: the 3rd and 6th

Two upachaya houses (houses that improve with effort and time) carry a great deal of this chart's working energy.

The 6th holds exalted Mercury in its own sign: a strong 6th house is classically the signature of someone who beats competition, solves problems, serves well and handles pressure. The 6th lord in the 6th forms Harsha yoga; Phaladeepika (ch. 6, sl. 63) credits it with happiness, good fortune, a strong constitution, wealth, friends and fame. This suits analytical, technical, medical, legal, audit and service work, and success in competitive examinations or interviews.

The 3rd holds Jupiter (9th lord) and Rahu. Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 25) describes Rahu in the 3rd as making a person proud, strong-willed and wealthy; Rahu is traditionally at its best in the 3rd, 6th and 11th. Jupiter in the 3rd is less comfortable (Phaladeepika ch. 8, sl. 14 speaks of being underrated and careful with money), but it brings the 9th house's fortune into skills, communication, writing, media, technology and self-driven initiative. Parashara's line for the 9th lord in the 3rd (v. 99) adds happiness through siblings.

Career significators: karakas

Ranking the seven visible planets by degree within their signs (the Jaimini chara karaka method) gives: Moon 25°32' as Atmakaraka, Saturn 20°36' as Amatyakaraka (the "minister", significator of career and counsel), then Sun, Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, and finally Mars as Darakaraka. If Rahu is included in the eight-karaka scheme (its degree counted backwards, 21°12'), Rahu becomes Amatyakaraka and Saturn moves down one place; the Atmakaraka and Darakaraka are unchanged.

So the career significator is Saturn (or Rahu with Saturn just behind it), the 10th lord itself in both schemes. That double emphasis supports the picture of a career built through persistence, structure and responsibility, possibly involving large organisations, technology or the public (Saturn and Rahu both relate to the masses and to systems).

The classical profession rule

Phaladeepika (ch. 5, sl. 1) derives profession from the navamsa lord of the 10th lord counted from the lagna, Moon or Sun, whichever is strongest. Here the Sun is strong in its own sign and the Moon is full, so all three references are worth reading.

Table 6. Profession indicators (Phaladeepika ch. 5)

 | Reference | 10th lord | Its D9 sign | Navamsa lord | Classical field, in modern terms

The readings are varied, which fits a chart whose career runs through upachaya houses and is shaped more by skill than by one fixed calling. The common thread is the Moon twice over: work that serves, feeds, cares for or deals directly with the public.

The D10 (dashamsa), with caution

As noted in section 1, the D10 lagna is about five to seven minutes of birth time from changing sign, so this chart is offered as fairly but not fully reliable.

If the computed D10 is right, its most striking feature is an exalted Mars, ruler of the D10 10th house, in the D10 7th with the Sun: a strong signature for leadership in business, partnerships, client work, contracts or dealing with the public, and for career strength that grows through decisive action. Saturn in the D10 lagna confirms that work and duty sit at the centre of his life.

Suitable fields and working style

Combining all of the above, the most consistent fields are: technology, data and analytical work (exalted Mercury, Rahu in the 3rd, Saturn/Rahu as career karakas); finance, banking, accounting and audit (Saturn in the 2nd, Mercury in the 6th); healthcare, medicine and wellness (Ashwini lagna, strong 6th, the Moon in the profession rule); law, compliance and dispute resolution (6th house, Mars in the 9th); communications, media, writing and marketing (the 3rd house with Jupiter and Rahu); management, education and administration (Sun in its own sign in the 5th). Engineering and property are a secondary thread through Mars.

Working style: fast, direct and self-starting (Aries, Mars), precise with detail (Mercury), more comfortable leading his own piece of work than being micro-managed, and steadily rewarded for persistence. The Saturn emphasis means promotions and recognition tend to come later than he would like but to last; the Mars–Ketu pair warns against walking out abruptly when frustrated.

Career timing in brief

Saturn mahadasha, Jan 2011 – Jan 2030 (ages 9–28): the 10th lord's own period, covering schooling, higher education and the start of working life. Its sub-periods of Mars (Jul 2023 – Aug 2024) and Rahu (Aug 2024 – Jun 2027) are the natural window for first jobs, early career moves and skill-building.

Saturn–Rahu, now: ambition, risk-taking, technology, media and change of role; uneven but energetic.

Saturn–Jupiter, Jun 2027 – Jan 2030: the 9th lord, linked with the lagna lord by mutual aspect. The most promising stretch of the next few years for higher studies, a better position, mentors, and possibly work involving travel or foreign links (Jupiter also rules the 12th).

Mercury mahadasha, from Jan 2030: the exalted 6th lord's period, likely to emphasise specialist skill, competitive success, analytical or service work, and steady advancement through expertise. Its Venus sub-period (Jun 2033 – Apr 2036) adds client work, finance and recognition.
