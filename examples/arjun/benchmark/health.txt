6Health and vitality

Please read first

This section describes only what traditional astrology associates with this chart. It is not medical advice, is not a diagnosis, and should never replace a doctor's opinion or routine screening. Astrological "indicators" describe areas that tradition says deserve attention, not illnesses that will happen.

Overall constitution

Vitality in jyotish is judged mainly from the lagna, the lagna lord, the Sun and the Moon. Here the Sun, the natural significator of vitality, is in its own sign; the Moon is full; and the lagna lord Mars is in a trine in a friendly sign, aspected by Jupiter. The 6th house of illness and its cure holds its own lord Mercury, exalted, which classically gives good resistance and recovery. Overall: a sound, energetic constitution with good recuperative power. The main traditional caution is the Aries tendency to overdo things, followed by sudden fatigue, and the Mars–Ketu pair's association with haste and minor injuries.

Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 12) mentions Mercury in the 6th in connection with temper in disputes, and Parashara (BPHS ch. 24, v. 47) mentions "fear of hidden ailments" for the 4th lord in the 11th; both are best read as reminders that stress, worry and conflict show up in the body, and that routine check-ups catch what is quiet.

Timing notes

In traditional practice, health deserves extra attention during Saturn's transit over the 12th from the lagna and 2nd from the Moon (now, until June 2027, with a return from October 2027 to February 2028), Saturn's transit over the lagna (2027–2030, Saturn debilitated in Aries), and Saturn's later transit 4th from the Moon (Kantaka Dhaiya, April 2030 – May 2032). Sub-periods of Ketu and Mars (both in the 9th) are traditionally periods for care with physical risks. None of these implies illness; they are windows when the tradition advises regular check-ups, steady routines and not overloading the body.

Some classical texts make statements about longevity for individual placements. Those statements are not meant to be read in isolation, and longevity judgement is deliberately not part of this report.
