The 2nd house: Saturn, lord of career and gains

The 2nd house is Taurus, Venus's earthy sign, a natural sign of wealth and stored value. Saturn sits here as lord of both the 10th and 11th houses. Parashara (BPHS ch. 24, vv. 110 and 122) gives both lords in the 2nd wealth and accomplishment, and modern lagna-wise writers treat the 11th lord in the 2nd as a classic Dhana (wealth) combination of the slow-building kind. Phaladeepika's line on Saturn in the 2nd (ch. 8, sl. 21) is pessimistic, and the honest reconciliation is that money comes, but through effort, time and restraint rather than ease: lean years early, compounding later. Saturn is in the "youth" avastha and gains strength with age, which fits that pattern.

The 2nd lord Venus is in the 4th house, a kendra. Parashara (v. 16) gives the 2nd lord in the 4th "all kinds of prosperity", and Venus in the 4th is classically the placement for a comfortable home, vehicles and good possessions (Phaladeepika ch. 8, sl. 17). Wealth is likely to be visible in home, property and comforts.

Dhana yoga: the lagna lord and the 9th lord

The computation records a Dhana yoga from the mutual aspect of Mars (lagna lord) and Jupiter (9th lord). The lagna lord linked with the lord of fortune is one of the most reliable signs that a person's own efforts are rewarded by luck. Mars in Jupiter's sign and Jupiter aspecting Mars also make the 9th house strong. In practice this favours money made through his own initiative, education and principles, and through teachers, mentors or the father's support.

Gains vs. expenses

Gains (11th): this is the most heavily influenced house in the chart. It holds the full Moon (4th lord) and is aspected by the Sun (5th lord), Jupiter (9th lord), Saturn (its own lord) and Rahu. Three of these are lords of trines or kendras. Gains are likely to come through several channels at once: employment and organisations (Saturn), personal talent (Sun), mentors and fortune (Jupiter), and technology, foreign contacts or unconventional networks (Rahu). The 4th lord here adds gains through property, home-related business or the mother's side. One caution: Aquarius is the badhaka (obstruction) house for Aries under the Prasna Marga rule, so some gains come with delays, conditions or obligations attached.

Expenses (12th): the 12th is empty apart from Gulika; its lord Jupiter sits in the 3rd. Parashara's line for the 12th lord in the 3rd (v. 135) mentions reduced happiness from siblings rather than heavy loss. Spending is likely to go into learning, skills, travel, communication and devices, and into ventures he starts himself. Rahu next to Jupiter can make some of this spending impulsive.

Saturn's transit: a note for now

Transiting Saturn is in Pisces, the 12th from the lagna and the 2nd from the Moon, the setting phase of his Sade Sati. Phaladeepika's transit chapter (ch. 26, sl. 22) links Saturn's transit of the 2nd from the Moon with strain on wealth; together with the 12th-house position from the lagna this describes a phase of high expenses and careful budgeting until June 2027 (with a brief return from October 2027 to February 2028). Transiting Rahu in the 11th, by contrast, supports gains through networks and technology, but also invites speculation that should be resisted.

Overall

The wealth picture is above average and steadily improving, but not effortless. Income is likely to track career closely, rise noticeably once skill and position are established, and arrive through several channels at once. The strongest supports are the heavily aspected 11th house and the lagna lord–9th lord link; the main thing to manage is impatience, the Aries–Rahu urge for fast results against Saturn's insistence on slow, compounding growth. Regular saving, property as a long-term asset, and avoiding borrowing for speculation suit this chart best.
