7Relationships, marriage and partner

The 7th house: Libra, empty, under Jupiter's gaze

The 7th house is Libra, an airy, movable sign ruled by Venus, the sign of balance, partnership and fairness. It contains no planets and receives two aspects, both from the 3rd house: Jupiter's (its fifth-house aspect) and Rahu's. Jupiter's aspect on the 7th is one of the classic protective factors in marriage: it brings goodwill, ethics, maturity and a partner of some wisdom or good family. Rahu's aspect adds an unconventional element: a partner from a different background, culture, language or region, or a relationship that begins in an unusual way.

Libra describes a partner who is graceful, sociable, diplomatic and aesthetically minded — a natural counterweight to Aries's speed and directness. The Aries–Libra axis is the zodiac's axis of "me and you", and relationships are where he learns to slow down and weigh another person's view.

The 7th lord Venus in the 4th

Venus, ruler of the 7th (and 2nd), sits in Cancer in the 4th house of home, mother and inner contentment, in the nakshatra Pushya, traditionally one of the most nourishing nakshatras. This ties marriage closely to home and family life: a partner who values domestic comfort, who may get on well with (or be chosen with the involvement of) his mother and family, and a relationship whose success is measured by the quality of home life. Parashara's line for the 7th lord in the 4th (BPHS ch. 24, v. 76) says the wife will not always be under his control, which reads in modern terms as a partner with her own mind and her own independence. Venus in a kendra is favourable; Venus in the Moon's sign is somewhat uncomfortable by sign (an "enemy" sign), so emotional sensitivity and moods, his and hers, need patience.

Venus, significator of marriage

For a man, Venus is the main significator (karaka) of the wife and of romantic life. Here it is in the adult (yuva) avastha, in a kendra, and aspects the 10th: affection, charm and a sense of beauty are real assets, and the partner may play a role in his public or professional life. Saturn's aspect on Venus from the 2nd (its third-house aspect) adds seriousness and some delay: commitment tends to come after deliberation and once he feels materially secure. Mars also aspects Venus's house (the 4th) from the 9th; differences of opinion about home and family arrangements are the typical friction point.

The navamsa (D9) view

The navamsa is traditionally the chart of marriage. Its lagna is Cancer, and its 7th house is Capricorn, empty, whose lord Saturn sits in the D9 lagna. A 7th lord in the navamsa lagna is a strong link between self and partner: marriage is taken seriously and shapes him deeply. Saturn describes a partner who is responsible, mature, practical and dependable, possibly older or more serious than expected. Venus falls in Scorpio in the D9 5th house of romance: an intense, private and loyal romantic nature. The exalted Moon in the D9 11th, with Mars, supports emotional resilience and a shared circle of friends.

The Darakaraka (spouse significator by Jaimini's degree method) is Mars, in both the seven- and eight-karaka schemes. Mars sits with Ketu in the 9th: a partner who is energetic, principled, independent and perhaps educated, religious or from a different place; someone who has her own convictions. Mars is in Taurus in the navamsa, with the exalted Moon, which suggests that over time the relationship becomes warm and stable.

Timing

Relationship matters are likely to be especially prominent in:

The Saturn–Jupiter sub-period (27 June 2027 – 7 January 2030), since Jupiter aspects the 7th house natally. Within it, the "double transit" that K.N. Rao's school uses for timing marriage (both slow planets influencing the 7th house or its lord at once) occurs when Jupiter is in Libra (his 7th) while Saturn in Aries aspects it: 28 December 2028 – 30 March 2029, and again 6 October 2029 – 26 January 2030. Jupiter in Leo also aspects the lagna while Saturn occupies it (late June to October 2027, and March to July 2028), a turning point in personal direction. Phaladeepika's transit chapter (ch. 26, sl. 19) adds that Jupiter's transit 7th from the Moon, as now in Leo, favours happiness with one's partner.

The Mercury–Venus sub-period (2 June 2033 – 2 April 2036): the 7th lord's sub-period, traditionally favourable for marriage, family and comfort; BPHS's description of Venus's sub-period in Mercury's major period is good when Venus is in a kendra or 11th from the dasha lord, both of which hold here.

For a single person these are classic windows for commitment; for a married person they more often show as major shared decisions, a home, or a partner's important life event.

Friendships

The full Moon in the 11th, the house of friends, gives a natural capacity for friendship and loyalty to groups; Aquarius adds a liking for like-minded communities and causes. Four planets aspect the 11th, so friendships are numerous and varied: senior and dutiful (Saturn), proud and creative (Sun), wise and helpful (Jupiter), unusual or foreign (Rahu). Saturn's ownership of the 11th favours a smaller number of lasting friendships over time, and Rahu warns against trusting new networks too quickly.
