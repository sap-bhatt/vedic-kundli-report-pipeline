3Mind, emotions and inner life

The full Moon in Aquarius, in the 11th house

The Moon governs the mind, feelings and the way experience is digested. Here it is in Aquarius, Saturn's airy sign (a neutral sign for the Moon), in the 11th house of gains, friendships, groups and aspirations. It is a full Moon, born on the first lunar day after Purnima, about 188° from the Sun, which gives it maximum paksha bala (strength from lunar brightness). Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 7) gives the Moon in the 11th a high-minded nature, long life, wealth, children and supporters. The Moon also rules the 4th house for Aries, so the 4th lord (home, mother, inner peace) sits in the house of gains; Parashara (BPHS ch. 24, v. 47) describes the 4th lord in the 11th as generous, virtuous and charitable, with some fear of hidden ailments.

Aquarius gives the mind an independent, idealistic, slightly detached quality: interest in ideas, causes, systems and the wider community rather than only personal feeling. In the 11th, his emotional well-being is strongly linked to friends, networks and shared goals. He is likely to think about people in terms of groups and fairness, and to feel most himself when he is part of something larger.

Purva Bhadrapada, the nakshatra of Aja Ekapada

The Moon's nakshatra, Purva Bhadrapada (20°00' Aquarius – 3°20' Pisces), is ruled by Jupiter and presided over by Aja Ekapada, the "one-footed unborn one", a fierce form of Rudra. Its symbol is the front legs of a funeral cot, or a man with two faces. Sources describe it as principled, intense and transformative: outwardly calm, inwardly fiery, willing to sacrifice a great deal for an idea, and attracted to reform (onlinejyotish's lesson calls it "outwardly calm but inwardly intense and spiritual"). The 2nd pada falls in the Taurus navamsa, which grounds that intensity in practical, material concerns: comfort, money and stable assets. The double-faced symbol is a good description of his inner life: a sociable, cooperative surface and a private, idealistic core.

The Sun opposite: head and heart in dialogue

The single most important influence on this Moon is the full aspect of the Sun from its own sign in the 5th, which the Moon returns. Mutual aspect of the 4th and 5th lords is a Raja Yoga; psychologically, a full-Moon birth shows an awareness of two sides of himself that do not always agree: the personal, creative, self-expressive Sun and the group-minded, idealistic Moon. Many modern astrologers describe full-Moon natives as emotionally vivid, visible and responsive to others, sometimes torn between their own ambitions and what others need from them. The healthy expression is balance: he can lead (Sun) and belong (Moon) at once.

Other influences on the Moon

Jupiter aspects the Moon from the 3rd (its ninth-house aspect). This is protective: it brings optimism, ethics and a sense of meaning, and it is one of the reasons the Kemadruma condition (an "isolated" Moon) is cancelled (see section 10). Jupiter is the Moon's nakshatra lord as well, so faith and good counsel are natural supports for his mind.

Saturn, the Moon's dispositor, also aspects it from the 2nd (its tenth-house aspect). Saturn adds seriousness, a sense of duty, and a tendency to carry worry quietly, particularly about money and responsibilities.

Rahu, too, aspects the Moon from the 3rd. Rahu on the Moon typically gives a restless, curious, unconventional mind, quick to take up new ideas and sometimes prone to anxiety or overthinking. At the date of this report transiting Rahu is in Aquarius, over the natal Moon, which amplifies this restlessness for the time being.

Under the strict rule, no planet occupies the 2nd or 12th from the Moon, which is the definition of Kemadruma yoga. It is cancelled here several times over, but it can still show as moments of feeling on his own even when surrounded by people.

In the navamsa the Moon is exalted in Taurus, in the D9 11th house with Mars. This is a strong indicator that, beneath the airy surface, the emotional nature is resilient and stable, and that it grows steadier with age.

By Jaimini's degree method the Moon is the chart's Atmakaraka (the significator of the soul, the planet at the highest degree in its sign). The soul's lessons in this life therefore run through the mind, emotions, the mother and the public: learning to feel fully without being carried away.

The mental style: exalted Mercury in the 6th

Mercury, the planet of intellect and speech, is at 9°56' Virgo, exalted and in its own sign, in the 6th house, in Uttara Phalguni, ruled by the Sun. This gives a precise, analytical, detail-oriented mind with a gift for diagnosis, problem-solving and practical reasoning. Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 12) warns that Mercury in the 6th can make a person argumentative, sharp-tongued and idle; with an exalted Mercury the better reading is a mind that loves to debate and to find the flaw in an argument, and that needs real problems to work on. Mercury is debilitated in the navamsa, which modern practitioners often read as cleverness that has to learn humility and patience before it becomes wisdom.

In plain terms

A bright, warm, sociable mind with an independent and idealistic streak, a sharp analytical intelligence, and a private intensity underneath. He feels things vividly and shows them, but also worries more than he lets on, especially about duties and money. The main emotional work in life is to keep his own ambitions and the needs of the people around him in balance, to calm Rahu's restlessness with routine and good company, and to let Jupiter's faith rather than Saturn's worry have the last word.
