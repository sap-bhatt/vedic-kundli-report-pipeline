8Family, home, siblings, children, education

Family of origin and speech (2nd house)

The 2nd house (family, speech, food, values) is Taurus with Saturn inside, and receives no aspects. Saturn in the 2nd suggests a family environment in which duty, work and money matter a great deal: perhaps a modest or careful start, responsibilities taken on early, or a family that values discipline. As lord of the 10th and 11th, Saturn also links family to career and income; family business, or a family that strongly shapes his professional choices, is a common expression. Speech is measured, practical and sometimes blunt or sparing; lagna-wise sources describe this Saturn as someone who speaks little but meaningfully. His 2nd lord Venus is in the 4th, which ties family wealth to home and property and is a pleasant indication for family comfort over time.

Mother and home (4th house)

The 4th house (Cancer) holds Venus, and its lord the Moon is full and placed in the 11th. Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 17) gives Venus in the 4th good vehicles, a good house, ornaments, clothes and fragrances, and Parashara (BPHS ch. 24, v. 47) gives the 4th lord in the 11th a generous, virtuous, charitable person. The mother is likely to be affectionate, graceful and well connected, someone who has a social circle of her own and through whom some gains or contacts come. The 4th receives the aspects of Mars and Saturn: Mars brings activity, renovation, relocation or occasional disagreement over property; Saturn adds responsibility for the home and the mother as time goes on. Home, vehicles and property are well supported, especially in Moon and Venus sub-periods.

Father and fortune (9th house)

The 9th house (Sagittarius) holds the lagna lord Mars and Ketu; its lord Jupiter sits in the 3rd with Rahu. The Sun, natural significator of the father, is strong in its own sign. The classical lines on the 9th house are mixed: Phaladeepika is severe about both Mars (ch. 8, sl. 10) and Ketu (ch. 8, sl. 32) in the 9th where the father and fortune are concerned, while Parashara's line for the lagna lord in the 9th (v. 9) is very favourable. The computation also flags two modern Pitra dosha indicators (Mars with Ketu; the 9th lord Jupiter with Rahu; see section 10).

In practice such a configuration often coincides with a father who is energetic, principled, perhaps strict or independent-minded, with whom Arjun argues about beliefs as much as he learns from him; with a father whose own life involves change, travel or a break from tradition; and with a sense that Arjun has to find his own dharma rather than simply inherit it. The strong Sun and Jupiter's aspect on the 9th soften this considerably. These are tendencies to understand, not judgements on the father.

Siblings (3rd and 11th houses)

The 3rd house (younger siblings, courage, skills, communication) holds Jupiter and Rahu, is aspected by Mars and Ketu, and its lord Mercury is exalted in the 6th. The classical lines here pull against each other. Parashara gives the 9th lord in the 3rd happiness from siblings (v. 99), but the same Jupiter is 12th lord, and the 12th lord in the 3rd reduces that happiness (v. 135); the 3rd lord in the 6th (v. 30) is linked with friction with siblings but great wealth, and Phaladeepika's Rahu in the 3rd (ch. 8, sl. 25) also mentions hostility with brothers. The balanced reading: siblings or cousins are significant and probably capable, even successful, but the relationship can involve rivalry, distance or differences of outlook. Elder siblings and senior figures are seen from the 11th, which holds the full Moon: warmth, support and help from elders and seniors.

Children (5th house)

The 5th house is Leo, the Sun's own sign, occupied by the Sun, and aspected by the Moon and Ketu. Parashara's verse for the 5th lord in the 5th (v. 53) promises children, and warns of difficulty only if the house is aspected by malefics; here the full Moon's aspect is benefic and Ketu's is mildly malefic. Phaladeepika's line on the Sun in the 5th (ch. 8, sl. 3) is cautious about children, but a Sun in its own sign is generally held to protect its own house. Jupiter, the natural significator of children, is 5th from the Moon, which is a good indication.

The balanced reading is that children are indicated, with some delay or deliberate planning and perhaps a small family, and that he will be a proud, involved and somewhat authoritative parent. The D7 (saptamsa, the chart for children) has a Gemini lagna; its 5th house (Libra) is empty and its lord Venus sits in the D7 11th house, a house of fulfilment, which is favourable. Phaladeepika's transit chapter (ch. 26, sl. 19) links Jupiter's transit of the 5th from the Moon with children; for Arjun that is Jupiter in Gemini, which next happens in the later 2030s. Within the next six years, the nearest traditional window is Jupiter's transit of Sagittarius (February 2031 – March 2032, with a short return to Scorpio in mid-2031), from where it aspects the natal 5th house and the Sun.

Education and learning

The 5th lord in its own sign and an exalted Mercury are strong signatures for intelligence and examinations, especially in analytical, technical, commercial, medical or legal subjects. Jupiter in the 3rd favours self-directed learning, languages, writing and communication; Mars with Ketu in the 9th gives interest in philosophy, law, higher learning and the "why" behind things. His school and college years fell entirely within the Saturn mahadasha, and from January 2020 within his first Sade Sati, which typically coincides with heavy academic pressure, disrupted routines and growing up quickly. The 4th lord Moon in the 11th suggests that friends and peer groups strongly shaped his learning, and the Saturn–Jupiter sub-period (2027–2030), with Jupiter as 9th lord, is a natural window for higher or professional study.
