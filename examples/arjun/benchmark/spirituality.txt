9Spirituality, hidden talents and foreign links

The 9th house: the lagna lord with Ketu

The 9th is the house of dharma, faith, teachers, pilgrimage, higher learning and the father. In this chart it holds the lagna lord Mars and Ketu, both in Mula, Ketu's own nakshatra, presided over by Nirriti, the goddess of dissolution; Mula means "root", and is classically the nakshatra of getting to the bottom of things. It is aspected by Jupiter, its own lord, and by Rahu.

Ketu is the planet of detachment, intuition and moksha (liberation). Phaladeepika (ch. 8, sl. 32) is severe about Ketu in the 9th, describing someone who goes his own way against convention and has troubles regarding the father and fortune. Modern authors tend to read the same placement as a person who cannot follow religion by rote: he wants to test, question and experience it directly, and is often drawn to philosophy, older traditions or a teacher outside the mainstream. With the lagna lord beside Ketu, this search is personal: his spiritual life is something he does, rather than something he attends. Pilgrimage, long journeys and study of philosophy or law are natural expressions.

Ketu's role is unusually large in this chart. It rules the lagna nakshatra (Ashwini), aspects the lagna, sits with the lagna lord, and is in its own nakshatra. The Ketu mahadasha (2047–2054) and the Ketu sub-periods (for example Mercury–Ketu, June 2032 – June 2033) are therefore likely to be turning points of inner life, when detachment, simplification and spiritual interest come to the front.

Jupiter with Rahu in the 3rd

Jupiter, the guru planet and lord of the 9th and 12th, sits with Rahu in Gemini, in Ardra, Rahu's own nakshatra, presided over by Rudra, the storm god. This is the Guru-Chandal combination. Its spiritual meaning is a person whose relationship with teachers and belief systems is unconventional: he may change beliefs more than once, learn from unusual or foreign teachers, or be drawn to spirituality through ideas, books, the internet and discussion rather than ritual. Modern writers on the combination (for example astromedha's note on the Jupiter–Rahu conjunction) describe both its risk, of claiming wisdom before the inner work is done, and its gift, of wide intellectual range and spiritual seeking when Jupiter is otherwise supported. Here Jupiter aspects the Moon, the 7th and the 9th house, and is in mutual aspect with the lagna lord, which is good support.

The Moon's nakshatra: Purva Bhadrapada

The Moon's nakshatra is ruled by Jupiter and presided over by Aja Ekapada, a fierce, ascetic form of Rudra. It is the nakshatra of intense idealism and of a willingness to sacrifice comfort for a belief. Together with the 9th-house Ketu it gives a mind that wants spiritual life to be real and demanding rather than decorative.

The 12th and 8th houses

The 12th house (Pisces) is empty apart from Gulika, which marks a sensitive point in matters of sleep, solitude and expenditure; its lord Jupiter is in the 3rd. The 8th house (Scorpio) is empty, its lord Mars in the 9th, and it receives Saturn's aspect. Neither house is heavily occupied, so research, occult study and retreat are interests rather than the centre of life; the 8th lord in the 9th turns research energy towards philosophy, law and belief.

Hidden talents

Healing and helping: the Ashwini lagna (the divine physicians), the strong 6th house and the Moon in the profession rule all point to a real gift for care, rescue, diagnosis and service, whether in medicine or in any role where he fixes what is broken.

Communication and teaching: Jupiter and Rahu in the 3rd, with an exalted Mercury as their dispositor, give a talent for writing, explaining, presenting and teaching, and for using new media to do it.

Analytical precision: exalted Mercury in the 6th, a natural auditor, debugger or examiner.

Courage under pressure: Mars with Ketu, quick decisive action when others hesitate.

Foreign links

The traditional indicators of foreign residence or travel are the 12th house and its lord, the 9th house, Rahu and movable signs. Here the 12th lord Jupiter sits with Rahu (the classical significator of foreign things) in the 3rd house of travel and communication; Rahu aspects the 9th and 11th; Ketu occupies the 9th; and the lagna is a movable sign. These point to meaningful foreign or long-distance connections: travel for study or work, work with foreign organisations or overseas clients, or a period of living away from his birthplace. The current Rahu sub-period (to June 2027) and the following Jupiter sub-period (2027–2030), whose lord rules the 12th, are the most likely phases for such links to become active.
