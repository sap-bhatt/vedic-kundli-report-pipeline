How to read this report

Jyotish describes tendencies and timing, not certainties. The classical texts state their results bluntly; this report gives their sense, weighs each factor against the rest of the chart, and says where sources differ. The planetary positions come from one computation and are treated as correct; the reading is an interpretation and can be wrong. Nothing here is medical, financial or legal advice.

The shape of the chart

The planets pass rulership to one another in two ways. The Moon, Venus and Saturn form a loop, each ruling the sign the next one occupies, so none of the three ends the chain. Mercury, in its own sign, is where the chains of Mars and Jupiter finish. The Sun, also in its own sign, rules only itself, so it is not the chart's final ruler.

Birth-time sensitivity

The lagna falls at 10.9 degrees of Aries, so a change of sign would need the birth time to be off by about 43 minutes. The lagna sits about a quarter of the way through its navamsa division (26%) and about two-thirds through its dashamsa division (D10, 62%). The Moon moves only about half a degree an hour, so its sign and star are far less sensitive than the lagna and the divisional charts.

3Mind, emotions and inner life

The Moon's sign, house and nakshatra

Your Moon sits in Aquarius, in the eleventh house of friends, gains and networks. Aquarius is ruled by Saturn, and the Moon is neutral there, neither lifted nor weighed down. It is close to full, about 172 degrees from the Sun, so the Moon counts as a natural benefic in this chart.

The eleventh house is one of the most favourable places for the Moon. Traditional texts call it noble, polite and generous, with many friends and a fair prospect of gains. Modern writers stress supportive networks and steady growth, and add that the satisfaction from gains can be short-lived, so contentment matters as much as acquisition.

The Moon falls in the lunar mansion (nakshatra) of Purva Bhadrapada, ruled by Jupiter. The texts describe cleverness and a readiness to earn, together with an intense, devoted and persevering character. Jataka Parijata calls the native timid, while modern writers describe someone hard to win over but steady. Jupiter's benefic aspect on the Moon helps here. Jupiter itself sits in Gemini, its enemy sign, in the third house, so its support comes with some effort. The nakshatra is one factor among many, not a fixed fate.

Aspects and company of the Moon

Four planets aspect the Moon, and their natures shape its mood differently.

  • Jupiter's aspect is benefic by nature. It brings a protective, reflective quality, and as lord of the ninth and twelfth houses it links the mind to faith and quiet reflection.
  • Saturn's aspect is hard by nature, since Saturn is a natural malefic. As lord of the tenth and eleventh houses it adds a sober steadiness about work and money rather than fear.
  • Rahu's aspect is unsettling by nature. It feeds restlessness and strong wants, which fits the restless streak some texts note in the Moon's sign.
  • The Sun aspects the Moon, and the Moon aspects the Sun in return. The Sun is a natural malefic, so its aspect is firm rather than soft; as lord of the fifth house it links the mind to creativity and learning.

The Moon's sign lord, Saturn, sits in Taurus in the second house of money and family. Saturn is a friend in Taurus, so it is steady rather than commanding. Classical texts say the Moon's results come fully only when the Moon, its sign and that sign's lord are all strong, so here its gifts arrive with patience.

Moon yoga

The Moon also takes part in a Kemadruma pattern, which the texts link with a sensitive and sometimes sorrowful mind. Jupiter's aspect on the Moon softens it, and its fuller meaning is covered in the yogas part of this report.

Mercury and thinking style

Mercury sits in Virgo, in the sixth house, where it is exalted. This gives a precise, discerning and articulate mind with a gift for analysis, writing and the arts. It is not combust, being about 23 degrees from the Sun, and no aspect falls on it. Mercury is also the final dispositor of Mars and Jupiter, so its style runs through much of the chart.

The sixth house is a house of work and friction, so this strength shows through daily problem-solving rather than ease. Texts disagree on temper. Phaladeepika calls the native quick to anger; Saravali says not given to anger but often insulted. Modern writers describe a sharp, argumentative but effective problem-solver suited to analytical or service work.

In the navamsa (the divisional chart used for the finer detail of life) Mercury is debilitated in Pisces. Braha, a modern astrologer, notes that a planet exalted in the birth chart but debilitated in the navamsa gives very mixed results, so the mind's sharpness may come and go. Mercury's placement also forms what texts call a Viparita Raja yoga, which favours gain through hardship.

Restless patterns tend to show when life turns routine or predictable, and the texts link this Moon with fluctuating fortunes. Steady routines, time with trusted friends and a clear sense of what is enough help more than chasing each new gain.

The Amatyakaraka points to leadership, advisory or service roles, which is where this planet's influence on your rise is most visible.
