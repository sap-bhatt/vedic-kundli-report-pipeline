## Timeline

### How timing works

Vimshottari dasha is a fixed cycle of planetary periods that adds up to 120 years. The first period is set by the lord of the Moon's birth star. Your Moon is in Purva Bhadrapada, which Jupiter rules, so Jupiter's period came first, with about 9 years and 4 months still to run at birth. Each major period (mahadasha) is split into smaller sub-periods (antardasha), each ruled by one of the nine planets. A period brings forward what its planet rules, the house it sits in, and the planets it joins or is aspected by. Slow planets such as Saturn and Jupiter add a second layer by moving through the signs, and their effects are felt most when a period of the same planet is running.

### Past periods: validation checks

These are checks to hold against your own life, not claims about what happened.

The Jupiter period (Sep 2001 to Jan 2011, your first nine years) often coincides with early years of learning and family support, with some early worries. Jupiter rules the 9th, a house of fortune and faith, and also the 12th, a house of expenses and withdrawal. It sits in Gemini, a sign where it is less at ease. The classical texts link Jupiter with the mother and, for some, with children, so the early family picture is a natural place to check. The texts also say that when this planet is weakened, its harder results come in the opening years and the later part improves. Does that match your childhood?

### The current period: Saturn (Jan 2011 to Jan 2030)

Saturn is in Taurus, in the 2nd house, a sign where it is at home and friendly with Venus. It rules the 10th (career and public standing) and the 11th (gains and networks). Saturn is a natural malefic in this chart, so its aspects on the Moon and on Venus are hard in manner, but it carries the career and gains houses, which matter most here. The chart also marks Saturn as the minister planet (Amatyakaraka), which the classical texts link with one's profession and with rising through steady work.

The overall expectation is a long, slow period of responsibility. Results tend to come in steps through effort rather than in leaps. The classical texts describe Saturn periods as heavy in general and strongly dependent on the chart. Here the friendly sign and the career and gains houses lean toward steady reward. Saturn's testing side, since the classical rules count a lord of the 3rd, 6th or 11th as a testing ruler, asks for patience, and the reward comes slowly.

The period asks for extra care of health and vitality, especially joints, chronic and wind-type complaints, and steady rest. This is a general note, not medical advice. Decisions about money, property, health and family stay with you. A Kemadruma pattern in the chart is cancelled by Jupiter's aspect on the Moon, so it adds nothing heavy here.

### Sub-periods of the Saturn period

Past sub-periods, as validation checks:

Saturn-Saturn (Jan 2011 to Jan 2014) is Saturn's own sub-period, which opens the period. The texts treat it as a heavy start, with a favourable side that rests on the friendly sign and the career and gains houses. It often coincides with a slow, demanding first stretch. Does that fit those years?

Saturn-Mercury (Jan 2014 to Sep 2016). Mercury is exalted but sits in the 6th. The texts describe early gains in reputation, work and learning, followed by strain on health and setbacks in projects. Does that match your experience of those years?

Saturn-Ketu (Sep 2016 to Oct 2017). The most unsettled of the early sub-periods. Ketu sits in the 8th from Saturn, a testing position, and the texts link this stretch with family quarrels, worry and setbacks at work. Their advice is simplicity and careful health habits.

Saturn-Venus (Oct 2017 to Dec 2020). A mixed stretch. The favourable side is marriage, family, comforts and goodwill from relatives, which the texts give to Venus. The heavier side is linked to Venus sitting in an enemy sign, so physical strain is the caution here. Venus and Saturn give each other's results in their shared periods, so both planets' themes show in this stretch.

Saturn-Sun (Dec 2020 to Dec 2021). The Sun is strong here, in its moolatrikona zone in the 5th, so the favourable side leads: better standing with officials and employers, and steadier family life. Saturn and the Sun are natural enemies, which the texts usually read as hard, and a strong Sun softens that. Does that match your memory?

Saturn-Moon (Dec 2021 to Jul 2023). Mixed. The Moon, the chart's self-planet (Atmakaraka), sits in the 11th and rules the 4th. The texts give it home comforts, conveyances and friends, and also link it with family strain and emotional heaviness. It often coincides with a sensitive stretch, warm at home and worried at the same time.

Saturn-Mars (Jul 2023 to Aug 2024). Mars sits in the 9th, a house of fortune, which supports property and energetic work. Mars also rules the 8th, and the texts read this sub-period as high-friction: quarrels, strain with siblings and partners, and a need for health caution around heat and fire. Mars is the classical marker of the spouse, so partner matters are a natural check.

The sub-period running now:

Saturn-Rahu (Aug 2024 to Jun 2027). Rahu sits in the 3rd, in Gemini, joined with Jupiter. Rahu rules no house of its own, so it takes on the lordships of the planet it joins (Jupiter's 9th and 12th) and the nature of its sign lord, Mercury. The 3rd is one of Rahu's better places: courage, initiative and a strong will, at some cost to harmony with siblings. The stretch is mixed. The favourable side is initiative, skill-building and money through effort. The cautious side is quarrels, mental strain, friction with officials and the need to watch spending. The closing phase of Sade Sati is running alongside, and so is the Rahu yogini (Sankata), so Rahu's themes are strong until mid-2027. Hold off on large commitments and keep money decisions simple.

Saturn-Jupiter (Jun 2027 to Jan 2030). This is the most supportive stretch of the next few years. Jupiter rules the 9th (fortune, teachers, faith) and the 12th, is a lord of a trine (the fortune houses 1, 5 and 9), and sits in the 2nd from Saturn, a good house for it. It is in an enemy sign, which limits its full measure, and it shares its place with Rahu, which the texts read as friction with officials and setbacks in projects. Jupiter and Mars form a Raja link (the classical pattern for rising responsibility and recognition) and a Dhana link (capacity to earn). The texts promise capacity, not wealth. Expect steady recognition and a sense of rising responsibility, with the cautions above. Its double-transit stretches, described below, are the densest of the decade.

### The Mercury period ahead (Jan 2030 to Jan 2047)

Mercury sits in the 6th, in Virgo, where it is exalted and in its own sign at once. It rules the 3rd and the 6th. The classical texts describe the start of a Mercury period as good, the middle as bringing recognition from authority, and the last part as more distressing. Here the strong dignity and the Virgo sign lean favourable; the modern astrologer Raman calls Virgo Mercury the most auspicious period of life for that sign. The cautions are the 6th house (competition, effort, health), the lordship of the difficult houses, and a weak Mercury in the navamsa (the finer divisional chart). So the full promise of this strong placement should not be predicted. Its Viparita Raja (Harsha) pattern points to resilience: success after a struggle, not easy success. This period runs from age 28 to 45.

Mercury-Mercury (Jan 2030 to Jun 2032) is Mercury's own sub-period, which opens the period. It is mixed. The exalted dignity is associated with learning, gains through relatives, a good house and clothes. The 6th house and the lordships of the 3rd and 6th are associated with competition, nerves and some setbacks. The texts put the early months on the favourable side and treat the own sub-period as a partial taste of the period's promise. The Dhaiya described below overlaps most of it.

Mercury-Ketu (Jun 2032 to Jun 2033). Ketu is the lord of your rising sign's birth star, so this sub-period touches the base of the chart. The texts mostly describe Ketu's sub-period as unsettled. But Ketu sits in the 4th from Mercury, a kendra (the angle houses 1, 4, 7 and 10), and Jupiter aspects it, so the favourable side leads: comfort, help from siblings and steady learning. Keep patience in business and travel, and calm dealings with relatives.

Mercury-Venus (Jun 2033 to Apr 2036). Among the best parts of the decade. Venus sits in the 4th, a home house, and rules the 2nd and 7th. It is in the 11th from Mercury, a gains house. The texts describe worship, generous acts, clothes and ornaments, married happiness, and help from friends and rulers. Keep up health habits, since the classical texts link Venus with physical strain in this position.

Mercury-Sun (Apr 2036 to Feb 2037). The key stretch of the next decade. The Sun rules the 5th, a trine house, sits in its moolatrikona zone in the 5th, and forms a Raja link with the Moon. Its favourable side is the strongest among the sub-periods in view: favour from officials, support from friends and better standing. Against it, the Sun sits in the 12th from Mercury, a testing position, and the texts associate it with caution around property, fire and authority, and with general health such as headaches and digestion. The stretch is mixed, and its strongest side is standing and support. It ranks first among the sub-periods ahead because its lord is a trine ruler in a strong sign. Jupiter's sub-period is also a trine ruler, but it sits in an enemy sign.

### Transits, Sade Sati and yogini timers

Sade Sati is a seven-and-a-half-year cycle, when Saturn passes the sign before your Moon, the Moon's own sign and the sign after it. Saturn is now in Pisces, 2nd from your Moon, in the closing phase of the cycle that began in 2020. The texts associate the 2nd from the Moon with money worries, earnings that do not stay, concern for children and family, and loss of comfort. Modern writers read this phase as rebuilding. Sade Sati and Dhaiya are popular traditions built on older house-by-house transit rules, and modern authors disagree about how severe they are.

Jupiter is in Leo, 7th from your Moon, a favourable place for marriage, comfort, wealth and purposeful travel, until late January 2027. It then moves into Cancer, where it is exalted, and the texts say it does no harm there, even though Cancer is the 6th from the Moon. Later it passes through Virgo, the 8th from your Moon, in late 2027 to early 2028, from summer to December 2028, and from spring to summer 2029. The texts link that sign with tiring journeys, expenses and grief. Its more favourable passages are Libra (9th from the Moon) from December 2028 to March 2029, and Sagittarius (11th from the Moon) in 2031.

Saturn's moves are as follows. It enters Aries in June 2027, slips back into Pisces from October 2027 to February 2028, then settles in Aries until August 2029. It enters Taurus in August 2029, returns to Aries from October 2029 to April 2030, and settles in Taurus from April 2030 to May 2032. From late May 2032 it moves into Gemini.

Each position is read from the Moon. Aries is the 3rd from the Moon, where the texts give Saturn's good results. Aries is a sign where Saturn is debilitated, so that gain is weakened, and the stretch supports steady effort and initiative. Taurus is the 4th from the Moon, the position popularly called the small Dhaiya. The texts associate it with unrest at home, strain with close relatives and money worry, and it is also Saturn's return to its own natal place. Gemini, the 5th from the Moon, is linked with concern for children, a restless mind and quarrels.

Taken with the dasha, the Aries stretch from February 2028 to August 2029 runs mostly under the Jupiter sub-period, which is the supportive one, so the two timers point the same way. The Taurus Dhaiya from August 2029 to May 2032 overlaps most of Mercury's opening sub-period, so Mercury's favourable start is paired with home and family caution.

Past phases, as checks: the Dhaiya in the 4th (Jun 2000 to Apr 2003) began in Jupiter's period, in its Mercury sub-period. The Dhaiya in the 8th (Sep 2009 to Aug 2012) began in Jupiter-Rahu and ran into Saturn's own first sub-period. The rising phase of Sade Sati (Jan 2020 to Apr 2022) began in Saturn-Venus and passed through Saturn-Sun and Saturn-Moon. The peak (Apr 2022 to Mar 2025), when Saturn sat in Aquarius, your Moon's own sign, ran through the Moon and Mars sub-periods and into Rahu. The texts treat the peak as the most demanding phase, though Saturn was in its own sign during the rising and peak phases, which the texts say softens its harder side. Do these match your experience?

Yogini periods are shorter tone cycles that run alongside the dasha. Sankata (Rahu, to Jan 2030) is the crisis tone in the texts: losses, setbacks and weakness, fear of enemies, separation and official trouble, and confusion. Its planet is Rahu, the same planet as the current sub-period, so the theme repeats. Mangala (Moon, Jan 2030 to Jan 2031) is gentle: comfort, devotion, fame, auspicious events at home and growth in learning. Pingala (Sun, Jan 2031 to Jan 2033) has a demanding tone in general, but the Sun's strong dignity here softens it, with favour from authority and general health habits to keep up. Sign-based chara periods, given as dates only, run Leo (2020 to 2032), Virgo (2032 to 2045) and Libra (2045 to 2054).

Double-transit stretches are times when Jupiter and Saturn both act on the same house, counted from your rising sign, Aries. These are the sharpest timing points ahead. During the Rahu sub-period, the 9th (fortune and teachers) is active from November 2026 to January 2027, and the 12th from January to June 2027.

From June 2027 to January 2030, during the Jupiter sub-period, the stretches are the densest of the decade. The 10th (career) is active in July to December 2028 and again in March to August 2029. The 7th (partnership) is active in December 2028 to March 2029 and again from October 2029. The 2nd (money and family) is active in November 2027 to February 2028 and in August 2029. The 1st (self and health) is active in March to July 2028.

In Mercury's opening sub-period, the 4th (home and property) is active in April to May 2030, September 2030 to February 2031, June to October 2031 and March to May 2032. The 2nd (money) is active in the same months. In Mercury-Ketu, the 5th (children and creative work) and the 9th (fortune) are under review in August to October 2032.

Saturn over natal planets: Saturn returns to its own natal place in Taurus in August to October 2029 and again from April 2030 to May 2032. For those dates, its 2nd house, and the 10th and 11th it rules, come under review. From late May 2032 to May 2033 Saturn passes over natal Jupiter and Rahu in Gemini, so Jupiter's houses (the 9th and 12th) and the 3rd come under review.

Remedies (optional): for the Rahu sub-period, a charity of a goat and a chant to Shiva are traditional practices. For the Sun sub-period at the end of the decade, worship of the Sun is a traditional practice. None of these is needed.

### The next ten years at a glance

The key stretch is the Sun sub-period inside Mercury's period (Apr 2036 to Feb 2037), a mixed stretch with the strongest support for standing and friends. The second bright stretch is the Jupiter sub-period from mid-2027 to early 2030, which has the densest double-transit timing. The testing stretches are the Rahu sub-period now, the Taurus Dhaiya from August 2029 to May 2032, and the short Ketu sub-period that follows Mercury's opening.
