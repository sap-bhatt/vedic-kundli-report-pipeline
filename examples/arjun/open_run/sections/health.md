## Health and vitality

This is traditional astrology, not medical advice. It points to the rhythms of rest, routine and care that classical texts link with vitality, and it is no substitute for a doctor.

### Overall constitution

Aries rising gives a forceful, active build. Its ruler, Mars, sits in Sagittarius in the 9th house, a friendly sign and a house of fortune and faith. The texts read this as stamina and a fighting spirit that keeps going under pressure. Mars also rules the 8th house of recovery, so the same drive can run hot, and regular rest is what keeps it sustainable. Ketu, a natural malefic in this chart, casts its aspect on the rising sign, which asks for pacing and steadiness rather than bursts of effort.

Mercury, lord of the 6th house of daily health, sits in its own 6th house in Virgo and is exalted there. Classical texts call this Harsha yoga, linked with a sound constitution and the ability to overcome obstacles. The modern astrologer Raman cautions that the 6th lord's difficult side is softened rather than removed, so good daily habits do the real work.

The Moon, a natural benefic in this chart, sits in the 11th house and supports steady vitality and good spirits. The Sun sits in a strong position in Leo, in the 5th house, and gives warmth and vigour. Because the Sun is a natural malefic here, that vigour comes with intensity, so it is worth keeping the body from overheating through overwork.

### Sensitivities from the 6th, 8th and 12th houses

- **6th house (daily health):** Mercury's strong constitution comes with a tendency, in the classical view, to carry tension and nervous strain. Unhurried routines and regular breaks ease this.
- **8th house (recovery):** Mars, its lord, sits in the 9th, and no planets occupy the 8th. Saturn's aspect on it is read by the astrologer Braha as supportive of resilience over the long run.
- **12th house (rest):** Its lord Jupiter sits in the 3rd house, and no planets occupy the 12th. The classical BPHS links this with a need for self-care and quiet time, and sleep is the natural place to start.

### Periods to take more care

Two stretches deserve attention. From late November 2027 to late February 2028, Jupiter and Saturn both sit over the 6th house, a classical timing marker for daily health and routine. The texts treat this as a time to keep rest and habits steady and to take things at a gentle pace. From July 2038 to July 2039, the Mercury-Mars sub-period is led by the ruler of the 6th house and supported by Mars, lord of the rising sign. It tends to favour steady effort, and rest still matters.
