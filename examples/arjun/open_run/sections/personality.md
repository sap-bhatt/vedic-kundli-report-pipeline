## Personality and temperament

You were born with Aries rising, so your first impression tends to be direct, quick and independent. Aries is traditionally read as brave and self-willed, ready to act before others have finished deliberating. The same energy can show up as impatience or a short fuse. The rising sign is a tendency, not a fixed pattern, and the planets around it shape how it comes across. Your rising degree sits well inside the sign (about 11 degrees in), so Aries holds unless the recorded birth time is off by more than about 40 minutes.

### The rising sign and lagna nakshatra

Your outer manner is shaped by two things. The first is the rising sign itself: fierce pride, competitiveness and a strategic mind, balanced by frankness and a wish to win fairly. Some texts describe the Aries native as lively but prone to extremes, so a clear sense of priorities helps.

The second is your lagna nakshatra, Ashwini, which is ruled by Ketu. Ashwini is described as a swift, healing and capable star: a person who is attractive, fond of good things, quick to learn and full of vitality. Ketu, its lord, sits in your 9th house, which adds a leaning toward belief, philosophy and a certain detachment.

### The lagna lord, Mars

Mars rules your rising sign and sits in the 9th house, in Sagittarius, a sign ruled by its friend Jupiter. A lagna lord in a trinal house is a strong start: it points to fortune, principles and a good name built on conviction. Traditional texts describe this placement as lucky, well-liked and eloquent, with a sincere interest in dharma and in teachers.

Mars also rules your 8th house, so its energy comes with some effort. You may need to work for results, but the work tends to pay off. Mars is well clear of the Sun, so it is not weakened by proximity. Ketu sits with it and Rahu's glance falls on it, which can add a restless, detached edge and sudden shifts of focus. Jupiter, a natural benefic, casts its aspect on Mars, and this softens the edge.

Mars in Sagittarius also asks for a managed temper. Traditional writers describe a fearless, frank and driven person who can lose ground when anger gets the better of them. Mars in the 9th brings logic, acuteness and a stubborn streak; the risk is dogmatism, so openness to other views is worth practising. The combination of the lagna lord in the 9th forms Raja Yoga, a union of kendra and trikona lords that traditionally brings rise in standing, and Dhana Yoga, a wealth combination.

### Planets in the rising sign or aspecting it

No planet occupies your rising sign. Ketu also casts a glance at it from the 9th, but traditional authorities disagree on whether the shadow planets cast aspects at all, so it adds little beyond its placement. Jupiter and Rahu also aspect Mars, as described above.

### The Sun

The Sun sits in Leo in your 5th house, in the first 20 degrees of Leo, its moolatrikona zone, which is a dignified position. The Sun is a natural malefic in your chart, so its manner can be direct and sometimes hard. As ruler of the 5th house, however, it carries creativity, teaching ability and a love of standing up in front of others.

Traditional writers describe a Sun-in-Leo native as enthusiastic, valorous and generous, but quick to anger and restless. Modern writers add stubbornness, independence and a strong sense of self-respect. The Moon's aspect on the Sun is a helpful note, pointing toward learning and recognition. Traditional texts are cautious about the Sun in the 5th house on matters of children and money; modern writers read that as a question of timing and patience rather than a fixed outcome. For your personality, the lead theme is intelligence and a creative or teaching gift, with a pride that does well when it is channelled rather than spent on quarrels.

### The Moon sign, briefly

The Moon is in Aquarius, in the 11th house, in the nakshatra Purva Bhadrapada. It is your Atmakaraka, the planet with the highest degree in its sign, which stands for the soul and the core drive of a life. It is a natural benefic in your chart because it is waning and well away from the Sun, and it is exalted in your navamsa chart, which is a sign of inner steadiness. Traditional writers link the Moon in Aquarius with a noble, polite and cultured manner, and modern writers add artistic taste, a gentle temper and gratitude, with a tendency at times to hold back what you really think. More on the Moon sign appears in the Mind section.

Purva Bhadrapada adds depth. Classical writers read it as clever, careful with money and sometimes troubled. Modern writers describe intense, devoted and persevering feelings, with a long memory for hurts. Jupiter's benefic aspect on the Moon supports the good side of this nakshatra. Saturn and Rahu also aspect the Moon, so feelings can run deep and sometimes heavy.

### The birth day and tithi

You were born on a Monday, the day of the Moon. Some texts suggest that this gives a calm, gentle and kind manner of speech, a settled presence beneath an active life. Your birth tithi is Krishna Pratipada, the first day of the waning half of the lunar month. Some texts link it with a large family circle, wisdom, discernment and a liking for gold and fine things. Treat these as light colouring rather than fate.

### Synthesis

The picture is a fierce, direct outer style resting on a steady, principled core. Mars and the Sun are the two drivers. Mars gives courage, conviction and fortune through action; the Sun gives self-respect, creativity and a need to be seen. The Moon, as your Atmakaraka, offers a softer inner centre: gentle, adaptable and caring, with depth of feeling that can turn inward. Ketu's presence in the 9th house adds a seeker's streak.

The main area for growth is temper. The rising sign, Mars and the Sun all run hot, and their quick anger comes from the same fire. Channelling that energy into one clear goal tends to serve you better than fighting every battle. Your strongest assets are courage, principle and the ability to learn quickly, and these grow when you pair them with patience.
