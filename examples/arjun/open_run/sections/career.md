## Career and vocation

Your career house is Capricorn, the tenth from your rising sign of Aries, and it is ruled by Saturn. Saturn sits in Taurus in the second house, where it is a friend of its sign. As a natural malefic in this chart, Saturn works in a steady, demanding manner rather than quickly, but functionally it rules two houses that matter here: the tenth (career) and the eleventh (gains). Classical texts link a tenth lord in the second house to work that feeds wealth and family standing, and to recognition from people in authority. Modern astrologers name family trades, food and catering as the typical fields. Your lagna lord, Mars, sits in the ninth, a supportive trine, so the drive to work is sound.

### The tenth house and its lord

No planet sits in Capricorn. Only Venus aspects it, from the fourth house. Venus is a natural benefic, and the classical text Saravali says a benefic aspect on the tenth makes working life pleasant. That rule is stated from the Moon rather than from the lagna, so treat it as a tentative, low-confidence support. It most likely shows up as a cooperative, agreeable working environment.

### Career significators

**The Sun.** The Sun sits in Leo, its moolatrikona sign, in the fifth house of learning and creative skill. Strong by dignity, and lord of the fifth (a trine, a supportive house), it gives leadership and organising ability. Saravali links the Sun with notable deeds and a leading position. Raman reads organising capacity and a talent for public persuasion. Jataka Parijata sees a Sun in the fifth as service near a ruler, and modern astrologers add teaching authority. Because the Sun is a natural malefic here, its ambition needs discipline to turn into results.

**The Moon.** The Moon is your Atmakaraka, the planet of the soul in the chart. It sits in Aquarius in the eleventh house of gains, and it is exalted in the navamsa. Raman reads a Moon in the eleventh as a sign of good business profits. Saravali calls the Aquarius Moon an artisan, and Phaladeepika notes endurance on long journeys. The Moon is a natural benefic here.

### The Amatyakaraka

Saturn is also the Amatyakaraka, the "minister" significator. Among the chart's chara karakas (the planets ranked by degree within their signs) it has the second-highest degree. This planet shows counsel, professional standing and the means by which you rise. Saturn is both the tenth lord and the minister, so career and advisory roles run through it. It sits in the fourth from the Moon, a kendra from the Atmakaraka, which modern astrologers read as an associate liked by people in authority. No weakening factor shows. Saturn is in a friend's sign, not debilitated, and no planet aspects it. Its role is therefore sound, though as a natural malefic it grows through steady effort. Classical texts differ on whether the minister is counted among seven or eight significators; the seven-count reading used here gives Saturn this role.

The Amatyakaraka points to leadership, advisory or service roles, which is where this planet's influence on your rise is most visible.

### Suitable fields

The navamsa (D9), a zoomed-in chart, is the classical check on livelihood. The method takes the lord of the tenth, finds the navamsa sign it occupies, and reads that sign's lord as the indicator. Three starting points are used (lagna, Moon, Sun). The lagna reading comes first here.

- **From the lagna:** Saturn sits in Cancer in the navamsa, and Cancer's lord is the Moon. The Moon points to nurturing, public-facing or hospitality work, and to fields tied to food, water, travel, textiles and caring roles. Its exaltation in the navamsa suggests gains that come with less effort. Phaladeepika adds cattle, pilgrimage and the cloth trade.
- **From the Moon:** Mars, the tenth from the Moon, sits in navamsa Taurus. Venus indicates creative and aesthetic work: design, music, beauty, jewellery, hospitality and partnership-based work.
- **From the Sun:** Venus, the tenth from the Sun, sits in navamsa Scorpio. Mars indicates hands-on, technical and protective work: engineering, machinery, land, cooking, defence or security, and medicine or surgery.

Neither Venus nor Mars is strong in the navamsa, so their readings add colour rather than lead. Read together, the picture is nurturing and public-facing work, creative or relationship-based work, and practical or technical work. These are families of work, not job titles. This is one classical view among several, and effort and choice shape the career.

### The D10 chart

The dashamsa (D10) divides each sign into ten parts and is the classical chart for work and recognition. It refines the career picture in the birth chart rather than replacing it. Here the D10 lagna is Cancer and the D10 tenth house is Aries, ruled by Mars. Mars sits in Capricorn in the D10 seventh house, where it is exalted. Mars is strong in D10 and is also the D10 tenth lord, so its periods are the ones the classical view links to professional growth. Growth is a likelihood, not a guaranteed promotion. The D10 tenth house itself is empty.

The D10 seventh house (own business) holds two planets, the Sun and Mars. The D10 sixth house (service) is empty. The texts treat whichever of these is more occupied as the path, so the lean is toward own business or independent work. This is a low-confidence reading to test against your own experience.

**Birth-time caution:** the D10 reading depends closely on the exact birth time. Your lagna sits 10.9 degrees into Aries, so an error of about 43 minutes would change the rising sign itself. The D10 lagna sits 62 per cent of the way through its division, so a few minutes' error is less likely to change it than near an edge, but the D10 detail should still be treated as supporting rather than decisive.

### Working style

The pattern points to steady, long-term, accumulative work rather than quick leaps, with the tenth lord tied to family finances. Mercury, exalted in the sixth house of daily work, supports a good job with capable colleagues and an interest in healing (Braha). Mercury in Virgo is skilled in writing, poetry, and fine or mechanical arts, and is disposed to serve others (Saravali). Together with the D10 leaning toward independence, the likely style is a practical, service-minded skill, used on your own terms.

### When this area is most active

Three stretches stand out for career matters:

- **July to December 2028:** Jupiter and Saturn both aspect the tenth house from the lagna. Classical astrologers treat this as a timing marker for career matters, a spotlight on work and standing rather than an event. It is the longest such spell in the listed years.
- **January 2030 to June 2032:** Mercury's own sub-period. It is an active stretch for work, but a demanding rather than best time. Mercury rules the third and sixth houses and sits in the sixth, so the emphasis falls on daily work, detail and skill-building rather than big moves.
- **April 2036 to February 2037:** the Sun's sub-period within Mercury's. This is supportive, because the Sun is a career significator here and rules the fifth, a trine house.
