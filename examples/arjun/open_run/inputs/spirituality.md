Rao, Raman, PVR and Murthy take the Atmakaraka's navamsa sign; Murthy also uses the same sign in the rasi chart ('karakamsa rasi') and notes that some read 'swamsa' as the navamsa lagna; Abhyankar's translation speaks only of the 'division occupied by' the significator, counted from Aries; Kalidasa (Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4) reads the Atmakaraka's rasi position, which P.S. Sastri rejects.
  - Phrasing: The sign results are old, often literal (animals, water, falls); present them as areas to be careful with or symbolic themes, never as predicted accidents or diseases. Lead with supportive themes where sources give them, and do not state illegitimacy, adultery or criminality claims to a user.
