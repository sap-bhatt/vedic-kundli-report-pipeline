## Chart at a glance

Aries rises, with the Moon in Aquarius in the Purva Bhadrapada star and the Sun in Leo in the 5th house. The Moon is the chart's central planet: it is the Atmakaraka, the planet with the highest degree in its sign, which stands for the self, and it is exalted in the navamsa (the D9 chart, which texts use to read inner values and partnership).

### The shape of the chart

Three of the four corner houses are empty: the 1st, 7th and 10th. Venus holds the 4th. Mercury is the only planet in a difficult house (the 6th, 8th or 12th). Strength shows most clearly in the navamsa, where the Moon is exalted, and no planet is vargottama, meaning in the same sign in the birth chart and the navamsa.

The planets pass rulership to one another in two ways. The Moon, Venus and Saturn form a loop, each ruling the sign the next one occupies, so none of the three ends the chain. Mercury, in its own sign, is where the chains of Mars and Jupiter finish. The Sun, also in its own sign, rules only itself, so it is not the chart's final ruler.

### Six themes

1. **The lagna lord is steady in the 9th.** Mars rules the Aries rising sign and sits in the 9th, in Sagittarius, a sign friendly to it. It helps form two combinations: Raja (the lords of the angular and trine houses joined) and Dhana (the lords of the wealth houses joined). The rising star is Ashwini, ruled by Ketu, which sits in the 9th alongside Mars. Ketu also aspects the rising sign, a malefic aspect, though only some authors count a node's aspect. Personality explains the lagna lord and the rising sign; Spirituality covers the 9th.

2. **The Moon is the core self.** The Moon sits in the 11th, in Aquarius. It counts as a benefic in this chart because it is waning and more than 90 degrees from the Sun, though its sign placement is neutral. Its exaltation in the navamsa gives it strong backing from inside the chart. Mind explains the Moon.

3. **Mercury offers promise that needs effort.** Mercury is exalted in Virgo in the 6th, but debilitated in the navamsa, in Pisces. Its gifts look strong on the surface and may take more effort to deliver. Mind explains Mercury; Health covers the 6th house.

4. **The Sun is in its strongest zone.** The Sun is in Leo, in its moolatrikona zone (the part of its sign where it is at its strongest), in the 5th house. Personality explains the Sun; Family covers the 5th.

5. **Venus holds the 4th in an enemy's sign.** Venus sits in Cancer, a sign ruled by its enemy the Moon, and it is the only planet in a corner house. It rules the 2nd and 7th, so money and partnership run through it. Family covers the 4th; Relationships covers the 7th.

6. **Jupiter and Rahu share the 3rd.** Jupiter rules the 9th and 12th and sits in Gemini, an enemy's sign, alongside Rahu. Jupiter is one of the planets that help form Raja and Dhana. Family covers the 3rd; Yogas and doshas gathers the combinations.

### Strengths and tensions

Strengths: the lagna lord sits in a trine house; the Moon is exalted in the navamsa; the Sun is in moolatrikona; Mercury is exalted in the main chart; Jupiter rules a trine house; several planets help form Raja and Dhana.

Tensions: Mercury is debilitated in the navamsa; Venus is in an enemy's sign; three corner houses are empty; Mars also rules the 8th, a difficult house, and shares the 9th with Ketu; Rahu sits with Jupiter.

### Birth-time sensitivity

The lagna falls at 10.9 degrees of Aries, so a change of sign would need the birth time to be off by about 43 minutes. The lagna sits about a quarter of the way through its navamsa division (26%) and about two-thirds through its dashamsa division (D10, 62%). Divisions this close to their edges can shift with a few minutes' error, so the navamsa and dashamsa readings depend closely on the exact birth time, and D10 notes should be read with that caveat. The Moon moves only about half a degree an hour, so its sign and star are far less sensitive than the lagna and the divisional charts.

### Functional roles for this lagna

- Mars: lagna lord and ruler of the 8th; a natural malefic whose role is mixed, leading the chart while also carrying a difficult house.
- Sun and Jupiter: trine lords; Jupiter also rules the 12th.
- Moon: ruler of the 4th, an angular house.
- Venus: rules the 2nd and 7th, and is an angular lord through the 7th.
- Saturn: rules the 10th and 11th, an angular house and a gain house.
- Mercury: rules the 3rd and the difficult 6th.
