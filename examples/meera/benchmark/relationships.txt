7Relationships, marriage and partner

The 7th house: Cancer, empty, with its lord in the 12th

The 7th house is Cancer, a watery, movable sign ruled by the Moon. It holds no planet and receives the aspects of Mars (its eighth-house aspect from the 12th), Mercury (its seventh aspect from the lagna) and Rahu. Cancer describes a partner who is caring, emotional, home-loving and protective, and a relationship in which domestic life and emotional security matter a great deal. Parashara's chapter on female horoscopy (BPHS ch. 80, v. 25) describes the husband of a woman with Cancer on the 7th as charming, affectionate and gentle. Mercury's aspect adds conversation, friendship and youthfulness to the bond; Rahu's adds an unusual, cross-cultural or unconventional element; Mars's adds passion and the occasional quarrel.

The 7th lord, the Moon, sits in the 12th with Saturn and Mars. This is the most important relationship factor in the chart. Parashara's line for the 7th lord in the 12th (BPHS ch. 24, v. 84) is unflattering — want of means and an extravagant spouse — but the 12th-house placement is more usefully read in its broader meaning: a partner connected with distance, foreign places, travel or institutions; a relationship that involves sacrifice, private devotion or periods apart; and significant shared expenditure. With Saturn beside the 7th lord, commitment tends to be serious, sometimes delayed, sometimes with an age or background difference, and tested by responsibility before it becomes durable. With Mars there, there is real warmth and attraction, and also a need to manage temper and impatience on both sides.

Jupiter and Venus: the protections

In a woman's chart, one long-standing school treats Jupiter as the significator of the husband, alongside Venus as the significator of love and marriage generally. Jupiter here is vargottama in the 4th house of home, and it aspects the 7th lord Moon directly. This is a strong, protective indication: a partner of principle and good intentions, a marriage rooted in shared values and a shared home, and a relationship that recovers well from difficulty. Venus, the general significator of marriage, is exalted and in its own sign in the navamsa, which promises affection, refinement and the capacity for a loving, harmonious partnership; with Rahu beside it, attraction to someone different from her own background is a recurring theme.

The navamsa (D9) view

The navamsa is traditionally the chart of marriage. Its lagna is Cancer, with the Moon in its own sign there; its 7th house is Capricorn, holding Ketu, and ruled by Saturn, which sits in Taurus in the D9 11th house of gains, a friendly sign. The partner is described as serious, responsible, practical and perhaps reserved or spiritually inclined (Ketu), and the relationship as one that grows more rewarding with time (Saturn in an upachaya house). Venus in its own sign in the D9 4th house of home is a good indicator of domestic happiness. Mars, the darakaraka, is in its own sign Aries in the D9 10th with Jupiter: the partner is likely to be capable, active and career-oriented, with a strong public or professional role.

The darakaraka (spouse significator by Jaimini's degree method) is Mars, in both the seven- and eight-karaka schemes. It describes a partner with energy, courage and drive, possibly from a technical, property, sport, defence, medical or engineering background, and, through its 12th-house placement, possibly from or living in a distant place.

Manglik status, with its references

Manglik (Kuja) dosha is checked from three reference points, using houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 and 12.

Table 10. Manglik check

 | Reference | Mars counted from it | Manglik?

 | Lagna | 12th house | Yes

 | Moon | 1st house (with the Moon) | Yes

 | Venus | 10th house | No

So the chart is Manglik from two references, including the lagna, which most practitioners treat as primary. Mars is also joined by Saturn, which some authors say intensifies the indication. Against this, Mars receives Jupiter's full aspect, which is among the most widely cited cancellation conditions, and the computation flags the dosha as cancelled on that basis. The 12th house is usually rated among the milder Manglik positions. Sources disagree on the exact list of cancellations and on whether counting from the Moon and Venus is classical at all; several respected modern writers also note that the "three-reference" method lacks a clear basis in the oldest texts. The fair summary: a genuine but mitigated Manglik indication, best handled in the traditional way, by comparing with the partner's chart rather than treating it as an obstacle. The classical chapter on female horoscopy reserves its most serious statements about Mars in the 12th for a Mars with no benefic support, which is not the case here.

For traditional compatibility matching (Ashtakoota), the relevant birth details are: Moon sign Sagittarius, nakshatra Mula pada 4, Gana Rakshasa, Nadi Adi, Yoni Dog (female), Varna Kshatriya, Vashya Manava.

Timing

Relationship matters are likely to be especially prominent in:

The Moon mahadasha (September 2015 – September 2025), the 7th lord's own period, between the ages of 27 and 37. Whether or not this coincided with marriage, it is the period in which partnership questions would have been most central, under the added seriousness of Saturn's Sade Sati (2014–2023).

January to June 2027, when transiting Jupiter, exalted in Cancer, occupies the 7th house and aspects the lagna, while transiting Saturn in Pisces aspects the natal 7th lord Moon. This combination of both slow planets on the 7th house and its lord is the "double transit" that K.N. Rao's school uses for timing marriage and partnership events. For a single person it is a classic window for commitment; for a married one it more often coincides with a new shared undertaking, a move, or a partner's major life event.

The Mars mahadasha as a whole (2025–2032), because Mars is the darakaraka, and especially its Venus sub-period (August 2030 – October 2031).

Late 2032 into 2033, when Jupiter in Capricorn aspects the 7th house and Saturn in Gemini aspects the 7th lord.

Friendships

The 11th house of friends and networks is Scorpio, empty, with its lord Mars in the 12th and Rahu's aspect upon it. Friendships tend to be few, deep and loyal rather than broad; some of the most important ones are with people who live far away, or are formed through travel, study abroad, spiritual or charitable settings. Rahu's aspect brings friends of different backgrounds. Because the 11th lord sits with her lagna lord, friends are closely bound up with her sense of self, and she is likely to give a good deal to them.
