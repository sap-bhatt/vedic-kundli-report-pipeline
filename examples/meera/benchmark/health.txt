6Health and vitality

Please read first

This section describes only what traditional astrology associates with this chart. It is not medical advice, is not a diagnosis, and should never replace a doctor's opinion or routine screening. Astrological "indicators" describe areas that tradition says deserve attention, not illnesses that will happen.

Overall constitution

Vitality in jyotish is judged mainly from the lagna, the lagna lord, the Sun and the Moon. Here the lagna holds Mercury with directional strength, which is a good sign for alertness and adaptability, but Mercury is also the 6th lord, and Parashara's line for the 6th lord in the lagna (BPHS ch. 24, v. 61) mentions a tendency to ill-health alongside fame and courage. The lagna lord Saturn is in the 12th, the house of sleep, rest and recovery, where BPHS (v. 12) speaks of reduced bodily comfort unless a benefic supports it; Jupiter's aspect provides that support. The Sun is modest (enemy sign, debilitated in the navamsa), and the Moon is waning and joined by Saturn and Mars. Overall: a resilient, enduring Capricorn constitution that does not have large reserves to spare, and that depends heavily on rest, sleep and pacing.

Timing notes

In traditional practice, health deserves extra attention during: the present Kantaka (Ardhashtama) Dhaiya, Saturn 4th from the Moon, to 3 June 2027 and again from 20 October 2027 to 23 February 2028, which tradition links to tiredness, restlessness and the health of the heart and chest (4th house); the Mars–Saturn sub-period (February 2028 – March 2029), when the two 12th-house malefics' energies combine; and the Ashtama Dhaiya, Saturn 8th from the Moon, from July 2034 to August 2036. None of these implies illness; they are windows when the tradition advises regular check-ups, steady routines and not overloading the body.

Some classical texts make statements about longevity for individual placements. Those statements are not meant to be read in isolation, and longevity judgement is deliberately not part of this report.
