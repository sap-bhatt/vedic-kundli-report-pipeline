1Chart at a glance

Meera was born with Makara (Capricorn) lagna rising at 21°58' — the lagna is the sign on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth, and in Parashari astrology it is the main reference point for the body, the temperament and the whole house structure. The Moon was in Dhanu (Sagittarius) in the nakshatra (lunar mansion) Mula, on the eleventh lunar day of the dark fortnight (Krishna Ekadashi), about half an hour before sunrise. The civil date was Sunday 14 February 1988, but because the birth came before sunrise, the traditional sunrise-to-sunrise reckoning places it on Shanivara, Saturday: the day of Saturn, who is also the lord of her lagna.

The shape of the chart is striking. The lagna lord Saturn, the 7th lord Moon and the 4th lord Mars sit together in the 12th house of retreat, distance and expenditure: the self, the partner and the home are all drawn toward it. Yet there is real strength elsewhere: Venus, the best planet for Capricorn rising, is exalted in the 3rd; Mercury, ruler of the 9th house of fortune, sits in the lagna with directional strength; and Jupiter, vargottama (in the same sign in the birth chart and the navamsa) in the 4th, aspects the crowded 12th. It is the chart of someone with a strong inward and faraway pull, held steady by a capable, communicative outer self.

The six themes that define this chart

The 12th-house centre of gravity. Saturn (lagna lord), the Moon (mind, and lord of the 7th) and Mars (lord of the 4th and 11th) sit together in Sagittarius in the 12th, the house of distant places, solitude, sleep, expenditure, hospitals and ashrams, and the search for meaning; Jupiter's sign gives it a dharmic colour. Her energy is often spent on others or far from home, and she needs time alone to recover.

Jupiter as the protector. Jupiter is in Aries in the 4th, vargottama, and its ninth-house aspect falls directly on all three 12th-house planets. Jupiter is a functional malefic for Capricorn (it rules the 3rd and 12th), but it is the great natural benefic, and its aspect on the lagna lord and the Moon is the most important softening factor in the chart. It also exchanges signs with Mars (Jupiter sits in Mars's sign, Mars in Jupiter's), which binds home (4th) and distance (12th) together.

An exalted yogakaraka in the house of effort. For Capricorn rising, Venus rules the 5th (a trine) and 10th (an angle), which makes it the yogakaraka, the planet able to produce success on its own. Here it is exalted in Pisces, and in its own sign Libra in the navamsa — about as strong as Venus can be. It sits in the 3rd house of communication, skills, initiative and the arts, joined by Rahu. Career and creativity come through her own voice, hands and enterprise.

A bright, articulate self. Mercury, lord of the 9th house of fortune (and of the 6th), sits in the lagna, where Mercury gains directional strength. It is retrograde and combust (very close to the Sun), so the intellect is strong but often works inwardly, privately, or in the service of someone else's authority before it is fully her own. Mercury is also the atmakaraka, the "soul" planet by Jaimini's degree method.

Partnership under weight. The 7th house is empty; its lord, the Moon, is waning and sits in the 12th with Saturn and Mars. Mars aspects the 7th, and Mars is Manglik from both the lagna and the Moon. Relationships are serious, karmically significant, and tied to distance, duty or sacrifice; Jupiter's aspect on the 7th lord is again the key protection.

A Mars chapter, then a Rahu–Venus chapter. Since 28 September 2025 the chart has been running the seven-year Mars mahadasha (to 27 September 2032). Mars is the 4th and 11th lord in the 12th, exchanged with Jupiter: home, property, relocation, gains and expenses are its themes. From 2032 comes the 18-year Rahu mahadasha, with Rahu sitting beside the exalted yogakaraka Venus, which is one of the more promising signatures in the chart.

Parashara (BPHS ch. 34, v. 39) lists Mars, Jupiter and the Moon as malefics and Venus and Mercury as benefics for Capricorn rising, adding that Saturn by itself does not kill. Modern lagna-wise lessons (for example onlinejyotish's Makara lagna article and its Venus lesson) call Venus the raja yoga karaka for Capricorn; the same site classes Mars as "neutral/benefic" because the 4th is a good kendra, while warning that the 11th lordship is not.

How much does the birth time matter?

The lagna at 21°58' Capricorn is about 8° from the end of the sign. Capricorn is a sign of short ascension at Jaipur's latitude, so the ascendant moves quickly — roughly one degree every three and a half minutes at this time of the morning. Even so, the D1 lagna would hold if the true time were up to about 25 minutes later or more than an hour earlier.

D9 (navamsa): the Cancer navamsa lagna holds from roughly 6 minutes earlier to 4–5 minutes later. Usable, with some care.

D10 (career): the Aries dashamsa lagna lies about 3 minutes after a boundary; a birth time a few minutes earlier would change it. Treated as provisional.

D7 (children) and D12 (parents): each is within about 2 minutes of a boundary, so they are used only as light confirmation.

Dashas: depend on the Moon, which moves about half a degree an hour; a few minutes' error shifts dasha dates by only days.

A few terms used throughout

Kendra: angular houses 1, 4, 7, 10 (pillars of the chart). Trikona: trines 1, 5, 9 (fortune and merit). Dusthana: 6, 8, 12 (difficulty, but also service, research and release). Upachaya: 3, 6, 10, 11 (houses that improve with time and effort). Exalted / debilitated: the sign where a planet is strongest / weakest. Vargottama: a planet in the same sign in D1 and D9, which steadies it. Parivartana: an exchange of signs between two planets, which links their houses. Dasha: a planetary period in the Vimshottari timing system; mahadasha is the major period, antardasha the sub-period. Dispositor: the lord of the sign a planet occupies, which "hosts" and conditions it.

The dispositor chain: who is really in charge?

Following each planet to the lord of its sign shows the chart's chain of command. Mercury and the Sun sit in Saturn's signs; Saturn, the Moon and Mars sit in Jupiter's sign Sagittarius; Venus and Rahu sit in Jupiter's other sign, Pisces; Ketu sits in Mercury's sign. Jupiter sits in Mars's sign, and Mars sits in Jupiter's. Every chain therefore ends in the Jupiter–Mars exchange between the 4th and the 12th houses.

Two things follow. First, the real seat of power in this chart is the axis of home and distance: where she lives, what she owns, and what she is willing to move away for. Such choices are never small for her; they reorganise everything else. Second, because Mars is both final dispositor and current mahadasha lord, the period 2025–2032 activates the chart at its root and is likely to feel structural rather than incidental.
