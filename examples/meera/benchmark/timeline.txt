11Timeline

Parashari timing rests on the Vimshottari dasha system: life is divided into nine major periods of fixed lengths, starting from the lord of the Moon's nakshatra at birth. Meera was born in the Ketu mahadasha (Mula is Ketu's nakshatra) with about 1.6 years of it remaining. A planet's period brings forward what that planet signifies and rules in the chart; transits of the slow planets (Jupiter and Saturn) then indicate when those promises are triggered.

Past periods: validation notes

These are offered as checks. If the descriptions broadly fit, they support the recorded birth time and the reading.

Ketu (to Sep 1989): she was born with transiting Saturn over her natal Moon, at the peak of a Sade Sati (December 1987 – March 1990). Infancy under such a transit is traditionally linked with a household carrying strain or responsibility.

Venus (Sep 1989 – Sep 2009): the yogakaraka's twenty-year period covered her whole childhood and education, typically a comparatively cultured upbringing with early talent in language, art or social life. The Venus–Mercury sub-period (Sep 2005 – Jul 2008) overlapped the Ashtama Dhaiya (2004–2007): pressure and important choices around higher education.

Sun (Sep 2009 – Sep 2015): the 8th lord in the 2nd. Often an effortful entry into working life, the need to build financial independence, and changes in family circumstances or in the father's life.

Moon (Sep 2015 – Sep 2025): the 7th lord in the 12th, conjoined with Saturn and Mars, running almost entirely under her second Sade Sati (Nov 2014 – Jan 2023). This often coincides with relationship and marriage questions becoming central, relocation or long periods away from home, heavy emotional responsibility and significant expenditure. The closing Moon–Venus sub-period (Jul 2023 – Mar 2025), after the Sade Sati ended, typically brings relief, recognition and progress.

The current period: Mars mahadasha (Sep 2025 – Sep 2032)

Mars rules the 4th (home, mother, property, peace) and the 11th (gains, networks). It occupies the 12th (foreign lands, expense, retreat) in Sagittarius, a friendly sign, at the gandanta junction, with the lagna lord Saturn and the 7th lord Moon. It exchanges signs with Jupiter, receives Jupiter's aspect, sits in its own sign in the navamsa 10th, is the darakaraka and is the final dispositor of the whole chart.

Parashara (BPHS ch. 47, vv. 28–33) gives Mars's period gains of land, property and wealth, honours and standing in foreign lands when Mars is in its own or exaltation sign, a kendra, trikona or the 11th, and says that when Mars is weak or in an inauspicious house it does not give the desired results; it adds that the period often starts well and ends with effort. This Mars meets both descriptions in part: it is in the 12th, but in a friendly sign, under Jupiter's aspect, exchanged into the 4th and strong in the navamsa. The realistic expectation is a structural, effortful and ultimately productive seven years centred on home and place, income and expenditure, and partnership.

One structural point is favourable: the strongest sub-period lords stand well from Mars. Venus and Rahu are 4th from it (a kendra), Jupiter 5th (a trine), Ketu 10th (a kendra), and Mercury is in a kendra from the lagna. BPHS repeatedly treats such positions as supportive.

Mars–Rahu (Feb 2026 – Mar 2027): now

Rahu sits in the 3rd house of initiative and communication, beside the exalted yogakaraka Venus, and 4th from Mars. BPHS (ch. 54, vv. 9–11) gives this sub-period gains, honours, property and journeys abroad when Rahu is in a kendra, trikona or the 11th, or with benefics, and difficulties when it is in the 8th or 12th with malefics. Rahu here is with a benefic in an upachaya house, so the better description largely applies: bold initiatives, new skills, a step toward a larger audience, journeys and relocation. Rahu's dispositor Jupiter is in the 4th, so home and property decisions are part of it. The overlapping Kantaka Dhaiya and transiting Rahu over the natal Sun add restlessness at home and pressure on family finances. The remaining four months suit completing what has been started and keeping agreements in writing.

Mars–Jupiter (Mar 2027 – Feb 2028)

Jupiter is vargottama in the 4th, a kendra from the lagna, 5th from Mars and its exchange partner. BPHS (ch. 54, vv. 15–18) gives this sub-period fame, honour and gains when Jupiter is in a kendra or trikona and strong, and trouble only when it is in the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitated or combust — none of which apply. It favours home, education, the mother, property, teachers and spiritual life, with travel and expense from Jupiter's 12th lordship. It opens with exalted Jupiter transiting her 7th house (to June 2027). Saturn enters Aries on 3 June 2027 and moves over natal Jupiter, making property and family responsibilities concrete; it returns to Pisces from 21 October 2027 to 24 February 2028 for the last spell of the Dhaiya.

Mars–Saturn (Feb 2028 – Mar 2029)

Saturn is the lagna lord, conjoined with Mars in the 12th. BPHS (ch. 54, vv. 23–28) gives Saturn's sub-period favour from authority, fame and children when Saturn is in its own sign, a kendra or trikona, or linked with the lagna lord or a benefic aspect, and losses, illness and enmity when it is in the 8th or 12th. Both apply: Saturn is in the 12th, but it is the lagna lord and receives Jupiter's aspect. This is the most demanding sub-period of the chapter: heavy responsibility, work far from home or within large institutions, careful management of energy, and friction between haste and caution. It begins just as the Dhaiya ends. From 28 December 2028 Jupiter in Libra, her 10th house, aspects transiting Saturn in Aries: both slow planets then influence the 4th house, a classic window for buying, building or moving home, while Jupiter 11th from the Moon brings, in Phaladeepika's words (ch. 26, sl. 20), a new position and honour.

Mars–Mercury (Mar 2029 – Mar 2030)

Mercury, 9th and 6th lord and atmakaraka, sits in the lagna with directional strength, retrograde and combust. BPHS (ch. 54, vv. 36–39) gives good speech, righteous conduct, fame and comfort when Mercury is in a kendra or trikona, reduced when it is combust; both apply. Expect study, writing, teaching or publishing, a more visible professional voice, and matters concerning the father or teachers, with heavier workload. Saturn's first entry into Taurus (9 August 2029) is the brightest transit of these years: Saturn 6th from the Moon gives, in Phaladeepika (ch. 26, sl. 22), "happiness all round".

Mars–Ketu (Mar 2030 – Aug 2030)

Ketu is in the 9th, a trikona from the lagna and a kendra from Mars; BPHS (ch. 54, vv. 48–51) gives favour, peace and gains in this condition. Short and inward: suited to pilgrimage, retreat and completion rather than large ventures.

Mars–Venus (Aug 2030 – Oct 2031): the key window

Venus is the exalted yogakaraka, lord of the 5th and 10th, 4th from Mars, and in its own sign in the navamsa. BPHS (ch. 54, vv. 55–57) gives this sub-period gain of position, happiness, vehicles and increased fortune when Venus is exalted or in its own sign, in a kendra or trikona, or lord of an auspicious house. Every condition is met, and several timers agree: the Venus-ruled Siddha Yogini runs to July 2032; transiting Saturn is in Taurus, 6th from the Moon; transiting Jupiter enters Scorpio, her 11th house of gains, on 23 September 2030 and aspects natal Venus from there; and the Taurus Chara dasha, a 5th-house period, is running. This is the most promising stretch of the coming decade for career recognition, creative or communication work reaching a wider audience, partnership happiness, matters concerning children, and financial improvement. Jupiter's move over the natal Moon, Mars and Saturn (February 2031, finally October 2031) adds a more inward note in its second half.

Mars–Sun (Oct 2031 – Feb 2032) and Mars–Moon (Feb 2032 – Sep 2032)

The Sun is the 8th lord in the 2nd; its sub-period highlights family, finances, authority and the father, sometimes with sudden changes. The next chapter: Rahu mahadasha (Sep 2032 – Sep 2050)

Rahu sits in the 3rd, an upachaya house, beside the exalted yogakaraka Venus, with its dispositor Jupiter vargottama. A node with a strong benefic tends to deliver that benefic's results, so this is one of the more promising long periods in the chart: ambition, visibility and larger or foreign audiences. BPHS (ch. 47) gives Rahu's period honour from rulers and foreigners when Rahu is with benefics, but warns that its opening years may bring strain and loss. The start is indeed heavy: Rahu–Rahu (27 September 2032 – 11 June 2035) coincides with the Sankata Yogini and with Saturn's Ashtama Dhaiya (July 2034 – August 2036). Rahu–Jupiter (11 June 2035 – 3 November 2037) brings steadier ground.

Phaladeepika (ch. 26, sl. 22) associates Saturn in the 4th from the Moon with trouble concerning spouse, relatives and wealth; modern authors describe pressure on home, mother, property and peace of mind, often as relocation, repairs, paperwork and restlessness rather than misfortune. At the same time Saturn is in the 3rd from the lagna, where it traditionally helps effort, courage and steady achievement, and it is transiting the sign of her exalted yogakaraka. Secondary timers: Yogini and Chara dashas

Yogini dasha is a shorter 36-year cycle of eight "Yoginis", each linked to a planet, used by many practitioners as a confirming timer.

Siddha (Venus), 5 Jul 2025 – 5 Jul 2032: a benefic Yogini of accomplishment, wealth and relationship, here ruled by the exalted yogakaraka. It runs alongside almost the whole Mars mahadasha and is the main reason the Mars chapter, for all its effort, should be productive.

Sankata (Rahu), 5 Jul 2032 – 5 Jul 2040: traditionally the most severe Yogini (upheaval, conflict, losses), coinciding with the start of the Rahu mahadasha and the 2034–2036 Ashtama Dhaiya. With natal Rahu beside exalted Venus, read it as demanding change rather than misfortune: a time for simplicity and caution with health and money.

Mangala (Moon), 5 Jul 2040 – 5 Jul 2041: a short, benefic Yogini of success and relief.

Chara dasha (Jaimini's sign-based system, popularised in modern India by K.N. Rao) times life by signs rather than planets; a dasha sign is judged by its position, the karakas in or aspecting it, and the houses counted from it.

Mithuna (Gemini), 13 Feb 2021 – 14 Feb 2028: the 6th house of work, service and health, aspected by the amatyakaraka Venus (from Pisces, the 10th from Gemini) and the darakaraka Mars: hard work, career development and partnership questions.

Vrishabha (Taurus), 14 Feb 2028 – 14 Feb 2039: the 5th house of creativity, children, learning and merit, aspected by the atmakaraka Mercury: eleven years in which creative, educational and family matters are central.

Mesha (Aries), 14 Feb 2039 – 14 Feb 2047: the 4th house, holding vargottama Jupiter: home, property, inner peace and teaching.
