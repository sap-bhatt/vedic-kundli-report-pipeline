- `conjunction/mars_saturn` (high, accepted_caveats) - Mars with Saturn in house 12: Mars with Saturn, two malefics, is read classically as hardship, quarrelsomeness and unreliability, with skill in metals or mechanical tricks; Raman links it to accidents, operations and disputes with siblings.
  - General results (not specific to this area; use only what fits): hardship or struggle; quarrelsome or disputatious; work with metals or mechanical skill
  - Strengthened by: Planetary war: the winner is strong (Saravali 4.36, 5.40).; Saravali 15.23: good vargas improve the results.
  - Weakened by: Planetary war: the southern, dim or small planet loses (Brihat Samhita 17.9); Frawley: the lower longitude wins. The loser gives bad results (Saravali 5.27).; Sastri's note: a conjunction of two malefics, bad.; Saravali 15.23: bad vargas worsen the results.
  - Disagreements: All texts read are negative; Jataka Parijata alone adds music. Planetary-war winner rules differ between Brihat Samhita and Frawley.
  - Phrasing: Frame as determination and endurance under pressure, with a need for patience, safety and calm handling of disputes. Never predict accidents, surgery or death; suggest care with machinery and conflict.
- `aspect/venus/9` (medium, accepted_caveats) - Venus aspects house 9: Venus aspecting the 9th (from the 3rd) gives luck when Venus is strong; Saravali says fortune comes away from home unless the aspecting planet owns the 9th.
  - For this section (spirituality): a benefic aspect makes the 9th more auspicious (Saravali 32.26-27)
  - Strengthened by: Venus strong (Saravali 32.5); Venus owning the 9th: fortune in one's own country (Saravali 32.4); Venus as 9th lord (Braha)
  - Weakened by: Venus debilitated or combust (Phaladeepika 15.1)
  - Phrasing: Speak of supportive luck and opportunities, possibly through travel; avoid certainty.
- `aspect/saturn/9` (medium, accepted_caveats) - Saturn aspects house 9: Saturn alone aspecting the 9th gives prosperity with stomach complaints in Raman; Saravali gives fortune away from home unless Saturn owns the 9th, and specific results for Saturn's aspect on Jupiter in the 9th.
  - For this section (spirituality): a malefic aspect on the 9th lessens its good results without benefic help (Saravali 32.26-27)
  - Strengthened by: Saturn owning the 9th: fortune in one's own country and the malefic proves auspicious (Saravali 32.4, 32.27); Saturn yogakaraka or well aspected by Jupiter (Braha, Saturn in the 9th)
  - Weakened by: Mars also afflicting the 9th (Raman); no benefic aspect (Saravali 32.26)
  - Phrasing: Speak of fortune that builds slowly through effort, perhaps away from home; mention digestion only as general self-care.

- `karaka/atmakaraka` (high, accepted) - Mercury is the Atmakaraka: The Atmakaraka is the planet with the highest degree within its sign; it stands for the self or soul and is the 'king' of the chart, whose condition colours the whole horoscope and governs bondage and liberation.
  - For this section (spirituality): Jaimini 1.1.11: the AK rules bondage and liberation. B.S. Rao: exalted or benefic-joined AK gives release, residence in holy places and moksha; debilitated or malefic-joined AK gives confinement and denies moksha unless benefics aspect. Rath: a malefic AK suggests a more advanced soul than a benefic one; a combust AK gives spiritual insight; a retrograde AK shows a deep-rooted desire behind the birth. PVR 14.3: the lord of the 8th from the AK (Maheswara) shows the channels through which the soul seeks liberation.
  - Strengthened by: exaltation, moolatrikona, own or friendly sign (Murthy ch. 2; B.S. Rao on 1.1.12); benefic conjunction or aspect (B.S. Rao on 1.1.12); conjunction with the Putrakaraka or Amatyakaraka for raja yoga (PVR 11.7.3, 11.8; Murthy ch. 7)
  - Weakened by: debilitation, combustion or an inimical sign (Murthy ch. 2) - though Rath reads combustion of the AK as giving spiritual insight; malefic conjunction or aspect (B.S. Rao on 1.1.12; Raman art. 129)
  - Disagreements: 7- vs 8-karaka: BPHS 32.1-2 records both views without choosing; Jaimini 1.1.19 says some authorities merge mother and son karakas.
  - Phrasing: Describe the Atmakaraka as the planet that shows the person's core drive and life lessons. Do not call a malefic or weak AK bad luck; frame it as the area of growth. Avoid predictions of imprisonment, death or moksha as certainties.
- `karaka/karakamsha_leo` (medium, accepted) - karakamsha (AK Mercury in D9 Leo): With the Atmakaraka in Leo navamsa, the texts speak of danger from animals - tigers in Parasara, dogs in B.S. Rao and Raman, wild beasts generally in others.
  - General results (not specific to this area; use only what fits): Fear or danger from wild animals or beasts (BPHS 33.2-8; Jaimini 1.2.6; Murthy ch. 6).
  - Strengthened by: benefic aspect (BPHS 33.2-8)
  - Weakened by: malefic aspect (BPHS 33.2-8)
  - Disagreements: Which sign: BPHS, B.S. Rao, Raman, PVR and Murthy take the Atmakaraka's navamsa sign; Murthy also uses the same sign in the rasi chart ('karakamsa rasi') and notes that some read 'swamsa' as the navamsa lagna; Abhyankar's translation speaks only of the 'division occupied by' the significator, counted from Aries; Kalidasa (Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4) reads the Atmakaraka's rasi position, which P.S. Sastri rejects.
  - Phrasing: The sign results are old, often literal (animals, water, falls); present them as areas to be careful with or symbolic themes, never as predicted accidents or diseases. Lead with supportive themes where sources give them, and do not state illegitimacy, adultery or criminality claims to a user.
