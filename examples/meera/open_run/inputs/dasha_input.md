## Chara karaka meanings (library)

- Sun is the Gnatikaraka (`karaka/gnatikaraka`): The Gnatikaraka is the sixth planet by degree in the 7-karaka scheme (seventh in the 8-karaka) and signifies cousins and kinsfolk; it is widely used for rivals and obstacles. Governs: kinsfolk and cousins (Jaimini 1.1.17; B.S. Rao; Raman art. 13); rivals and enemies in common use; literal meaning paternal cousin (PVR 8.2 note)
- Moon is the Bhratrikaraka (`karaka/bhratrikaraka`): The Bhratrikaraka is the third-highest planet by degree and signifies brothers and sisters; some read it also for courage and manliness. Governs: brothers and sisters (Jaimini 1.1.13 Abhyankar / 1.1.14 B.S. Rao; Raman art. 10; PVR 8.2); valour and manliness (P.S. Sastri on Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4)
- Mars is the Darakaraka (`karaka/darakaraka`): The Darakaraka is the planet with the lowest degree in its sign and signifies the spouse; the planet itself (not the 7th from it) describes the partner. Governs: wife or husband (Jaimini 1.1.18; BPHS 32.13-17 'Streekaraka'; Raman art. 14)
- Mercury is the Atmakaraka (`karaka/atmakaraka`): The Atmakaraka is the planet with the highest degree within its sign; it stands for the self or soul and is the 'king' of the chart, whose condition colours the whole horoscope and governs bondage and liberation. Governs: the native's self and soul (Jaimini 1.1.10-11 Abhyankar numbering; BPHS 32.3-8); bondage and release - misery and happiness (Jaimini 1.1.11 Abhyankar / 1.1.12 B.S. Rao); the main reference point for the karakamsa (its navamsa sign) and for raja yogas, sustenance and spiritual progress
- Jupiter is the Putrakaraka (`karaka/putrakaraka`): The Putrakaraka is the fifth planet by degree in the 7-karaka scheme (sixth in the 8-karaka) and signifies children; with the Atmakaraka it forms a key raja yoga. Governs: children (Jaimini 1.1.16; BPHS 32.13-17; Raman art. 12); subordinates and followers (PVR 8.2 note)
- Venus is the Amatyakaraka (`karaka/amatyakaraka`): The Amatyakaraka is the planet with the second-highest degree; as the 'minister' to the Atmakaraka it shows counsel, career and the means by which the native rises. Governs: ministers and advisers (Jaimini 1.1.12 Abhyankar / 1.1.13 B.S. Rao; PVR 8.2); profession (P.S. Sastri on Uttara Kalamrita ch. 4); career elevation and social status (Murthy ch. 10)
- Saturn is the Matrikaraka (`karaka/matrikaraka`): The Matrikaraka is the fourth-highest planet by degree and signifies the mother; in the 7-karaka tradition it may also stand for children. Governs: the mother (Jaimini 1.1.14 Abhyankar / 1.1.15 B.S. Rao; BPHS 32.13-17; Raman art. 11); children as well, for those who merge the mother and son karakas (Jaimini 1.1.19; BPHS 32.13-17)
- Karakamsha Leo (`karaka/karakamsha_leo`): With the Atmakaraka in Leo navamsa, the texts speak of danger from animals - tigers in Parasara, dogs in B.S. Rao and Raman, wild beasts generally in others.

## Dasha principles (general rules; P-codes are the rules that hold in some period below)

- `dasha_principle/houses_ruled_and_occupied` (high): A dasha lord brings up the affairs of the houses it rules, the house it sits in, the planets it joins and the planets that aspect it. Its own nature as a planet gives the general flavour of the period; its placement and links decide the specific events. How to apply: List the houses the mahadasha lord rules and occupies, plus the planets it is joined with or aspected by; the period mainly activates those houses.; Read the planet's natural significations (e.g. Sun: father, government; Venus: spouse, comforts) as the background colour of the whole period, then narrow them by its houses.; A well-placed lord improves the houses it rules, occupies or aspects during its period; a weak, badly placed lord harms the same houses.
  - P1 = `dasha_principle/houses_ruled_and_occupied` [bad] md_house in DUSTHANA -> matters of the houses the planet rules suffer (BPHS 47.12-15; Phaladeepika 20.14)
  - P2 = `dasha_principle/houses_ruled_and_occupied` [bad] md_house in DUSTHANA and (md_with_malefic or md_aspected_by_malefic) -> losses and distress in the period (BPHS 47.12-15)
  - P3 = `dasha_principle/houses_ruled_and_occupied` [mixed] md_house in DUSTHANA and md_aspected_by_benefic -> some good results at times despite a weak placement (BPHS 47.12-15)
  - P4 = `dasha_principle/houses_ruled_and_occupied` [good] md_house in KENDRA or md_house in TRIKONA or md_house == 11 -> matters of the houses the planet rules prosper (BPHS 47.7-11; Phaladeepika 20.14)
- `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` (high): Whether a dasha is good or bad depends first on which houses the planet rules for that lagna: lords of the trikonas (1, 5, 9) give good periods, lords of 3, 6 and 11 give troubled ones, kendra lords depend on whether the planet is a natural benefic or malefic, and a planet ruling both a kendra and a trikona is the best of all (yogakaraka). How to apply: Before reading any dasha, classify each planet for the lagna: trikona lord (good), 3/6/11 lord (bad), kendra lord (natural benefics lose goodness, natural malefics lose harm), 2nd/12th lord (neutral, act by association), 8th lord (bad unless it also rules the lagna).; Expect the dashas of trikona lords and of yogakarakas to be the productive periods of life, and the dashas of 3/6/11 and 8th lords to be the testing ones, other factors equal.; When a planet rules two houses, the one holding its moolatrikona sign weighs more; a good lordship can override a weaker bad one (e.g. 9th lordship over 8th).
  - P5 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [good] 'trikona_lord' in md_role or 'lagna_lord' in md_role -> auspicious dasha (BPHS 34.2-7; Laghu Parashari 6; Phaladeepika 20.41)
  - P6 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [bad] 'trishadaya_lord' in md_role and not ('trikona_lord' in md_role or 'lagna_lord' in md_role) -> troubled period even if the planet is a natural benefic (BPHS 34.2-7; Laghu Parashari 6; Phaladeepika 20.41)
  - P7 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [bad] 'trishadaya_lord' in md_role or md_house in [3, 6, 11] -> unfavourable dasha (BPHS 48.18-20)
  - P8 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [good] 'yogakaraka' in md_role -> one of the best periods of the chart (BPHS 34.13; Laghu Parashari 12)
  - P9 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [mixed] md in ['Jupiter', 'Venus', 'Mercury', 'Moon'] and 'kendra_lord' in md_role and not ('trikona_lord' in md_role or 'lagna_lord' in md_role) -> natural benefic loses its goodness through kendra rulership (most for Jupiter and Venus, less for Mercury, least for the Moon) (Laghu Parashari 10; BPHS 34.8-10; Phaladeepika 20.50-51)
  - P10 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [neutral] md in ['Sun', 'Mars', 'Saturn'] and 'kendra_lord' in md_role and not ('trikona_lord' in md_role or 'lagna_lord' in md_role) -> natural malefic as kendra lord is not harmful but not positively good either (Laghu Parashari 12; BPHS 34.14)
  - P11 = `dasha_principle/functional_nature_by_lagna` [neutral] md in ['Sun', 'Moon'] and 'eighth_lord' in md_role -> 8th lordship does not spoil the period (Laghu Parashari 11; Phaladeepika 20.41)
- `dasha_principle/dignity_and_strength` (high): The same dasha gives more or less of its promise according to the lord's strength: exalted, moolatrikona, own or friendly sign raise the good results; debilitation, enemy sign or combustion reduce or spoil them. A planet moving toward its exaltation gives a better period than one moving toward its fall. How to apply: Scale the good results of a dasha by the lord's dignity: full when exalted, about three-quarters in moolatrikona, half in own sign, a quarter in a friend's sign, little or none when debilitated (Phaladeepika's scale).; Treat a combust lord like a debilitated one.; A strong lord in a good house gives the full positive results of its houses; the same lord weak and in a dusthana gives their negative side.
  - P12 = `dasha_principle/dignity_and_strength` [bad] md in ['Jupiter', 'Venus', 'Mercury', 'Moon'] and (md_dignity in ['debilitated', 'enemy'] or md_house in [6, 12]) -> only bad results (Phaladeepika 20.27)
  - P13 = `dasha_principle/dignity_and_strength` [bad] md in ['Sun', 'Mars', 'Saturn', 'Rahu', 'Ketu'] and (md_dignity in ['debilitated', 'enemy'] or md_house in [6, 12]) -> severe suffering (Phaladeepika 20.27)
  - P14 = `dasha_principle/dignity_and_strength` [good] md_dignity == 'exalted' -> full measure of the dasha's good results (Phaladeepika 20.30)
  - P15 = `dasha_principle/dignity_and_strength` [mixed] md_dignity == 'friend' -> about a quarter of the good results (Phaladeepika 20.30)
  - P16 = `dasha_principle/dignity_and_strength` [bad] md_dignity in ['debilitated', 'enemy'] or md_combust or md_house in DUSTHANA -> the weak-lord results of the houses it rules (Phaladeepika 20.14-20)
- `dasha_principle/navamsa_strength` (medium): The dasha lord's condition in the navamsa (D9) modifies its period: vargottama or an own/exalted or benefic navamsa improves results, a bad navamsa limits them even when the D1 position looks strong. How to apply: After judging the D1 position, check the lord's navamsa: vargottama or own/exalted navamsa supports the good results.; Do not promise the full results of a strong D1 position if the navamsa position is weak or afflicted.; A weak D1 planet in a good navamsa still gives mixed results, improving in the second half.
