## Personality and temperament

### The rising sign and the outer manner

Your rising sign is Capricorn, so the first impression is of someone steady, practical and quietly determined. The classical texts describe Capricorn rising as hard-working and persistent, someone who finishes what they start and aims high. Modern astrologers add a traditional, practical streak and a tenacity that keeps going when others stop. These are tendencies rather than a fixed fate, and the rest of the chart decides how they show up.

For this rising sign the planet most on your side is Venus. It rules your 5th house (a trikona, a house of creative intelligence and past merit) and your 10th house (a kendra, the house of work and public standing), which makes it the most helpful planet for Capricorn rising. Venus sits in Pisces, in the 3rd house, so its support works through daily life and the way you deal with people, more than through your own body.

### The lagna nakshatra: Shravana

Your birth star inside the rising sign is Shravana. Its traditional meaning is learning through listening: a receptive mind, a gift for teaching and organising, and a good reputation. Trivedi describes it as amicable, persevering and receptive, and Brihat Jataka calls such people learned and graceful. The same tradition warns that a Shravana native can be easily misled by bad company or poor advice, so the star's strength depends a good deal on whom you keep close. Its ruler is the Moon, which sits in the 12th house with Mars and Saturn.

### The lagna lord: Saturn in the 12th house

Saturn rules your rising sign and your 2nd house, so it is the planet that most directly carries your self. It sits in Sagittarius, in the 12th house, in a neutral sign. A lagna lord in the 12th house tends to bring its results with more effort and later in life. The classical texts link this placement with a self drawn toward spending, distance and withdrawal, and warn of heavy expense and a short temper unless benefic influences help. Rath describes the same pull as a spendthrift tendency and a quick temper. Modern readers add an interest in foreign residence and in inner, spiritual life.

Several things soften this placement in your chart. Jupiter aspects Saturn, and the texts treat a benefic aspect as the main relief for a lagna lord in the 12th. Saturn sits in Jupiter's sign, Sagittarius, which Saravali and modern writers (Foss, Braha) treat as favourable: a man of few words, soft in disposition, whose trust is earned and whose leadership matures with time. The one point to watch is Mars, which shares the house with Saturn. Classical texts see a malefic in company without a benefic aspect as the difficult case, and here Jupiter's aspect is what balances it.

Saturn also forms a Raja combination, linking the kendra and trikona lords, and a Dhana combination, linked to the 1st, 2nd, 5th, 9th and 11th lords. These are explained in the yogas section.

The practical reading is a call for budgeting, and for meaningful solitude, retreat or service, rather than a warning of hardship.

### Planets in the rising sign and aspecting it

Mercury sits in your rising sign. It is retrograde and combust, which means it lies only about 6 degrees from the Sun and its light is overshadowed by the Sun's. Mercury is also your Atmakaraka, the planet with the highest degree in its sign. In Jyotish the Atmakaraka stands for the self and shows the core drive and main lessons of this life. Murthy describes a Mercury Atmakaraka as intelligent and drawn to material gain, and Rath advises keeping speech truthful. Combustion usually weakens an Atmakaraka, but Rath reads it as giving spiritual insight. The bright side of Mercury here is quick wit, mental ingenuity, humour and adaptability, with a kind and forgiving nature (Bhrigu Sutras). Ketu, the node placed in the 9th house, is said to cast its aspect onto the rising sign, and Ketu's aspect on Mercury is also a malefic one. Several authors deny that the nodes cast aspects at all, so give this point only light weight.

### The Sun: courage, temper and money

The Sun is in Aquarius, in the 2nd house. Aquarius is an enemy sign for the Sun, and the Sun rules your 8th house, a house of difficulty. Its main strength is courage and firmness in action. Saravali describes such a person as very short-tempered but brave, and as unreliable in friendship and careful, even miserly, with money. Raman adds stubbornness, a strong sense of self-worth and rare faculties.

In the 2nd house the Sun ties money and speech to authority, and the texts disagree on the outcome. Jataka Parijata gives a liberal and eloquent speaker with property; Phaladeepika and Raman give poor earnings or stammering; Raman also gives good earnings that tend to leak away through waste. The Sun is aspected by Saturn. By its nature Saturn is a malefic, so the aspect is hard in manner, though Saravali links Saturn's aspect on the Sun with victory over enemies and honours. In the navamsa (D9) the Sun is debilitated, which leans its strength toward the demanding side.

The practical reading is courage with a need to manage temper and money. Be careful in official or legal disputes, and with spending for show.

### The Moon, briefly

The Moon is in Sagittarius, in the 12th house, with Mars and Saturn. It is waning, so in this chart it counts as a natural malefic, though it is strong in the navamsa, where it is in its own sign, Cancer. Its full character is covered in the Mind section.

### Birth day and birth tithi

You were born on a Saturday, the day of Saturn, which the traditional texts associate with a serious, enduring temperament. Jataka Bharanam also notes a readiness to anger that is worth watching. You were born on Krishna Ekadashi, which Jataka Bharanam describes as a tithi of devotion: a person devoted to the gods and the learned, who keeps vows, gives in charity, and is pure-hearted and capable. Think of these as small colourings of temperament, not as fate.

### Synthesis of temperament

Taken together, the chart shows a person of outward steadiness and inner depth. The rising sign and Venus give a practical, persistent drive, and Shravana gives a receptive mind that learns by listening and teaches well when it is sure of its ground. Mercury, the self-planet, sits close to the Sun in your rising sign, which makes your intelligence quick and inquisitive but still learning to speak carefully. Your birth star group is the Rakshasa gana, which the texts describe as a forceful, independent temperament; this is a style, not a moral judgement. Saturn, your lagna lord, sits in the 12th house, so your self is tested through spending, distance and inner work, and Jupiter's aspect on the 12th house, with Jupiter vargottama (in the same sign in the birth chart and the navamsa, here Aries), gives a steady source of help. Your courage runs hot, as the Sun in Aquarius shows, and it needs managing.

### When this area is most active

The Mars and Saturn sub-period from February 2028 to March 2029 is the most supportive stretch for your self, because Saturn, your lagna lord, is the sub-lord there and rules the 1st house. The double transit on the rising sign from July to December 2028, when Jupiter and Saturn both aspect it, is a classical marker for matters of the self. The Mars and Sun sub-period from October 2031 to February 2032 brings the Sun into focus; it is an active period rather than the best one for you.
