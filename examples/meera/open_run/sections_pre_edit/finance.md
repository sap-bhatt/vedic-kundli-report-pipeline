## Finance and wealth

Your money picture turns on one fact: the house of expenses is busy. Three planets sit in the twelfth house, and it also holds the lord of your savings and the lord of your income. Your lagna lord, Saturn, is one of them. The chart points to money that comes through steady effort and needs deliberate protection to stay with you.

### The second house: savings and family money

Saturn rules this house and sits in the twelfth, so holding on to savings is the central test. The modern astrologer Braha links this placement with difficulty keeping money. Saturn also aspects the second house, and so the Sun there, which adds the same message: money comes through hard effort.

The Sun in this house is an enemy in its sign and rules the eighth house, which tilts its results toward the difficult side. The classical texts split here. Brihat Jataka describes great wealth that authority may take away, while Saravali describes little wealth. Raman, a modern astrologer, says money comes through hard work and tends to slip away. Saturn's ownership of the second house helps, since Phaladeepika says a strong lord of savings limits loss, but Saturn is only neutral in its sign. Saturn also shares the twelfth house with the Moon and Mars, and one older text ties wasteful spending to such company. There is one hopeful note: Saravali associates Saturn in Sagittarius with affluence in later life.

### The eleventh house: income and gains

Mars rules this house and sits in the twelfth, in a friendly sign. It is strong in the navamsa, a finer chart used to judge inner strength, and Jupiter's benefic aspect supports it. The texts still agree that gains tend to flow out. Phaladeepika, Raman and Braha read this placement mostly negatively, and some older texts tie it to fines and penalties. The Moon and Saturn with Mars in the twelfth weaken it further. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra also notes spending on good causes, and modern astrologers add that money may flow to travel, charity or distant ventures. Keeping a budget and clear records is the practical answer.

### The fifth house: investments and intelligence

Venus rules this house from the third house, in the sign of its exaltation. Venus is the yogakaraka for Capricorn, the planet that rules both a trikona and a kendra, which makes it the most supportive planet in this chart. Its favourable results therefore carry weight. The texts differ, though. Saravali calls it very wealthy, Phaladeepika describes a poor outcome, and Raman says finances are not very successful. Venus sits in the third house of effort alongside Rahu, which suggests that gains come from steady work more than from speculation. Investment should be measured and patient.

### The ninth house: fortune and blessings

Mercury rules this house and sits in the first house. The classical view is that wealth comes through one's own efforts, and riches when the lagna lord joins it and a benefic aspects it. Only part of that is present: Saturn does not join Mercury, and Ketu, a malefic, aspects it. Mercury is also close to the Sun, which dims it, so fortune comes mainly through effort. Jataka Parijata links this placement with pursuing wealth alongside learning.

Ketu sits in the ninth house as well. Phaladeepika describes an indigent outcome, Raman says Ketu here is frugal and saves much, and another text links it with wealth from foreign sources. Ketu in its sign is said to bring wealth even in a weak state.

The house is also well supported by aspects. Venus, strong in this chart, favours luck and resources, as Saravali describes, and Saturn's aspect adds prosperity in Raman's view.

### The twelfth house: expenses and outflow

Jupiter rules this house and sits in the fourth, in a friendly sign. As a benefic, its aspect falls on the twelfth house itself and softens the three planets there. The texts link Jupiter in the fourth with wealth in grain and conveyances, and with income from property. Saravali does warn of heavy expenses under Jupiter's sign, so outgo tends to go toward home, property and transport. Jupiter's support keeps this house from being purely a drain.

### Dhana and other wealth combinations

Dhana is a combination of the lords of the wealth houses. Here Mars, lord of income, and Saturn, lord of savings, sit together in the twelfth. Uttara Kalamrita links the second and eleventh lords with affluence, so the potential is real, but it sits in the house of outgo.

Chandra-Mangala, the Moon and Mars together, is a combination that Raman calls a strong stabiliser of finances. Raman's exception applies only when both planets are debilitated, and neither is, so the combination leans positive. Sunapha, with Mercury second from the Moon, points to self-acquired wealth, which Raman says can go either way.

### Gains against expenses

Gains and outgo are closer than the gains alone suggest. The income house is weakened by its lord sitting in the expense house, while the expense house is busy but supported by Jupiter. Overall the chart tends to earn more than it keeps unless saving is deliberate.

### Overall

Money here comes through skill, effort and steady work. The strongest supports are the exalted Venus, the aspects on the ninth house, and the wealth combinations. The main difficulty is that both the savings lord and the income lord sit in the house of outgo. Steady saving and caution with loans matter more than bold moves. Big money and loan decisions remain yours to make, ideally with a qualified adviser.

### When this area is most active

From March 2029 to March 2030, a Mars period runs with a Mercury sub-period. Mercury rules the house of fortune and Mars the house of gains, so earnings through your own effort get focus. From May to September 2030, Jupiter and Saturn both touch the second house of savings, a good time to review budgets. From September 2030 to February 2031, Jupiter sits in the eleventh house of income and Saturn aspects it, so income matters draw attention, and the caution about gains flowing out matters most.
