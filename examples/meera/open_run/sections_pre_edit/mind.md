## Mind, emotions and inner life

### The Moon's sign, house and nakshatra

Your Moon sits in Sagittarius, in the twelfth house of private life, retreat and the unseen. The inner world is rich, and it is also where you withdraw to recharge. Sagittarius gives an articulate, principled and energetic mind, drawn to poetry, the arts and questions of dharma (right living), one that responds to persuasion far more than to force. Older texts describe such a person as self-confident, grateful and attached to family, though they disagree on how that attachment plays out with relatives: one reads it as friction, another as closeness. Treat any tension there as something to watch, not a settled pattern.

The Moon is waning, about 51 degrees from the Sun. In this chart that makes it a natural malefic, and the texts treat a waning Moon as weaker, so its gifts tend to arrive with more effort.

The Moon's nakshatra (lunar star) is Mula, ruled by Ketu. Mula gives pride, steadiness and a strong drive to get to the root of things. You are direct, bold and reserved, quick to decisive action, and fond of comfort. Traditional texts treat the opening stretch of Mula as sensitive, but your Moon sits well past that point, so that caution does not apply here. Mula is widely feared in Indian families. The verses behind that fear are traditional cautions, and the practice pairs them with optional peace-making rites. They say nothing about anyone's safety.

### Aspects and company of the Moon

Jupiter aspects the Moon, and this is the strongest support the Moon receives. Jupiter is a natural benefic, and Saravali links its aspect on the Moon with ministerial rank and steady judgement. Jupiter is the Moon's sign lord and sits in your fourth house (explained under Family). It also carries the twelfth house as a lord, so its help comes with some effort.

The Moon shares its house with Saturn, about four degrees away, and with Mars, about ten degrees away. Saturn is the closer companion. Raman links a waning Moon with Saturn to sloth and lethargy, so the pattern to notice is heaviness and a pull to withdraw when you are tired. Mars adds urgency, so when the Moon is strained, irritation can rise quickly.

### Moon yogas

Sunapha is present: Mercury sits in the second house from the Moon and forms this yoga. Saravali and the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra describe the person as intelligent, virtuous, peaceable and well regarded.

### Mercury and thinking style

Mercury, the planet of intellect and speech, sits in your first house. Every text read describes this as a learned, intelligent and pleasant-spoken placement. It shows as quick wit, adaptability, curiosity and a light humour. As the Atmakaraka (the planet that leads on the soul's path), Mercury makes thinking central to who you are.

Mercury comes with caveats. It is retrograde, and it is about six degrees from the Sun, which makes it combust. The texts say a combust planet loses much of its benefit, though Raman suggests Mercury may partly escape this burn. In Capricorn, Mercury gives a practical, cautious, work-focused mind that often carries responsibility for others. Older texts are less generous here, describing a timid streak and restless dreams. Ketu's aspect on Mercury is malefic in nature, so attention can scatter or turn idiosyncratic. Some authors count node aspects and others do not, so keep this as a tendency rather than a rule. The nervous restlessness is something to manage, not a fixed trait.

### Emotional needs and patterns

You feel things deeply and privately. You need quiet time and solitude to recharge, and a crowded, noisy stretch drains you. You respond best to reasoned, respectful talk and to meaning: learning, dharma, creative work. Pressure works against you. When the Moon is tired, the Saturn pull toward heaviness is the thing to watch. The steady Mula drive can harden into sharpness or sarcasm when left unchecked, and Ketu in the star can add an unusual edge to it. A practical habit helps: give your mind a regular rhythm of rest and reflection before deciding anything important.

### When this area is most active

Two stretches stand out. From March 2029 to March 2030, Mars is the main period and Mercury the sub-period, so intellect, study and work come forward, with a supportive tone. From February to September 2032, Mars is the main period and the Moon the sub-period, which brings emotional life to the centre; the tone is neutral, so it may be a time to look at your own needs and rhythms. Both are points to compare with your own experience, not predictions.
