## Relationships, marriage and partner

### The 7th house and its lord

The 7th house is Cancer, and its lord, the Moon, sits in the 12th house in Sagittarius, together with Mars and Saturn. The Moon is a natural malefic in this chart because it is waning, so its influence on partnership is more reserved. Several classical and modern texts agree that a 7th lord in the 12th points to less ease in marriage, expenses that need planning, and a partner from a different background. Partnership here asks for patience and careful budgeting.

Two factors soften this. Mercury's aspect reaches the 7th house, and Raman links a 7th lord aspected by Jupiter with a noble, virtuous spouse. Some texts also read a benefic aspect on the 7th house as protection for the house even when its lord sits in the 12th. No planet occupies the 7th house. Mars, Mercury and Rahu all aspect it.

Mars's aspect, from the 12th house, is the most active one. It adds passion, drive and heat to the partnership, and some texts tie it to friction. Mars rules the 4th and 11th houses, a mixed pair, and Jupiter's aspect on Mars helps channel its energy, so the texts ask for patience and fairness rather than predicting strain.

Mercury, which sits in the 1st house, aspects the 7th. Some texts suggest it brings a youthful, witty and communicative partner. Rahu, which sits with Venus, also aspects the 7th house.

### Venus, the marriage planet

Venus is the strongest partnership factor in this chart. It is exalted in Pisces, is the yogakaraka for a Capricorn lagna (ruling the 5th and 10th houses), and is a natural benefic. It sits in the 3rd house together with Rahu. Mars, a natural malefic, aspects Venus, and Venus and Ketu aspect each other.

Foss describes the Pisces influence as love that can deepen into devotion and unconditional acceptance. Saravali adds that such a person keeps promises. For Venus in the 3rd house, Saravali also suggests a partner with a firm will, so the marriage may have a strong voice on one side and a clear sense of commitment on the other.

### Darakaraka and the navamsa

Mars is the Darakaraka, the planet with the lowest degree in its sign among the chara karakas. The Darakaraka signifies the spouse, and the planet itself describes the partner. Mars here points to a partner who is energetic and independent. Jupiter, a benefic, aspects Mars, which the texts count as strengthening the Darakaraka. Murthy holds that the nature and friendship of the Darakaraka show the harmony of a couple. Some astrologers also judge the spouse from houses counted from the lagna, not from the karaka alone.

In the navamsa (D9), the chart Parashara assigns to the spouse, Capricorn is the 7th house, and its lord Saturn is placed in Taurus, the 11th house of the navamsa. Ketu occupies the navamsa 7th house. Saravali describes a partner ruled by Saturn as older and more serious, with harder qualities, and asks for patience. The navamsa refines the main chart rather than replacing it. Venus, the marriage planet, is moolatrikona in Libra in the navamsa, in its 4th house, which supports its promise in the main chart.

### Manglik status

The Manglik (Kuja) dosha is cancelled by Jupiter's aspect on Mars. Counted from the lagna and from the Moon, it is present, because Mars sits in the 12th house from the lagna and shares the Moon's house. Counted from Venus, Mars is 10th, so it is not present from that point. The Manglik houses are 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 and 12.

Older texts treat the dosha with more seriousness, while modern astrologers from Raman onward say it is over-applied. A matching Mars dosha in a partner's chart is the other widely shared cancellation, and most astrologers consider the dosha largely cancelled when both partners have it. Read it as a Mars theme in partnership: energy, independence and friction to channel. It is common and is only one factor among many in marriage matching.

### Friendships (11th house)

The 11th house is Scorpio, ruled by Mars, which sits in the 12th house. Braha reads this as few friends, or friendships that need care, so a smaller and more selective circle of friends is the likely pattern. Mars sits in a friendly sign, Sagittarius, which softens the point. Rahu also aspects the 11th house.

### When this area is most active

Three stretches stand out. The Venus sub-period within the Mars main period, from August 2030 to October 2031, brings the chart's strongest partnership planet into focus, and the texts treat it as supportive. Jupiter and Saturn both aspect the 7th house in two spells: September 2030 to February 2031, and June to October 2031. Classical texts treat these as timing markers for partnership, a time to look closely at commitments and shared finances rather than a forecast. Details are in the Timeline.
