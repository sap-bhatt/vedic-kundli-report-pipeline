## Timeline

### How timing works

Vimshottari dashas are nine planetary periods of fixed length, run in a set order and starting from the lord of the Moon's lunar mansion (nakshatra) at birth. The Moon was in Mula, so the first period was Ketu's, with about 1.6 years still to run at birth. A period brings forward what its planet rules and signifies in this chart: the houses it governs, the house it sits in, and the planets it joins or is aspected by. Slow transits of Saturn and Jupiter act as timers inside those periods. They show when promises are tested or triggered, and the texts read them that way only where they say so.

Each past-period note below is a check to compare with your own life, not a claim about what happened.

**Ketu period, Feb 1988 to Sep 1989 (age 0-1).** Ketu sits in the 9th house, a trikona, one of the fortunate houses alongside the 1st and 5th, and Venus's benefic aspect reaches it. That supports the good side of this period. The texts also describe Ketu as unsettled and prone to sudden change, a picture that Ketu in Virgo sharpens. This stretch is hard to remember directly, so the useful question is whether family accounts of those first years include a move or a disrupted household.

**Venus period, Sep 1989 to Sep 2009 (age 1-21).** Venus is exalted in Pisces and is the chart's yogakaraka, a planet that rules both an angle and a trine for Capricorn, which the texts treat as among the most supportive planets. Its one caution is its placement in the 3rd house, where the texts speak of reserve with money and of a spouse who takes the lead. The period is therefore mixed, with the strong side dominant. It often coincides with comfort at home, family celebrations, a growing sense of possessions and the arts, and recognition from people in authority. Venus also carries the role the texts call the Amatyakaraka, linked with counsel, profession and rising through work. Does this fit your childhood and early youth?

**Sun period, Sep 2009 to Sep 2015 (age 21-27).** The Sun is in Aquarius, an enemy sign, weakened in the navamsa (the ninth-division chart), aspected by Saturn, and it rules the 8th house. The texts read a Sun in this state on its harder side: friction with authority, concern for a parent's wellbeing, and moves away from home. This period often coincides with pressure at work or under a difficult manager, family strain, or a change of city, if those match your twenties. The texts ask for patience with superiors and a steady routine of rest.

**Moon period, Sep 2015 to Sep 2025 (age 27-37).** The Moon is waning, sits in the 12th house with Mars and Saturn, and rules the 7th. The texts tie a waning Moon in the 12th to a more inward life, money strain, concern for the mother and friction with those in authority. Jupiter's aspect and the Moon's own sign, Sagittarius, give some steadiness to home life. The period often coincides with financial pressure, family worries and a need to pull back, and the texts ask for extra care of health and vitality. Does this match your late twenties and early thirties?

### The Mars period, Sep 2025 to Sep 2032 (current)

Mars is the period lord now, and it shapes the next six years more than any other planet. It sits in the 12th house with the Moon and Saturn, a crowded placement that the texts read as one of expense, distance and rest. It rules the 4th (home, land, vehicles) and the 11th (gains, friendships, elder siblings). A well-placed 4th lord points to gains in property, while the 11th lordship is one the texts read as more demanding for this lagna.

Mars is a friend in Sagittarius and strong in the navamsa, so its good results show in part despite the 12th house, and Jupiter aspects it. It forms a link with Saturn, the lagna lord, of a kind the texts associate with rising responsibility and recognition. Both planets sit in the 12th, so this is potential rather than guaranteed. Mars is also counted as Manglik from the lagna and from the Moon, and that is cancelled because Jupiter aspects it.

The texts disagree on shape. BPHS makes a Mars period strongly good or strongly bad according to dignity and house. Phaladeepika and Jataka Parijata describe a mix of gains through land and fighting, alongside quarrels with siblings and heat-related complaints. The mixed reading fits this chart best: property and effort on one side, expense, friction and a need for health care on the other. Mars sits in the first ten degrees of its sign, so its results tend to arrive early, and the texts expect some strain toward the close of even a good Mars period. The realistic expectation is an active, property-minded early phase, with steadiness and health care needed later.

### Sub-periods of the Mars period

**Mars within Mars, Sep 2025 to Feb 2026 (past).** The texts describe the start of a Mars period as hot and quarrelsome: friction with siblings and friends, heat-related complaints and trouble with officials. The strong navamsa gave ambition support through government or property, so this stretch often coincides with a driven, short-tempered beginning. Does the winter of 2025 to 2026 match?

**Mars within Rahu, Feb 2026 to Mar 2027 (current).** Rahu rules no house, so it acts through the 3rd house it sits in, through its sign lord Jupiter (in the 4th), and through the planets near it. The 3rd is one of Rahu's better placements, linked with courage, initiative and a long, active life, at some cost to harmony with siblings. Most older sources describe this Rahu stretch through its troubled side alone: health strain, misunderstandings, legal and official pressure, and losses. BPHS allows good results when Rahu is well placed, and this placement, in a benefic's sign, qualifies. Against that, Mars and Ketu both aspect Rahu, which keeps the difficult side alive. The net reading is mixed and leans toward caution. Take care with legal paperwork, travel, money and people of doubtful intent, and keep up health checks. This is traditional astrology, not medical advice. Remedies (optional): Naga puja, feeding Brahmins, or Mrityunjaya japa, the practices the texts list for this stretch, taken as traditional observances rather than guarantees.

**Mars within Jupiter, Mar 2027 to Feb 2028.** Jupiter sits in the 4th, a kendra and home house, in Aries, which is Mars's own sign, and it is vargottama (the same sign in the navamsa). That gives a welcome easing: support from mentors, home and property matters settling, and growth in faith and learning. Jupiter also rules the 3rd and 12th, so it is not a clean benefic for this lagna. Mars and Jupiter exchange signs, a Dainya exchange, a kind the texts read as lower in strength and as pointing to effort and obstacles around the 12th-house matters. The net reading leans favourable, with strain at the edges.

**Mars within Saturn, Feb 2028 to Mar 2029.** This is the most demanding Mars sub-period. Saturn sits in the 12th with the Moon and Mars. It is the lagna lord, and it also rules the 2nd house, which the texts link with the need for extra care of health and vitality. The softeners are real: Saturn rules the lagna, Jupiter aspects it, and its link with Mars is one the texts associate with recognition and with building resources. The practical emphasis is to avoid clashes with authorities, review legal and property papers, keep rest and routine, and look after family.

**Mars within Mercury, Mar 2029 to Mar 2030.** Mercury sits in the lagna, which the texts agree makes for a learned, quick and pleasant-spoken person. It rules the 9th (a trikona) and the 6th. It is, however, retrograde and combust, too close to the Sun to shine, so its results come with less ease and more effort. The stretch suits study, trade, negotiation and settling disputes. The texts want care with rivals, careless speech and travel, and a general note to keep up rest and health checks. Mercury also forms Sunapha, a yoga the texts read as earning through one's own effort.

**Mars within Ketu, Mar 2030 to Aug 2030.** A short stretch. Ketu sits in the 9th, a trikona, in Virgo, and Venus's benefic aspect reaches it. The older texts describe Ketu periods as turbulent and sudden, with departures from home. Here the placement and the benefic aspect tilt toward modest gains, government favour and some land, but the caution about sudden change still applies, so keep plans flexible, keep travel to what is needed, and avoid stirring up family disputes.

**Mars within Venus, Aug 2030 to Oct 2031 (the key stretch).** Venus is the chart's most supportive planet: the yogakaraka, exalted in Pisces in the 3rd house. It rules the 5th (children, creative work, fortune) and the 10th (career and standing), so this stretch reaches the chart's own matters of children and career. It sits four houses from Mars, a relationship the texts count as a kendra link, and Mars aspects it, so Mars's main results show here. That combination is why this is the key stretch of the next decade. The texts describe comforts, vehicles, jewellery, recognition from people in authority and a settled home life.

Two points belong on the cautious side. Rahu shares Venus's house, which the texts tie to strain in family life and money, and Mars and Ketu both aspect Venus. The timing also helps: Saturn in Taurus, the 6th from the Moon and one of its better transits, covers the whole stretch, and the Venus yogini period is running. Double transits fall on the 5th, 7th and 11th houses between Sep 2030 and Feb 2031, and again from Jun to Oct 2031. During Jupiter's passage through the Moon's 12th house, Sep 2030 to Feb 2031, the texts' warning about grief and anxiety around travel applies. Jupiter's move into Sagittarius, the Moon's own sign and its moolatrikona, softens that from Feb to Jun 2031. Remedies (optional): give a cow or female buffalo in charity, the traditional practice the texts list for this stretch.

**Mars within Sun, Oct 2031 to Feb 2032.** The Sun sits in the 2nd, in an enemy sign, and rules the 8th. The texts give this stretch honour from authorities, fame won through boldness and a brief opening for leadership, along with blame, quarrels with elders and heat-related complaints. Here the enemy sign and the 8th lordship keep the harder side more present. Use it for initiative, but with tact toward elders and superiors, and attention to heat-related complaints.

**Mars within Moon, Feb 2032 to Sep 2032.** The period closes with the Moon's sub-period. The older texts read it as mostly favourable: comforts, home improvements, festivities and favour from authorities. In this chart the Moon is waning, sits in the 12th and rules the 7th. The texts associate a weak Moon in that position with money strain and worry for family, along with the need for extra care of health and vitality. Home comfort and rest are the bright side.

### The Rahu period that follows, Sep 2032 onward

Rahu's period follows directly after Mars. Rahu rules no house, so it works through the 3rd house it sits in, through its companion Venus, and through its sign lord Jupiter. The texts read a Rahu period as unsettled unless Rahu is well placed, in which case it can give power, wealth and recognition, often through foreign contacts or the state. Here the placement is good: the 3rd is an upachaya house (one that improves with time), Rahu sits in a benefic's sign, and it is joined with Venus, the yogakaraka, which the texts say can make Rahu act more like a yogakaraka. Mars and Ketu aspect it, so that lift is partial. The texts also say gains in a Rahu period can be unstable and often come under strain toward the end. Overall the period leans good: ambition, change and unusual opportunities, with careful handling of gains, health, family matters and dealings with authority.

### Sub-periods of the Rahu period

**Rahu within Rahu, Sep 2032 to Jun 2035.** Rahu's own sub-period opens its period. The older texts describe it as disturbed, with family strain, health concerns in the household, distance from home and loss of money. The 3rd-house placement gives the good side: a high position, royal favour, happiness from spouse and children, and growth of property. The Venus link is a real support, so the stretch is a disturbed opening with a favourable core. Advice: care with health, family harmony and paperwork with officials.

**Rahu within Jupiter, Jun 2035 to Nov 2037.** Jupiter sits in the 4th house, a kendra and home house, and is vargottama. That placement gives the good side: steadiness of mind, royal favour, growth of wealth, vehicles, pilgrimages, and happiness from spouse, children and grandchildren. Jupiter is the second sign from Rahu, which the texts count as a less supportive position for this sub-period, and it rules the 3rd and 12th. Near its end the texts mention some bodily strain and royal displeasure. Treat that as something to watch, with a health check, rather than as a forecast. Net: one of the better parts of the Rahu period.

### Slow transits and dated stretches

**Saturn now.** Saturn is in Pisces from Nov 2026 to Jun 2027, which is the 4th house from the Moon and the 3rd from the lagna. The texts read the 4th from the Moon (the kantaka or ardhashtama stretch, the "small Dhaiya") as a strain on home life, close relationships and money, with a restless mind. Saturn is the lagna lord here, which the texts say softens its harsher results, and its effects are read as coming mainly in the last part of its sign.

**Saturn's next moves.** It enters Aries in Jun 2027, the 5th from the Moon and debilitated, where the texts intensify the difficulty: concern for children, a dip in money, confused thinking and quarrels. It returns to Pisces from Oct 2027 to Feb 2028, then stays in Aries, debilitated, until Aug 2029. Taurus, the 6th from the Moon, is one of Saturn's good transits, with relief from enemies and from health worries. It holds from Aug to Oct 2029 and again from Apr 2030 to May 2032, with Aries in between. From May 2032 Saturn is in Gemini, the 7th from the Moon, where the texts point to strain in marriage and tiring travel.

**Jupiter.** Jupiter is in Leo, the 9th from the Moon, a classical favourable placement (success, gains, children), until Jan 2027. Its later moves are mixed. Cancer from Jan to Jun 2027 is the 8th from the Moon, but exalted there, so the texts say it does no harm. Virgo from Nov 2027 to Mar 2028 is the 10th from the Moon, which the texts link with pressure on position and property. Libra from Dec 2028 to Mar 2029 is the 11th, favourable. Scorpio, the 12th from the Moon, returns for Jan to May 2030, Sep 2030 to Feb 2031 and Jun to Oct 2031, bringing the texts' grief and anxiety, especially around travel. Sagittarius, the Moon's own sign and Jupiter's moolatrikona, from Feb to Jun 2031 and Oct 2031 to Mar 2032 softens those older warnings. From Mar to Aug 2032 Jupiter is in Capricorn, debilitated, so its good results are lost.

**Dhaiya and Sade Sati.** The current phase is the kantaka (4th-from-Moon) Dhaiya, from Mar 2025 to Feb 2028, not a Sade Sati. The next full Sade Sati begins in Dec 2043. Within the decade comes the ashtama (8th-from-Moon) Dhaiya, with Saturn in Cancer, from Jul 2034 to Aug 2036. The texts count it among Saturn's harder transits: loss of money and possessions, health worries, separation from children and friends, and a fall in standing. Saturn's lordship of the lagna softens it, and it begins within Rahu's own sub-period, which one author reads as a time of trials. Sade Sati and Dhaiya are popular traditions built on older house-by-house transit rules, and modern authors disagree about how severe they are. Svoboda finds them gloomier than most, Kurczak calls the fear-based framing folklore, and Raman treats a second Sade Sati as only moderately hard.

**Past phases, as checks.** The first Sade Sati peak ran from just before birth to Mar 1990, through the end of Ketu's period and the start of Venus's. Modern authors describe that peak as a time of pressure and of character being tested. The Venus period also held the kantaka Dhaiya of 1995 to 1998 and the ashtama Dhaiya of 2004 to 2007, and the 2014 to 2017 rising and peak phases fell in the Sun and Moon periods, with the 2017 to 2023 phases in the Moon period. Do the years around 2004 and 2017 match your own accounts?

**Double transits.** The double transits that matter most for the running periods are these. The 4th (home and property, a house Mars rules) is double-transited in Jun to Oct 2027, Mar to Jul 2028, Dec 2028 to Mar 2029, and Oct 2029 to Jan 2030. The 10th (career, a house Venus rules) is double-transited in Dec 2028 to Mar 2029 and Oct 2029 to Jan 2030. The 2nd (money) is double-transited in Aug to Oct 2029 and May to Sep 2030. The 5th and 11th are covered in the key stretch above. The 7th (partnership) is double-transited in Apr to May 2030, Sep 2030 to Feb 2031, Jun to Oct 2031, and Mar to May 2032.

**Saturn over natal planets.** Saturn passes over natal Venus and Rahu in the 3rd house, in Nov 2026 to Jun 2027 and Oct 2027 to Feb 2028, so siblings, courage and short journeys are under review in the Mars-Rahu and Mars-Jupiter stretches. It passes over natal Jupiter in the 4th house, in Jun to Oct 2027, Feb 2028 to Aug 2029 and Oct 2029 to Apr 2030, so home and property are under review through the Mars period.

**Yogini periods.** The Venus yogini (Siddha) runs to Jul 2032. Venus is exalted and not weak, so the texts' happy reading applies: success, comforts, honour, and celebrations in the family. The cautions about a weak, afflicted Venus do not apply. The next, Sankata (Rahu), runs Jul 2032 to Jul 2040, and the texts call it a crisis period of setbacks, poor health and weakness, separations, and legal or official trouble. Rahu shares Venus's house, which can give a raja yoga here, but the texts say it seldom lasts, and Mars and Ketu's aspects keep the harder side in view. This is the stretch that most asks for patience and health care.

**Chara periods.** Given as timing markers only: Mithuna runs until Feb 2028, Vrishabha until Feb 2039, and Mesha until Feb 2047.

### The next ten years at a glance

The key stretch of the next decade is Mars within Venus, Aug 2030 to Oct 2031, when the most supportive planet leads and several double transits fall on the 5th, 7th and 11th houses. The brightest other stretches are Mars within Jupiter (2027 to 2028) and Rahu within Jupiter (from Jun 2035), while the most testing are Mars within Saturn (2028 to 2029) and the ashtama Dhaiya (Jul 2034 to Aug 2036).
