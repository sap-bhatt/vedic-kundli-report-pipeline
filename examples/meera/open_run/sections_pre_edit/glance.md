## Chart at a glance

**The shape of the chart**

This is a Capricorn rising chart. The rising nakshatra is Shravana, whose lord is the Moon, and the Moon sits in the twelfth house. Saturn, the lagna lord, is also in the twelfth house, alongside Mars, which makes it the most crowded place in the chart. The seventh and tenth houses are empty, so partnership and career are read through the planets that aspect them and through their lords. Three planets sit in the dusthanas (the 6th, 8th and 12th houses, associated with effort and obstacles). In the navamsa (D9, the division traditionally linked with partnership and inner values) the Moon is strong in its own sign, while Mars and Venus are strong in moolatrikona. Jupiter is vargottama, occupying the same sign in the birth chart and in the navamsa. The dispositor chains (each planet, then its sign lord, then that lord's sign lord, and so on) run into one loop: Mars and Jupiter dispose of each other, and the chains of the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus and Saturn all end there. Mars and Jupiter together therefore set the tone of the whole chart.

**Six themes that define this chart**

1. **Effort kept out of sight.** The Moon, Mars and Saturn share the twelfth house, and Saturn, the lagna lord, is among them. Classical texts associate this house with retreat, expenses and inner work, and they read a lagna lord here as asking for more effort before its results come. Spirituality explains the house in full.

2. **A self-questioning mind.** Mercury in the first house is the Atmakaraka, the planet with the highest degree in its sign, and so the one that stands for the self. It is retrograde and combust, overshadowed by the Sun, which points to an intellect that works by reflection. Mind explains Mercury.

3. **Venus as the yogakaraka.** Venus rules the 5th and 10th houses, which makes it the yogakaraka for Capricorn: a planet that rules both a kendra and a trikona. It is exalted in Pisces, placed in the third house with Rahu, and its moolatrikona strength in the navamsa suggests its gifts hold steady. Family covers the third house, and Career covers the 10th lord.

4. **The Sun in a tested position.** The Sun is in Aquarius in the second house, in an enemy's sign and debilitated in the navamsa, and it rules the eighth house. Its promises around money and savings need extra care and steady effort. Personality explains the Sun, and Finance covers the second house.

5. **Jupiter as a steadying force.** Jupiter is vargottama in Aries in the fourth house, and its benefic aspect falls on the Moon, Mars and Saturn in the twelfth house. It offers a benefic counterweight to that crowded house. Family covers the fourth house.

6. **Relationships and career drawn from elsewhere.** The seventh and tenth houses are empty. Their lords, the Moon (7th) and Venus (10th), sit in the twelfth and third houses, so relationships and career work through aspects and indirect routes rather than direct placement. Relationships and Career explain these houses.

**Strengths and tensions in one view**

Strengths: Venus gives the chart its yogakaraka, exalted and steady; Jupiter's vargottama position adds consistency; the Moon and Mars are well placed in the navamsa. Saturn, the lagna lord, forms Raja and Dhana combinations with Mars, which the Yogas section sets out.

Tensions: three planets sit in dusthanas and the lagna lord is in the twelfth house; the Sun is in an enemy's sign and weak in the navamsa; Mercury is combust and retrograde; Ketu aspects the lagna itself. The Moon counts as a natural malefic here because it is waning at birth, even though it is strong in its own navamsa sign. Taken together, the supporting planets are strong while the central planets carry the pressure, so steady effort and good timing are the recurring themes.

**Functional roles for this Capricorn lagna**

- Saturn: lagna lord and lord of the 1st and 2nd houses; neutral in the twelfth.
- Venus: yogakaraka, lord of the 5th and 10th houses; exalted.
- Mars: lord of the 4th and 11th houses; a kendra lord with a wealth-house role.
- Mercury: lord of the 6th and 9th houses; a trikona lord and a dusthana lord.
- Jupiter: lord of the 3rd and 12th houses; a dusthana lord.
- Moon: lord of the 7th house; a kendra lord.
- Sun: lord of the 8th house; a dusthana lord.

**Birth-time sensitivity**

The lagna sits at 22 degrees of Capricorn, so a change of sign would need the birth time to be off by about 32 minutes. The navamsa lagna sits 59% through its division and the dashamsa (D10) lagna 33% through its division. A varga lagna can shift with a few minutes' error when it sits near the edge of a division, and D10 depends closely on the birth time, so its details should be treated as provisional until the time is confirmed. The Moon moves about half a degree an hour, so its sign and nakshatra are far less time-sensitive than the lagna and the divisional charts.
