## Health and vitality

This is a reading from traditional astrology, not medical advice. For any health concern, please see a qualified doctor.

### Overall constitution

Capricorn rises, so steadiness of body is read through Saturn, the lord of the rising sign. Saturn sits in the 12th house, in Sagittarius, a sign neutral to it, and a lagna lord in the 12th usually gets its results with more effort than ease. The gain is that stamina builds slowly and rewards a steady routine. Saturn is a natural malefic in this chart, so its tone is disciplined rather than light. Ketu also aspects the first house.

The Sun sits in Aquarius in the 2nd house. That is an enemy sign for it, and it is debilitated in the navamsa (D9) chart. The Sun is a natural malefic here, so its strength is best supported by regular meals, sleep and rest. The Moon sits in Sagittarius in the 12th house. It was waning at birth and is a natural malefic in this chart. In the D9 chart it sits in its own sign, Cancer, which steadies it.

The rising sign would change only if the birth time were off by about half an hour, so the lagna reading is fairly secure.

### Traditional sensitivities

**The 6th house (daily health).** Its lord, Mercury, sits in the first house, which ties routine health to the self. Mercury is combust (close to the Sun) and retrograde. The texts describe a person who meets service and daily duties with discipline. Some texts read a more delicate constitution that needs care, while Rath reads the opposite, good health for the native. The 6th house itself is empty, but the Moon, Mars and Saturn all look toward it. Mars, which rules the 4th and 11th, casts its energy onto the 6th, read as drive and competitive strength rather than a fixed complaint. The Moon's aspect is read as care and emotional attention; it works better when the Moon is bright, and at birth it was waning.

**The 8th house (recovery).** It is empty, and its lord, the Sun, sits in the 2nd house. The texts point attention toward the face and vision. Jupiter, the lord of the 12th, aspects the 8th, and that is traditionally read as a protective influence on recovery.

**The 12th house (rest).** The Moon, Mars and Saturn gather here, so rest and sleep are central. Mars adds heat, and the texts associate it with restless sleep. Mars and Saturn together are read as a call for general caution, including in what one takes in. Jupiter, the lord of the 12th, sits in the 4th, a friendly placement, and its aspect on the 12th turns spending toward service and spiritual life.

### When this area is most active

Take extra care of rest and routine in these stretches, which are demanding rather than the best time to push: the Mars-Sun sub-period, October 2031 to February 2032, when the Sun, lord of the 8th, is central; and the two spells when Jupiter and Saturn both focus on the 6th house, from December 2028 to March 2029 and from August to October 2032. In those times, a steady routine of rest and regular meals is a sensible habit.
